🇨🇳⛵️ Satellites spot China’s high-tech desert test lab for hunting US warships China has reportedly built a detailed, full-scale 3D replica of an Arleigh Burke-class destroyer in the remote Taklamakan Desert. ♦️ Measuring ~155M, the desert ship features a precisely modeled forward gun, bridge, mast, funnel, helicopter flight deck and other elements, allowing for a precise recreation of the Burke’s radar profile. The mockup may even feature electronic countermeasures and a moving rail system simulating movement. ♦️ Built by engineers at the Ruoqiang Test Range, the mockup is expected to be used by the PLA to test its advanced anti-ship weapons, from coastal defense missiles to hypersonic projectiles, and even some drones – plus the radar and guidance systems supporting them. ♦️ Arleigh Burke-class destroyers are the backbone of the US surface fleet, equipped with Tomahawk strike, anti-sub and anti-air mission capabilities. They’re also the primary defenders of carrier strike groups. ♦️ Physical mockups can be crucial to doctrine development, from calibration of target algorithms and determination of optimal attack angles to synchronization of strikes across different service branches. ♦️ They can also be important psychological signaling – letting your adversaries know you know their weaponry and have the anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) tools to take them on.
