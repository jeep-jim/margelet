🇪🇺🇺🇸 Only 11% of Europeans now view the United States as an ally, a historic low, according to a new survey by the European Council on Foreign Relations covering 15 countries and nearly 20,000 respondents. The number has been in freefall: 22% in November 2024, down to 16% six months ago, now just 11%. Half of respondents call the US a "necessary partner" rather than an ally, and a full quarter now see Washington as a rival or adversary outright — with that hostility spiking hardest in Denmark, France, Spain and Switzerland. Majorities in every single country polled say they doubt the US would actually come to their defense if attacked.
