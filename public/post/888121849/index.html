​Elon Musk's statements are interesting not because he is a visionary, but because he is part of a team building a new digital future. Elon Musk warns that time is running out: According to him, there are only three years left during which it will still be possible to make a living, mainly by selling your working time. As AI progresses, most tasks can be automated, making this economic model less relevant. The idea of "I exchange my time for a salary," dominant for almost 200 years, is on the verge of disappearing. This could lead to one of the most significant wealth redistributions in modern history. According to this vision, the next three years should be used to transform your time into assets that are difficult to replace with AI: Bitcoin and other rare digital assets, land, energy, infrastructure, networks, and production capacities. As a result, the message is: in the future, the most valuable thing will not be the time you sell, but the assets you own. Those who anticipate this shift will be able to gain a significant advantage by 2035-2036. I think very few people believe in this right now, and no one is prepared for it.
