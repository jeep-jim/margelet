❗ Rising Treasury Yields Emerge as Key Threat to Stock Market The 3-month correlation between the 10-year Treasury yield and the S&P 500 has plunged to -0.62—its weakest level in 15 years. This sharp divergence shows that climbing yields are now closely tied to stock market declines. Back in the 2022 bear market, the correlation never dropped below -0.50. After the 2008 crisis, rising yields typically accompanied stronger equities, supported by improving growth and earnings. Now, the story is different.
