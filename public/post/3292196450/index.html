Karen Nyamu has shared her preferred political lineup for Nairobi ahead of the 2027 elections. The nominated senator said she would back Johnson Sakaja for a second term as Nairobi Governor, Jalango for Nairobi Senator, and herself for the Nairobi Woman Representative seat. According to Nyamu, the combination creates a balanced leadership team, arguing that Nairobi's large Kikuyu, Luhya, and Luo populations should all feel represented, with a Luhya as governor, a Luo as senator, and a leader from the Central region serving as Woman Representative.
