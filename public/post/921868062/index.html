Fun fact: For the filming of "Oppenheimer," the team used a special mirror system. Here’s why: IMAX cameras are so loud that they had to be placed inside massive soundproof housings to capture clean audio. But this created a problem—the bulky camera housing sat directly between the actors, blocking their line of sight and making it impossible to maintain eye contact. To preserve genuine emotional connection on screen, Christopher Nolan devised a mirror setup. Actors could see the reflection of their scene partner’s eyes, allowing them to perform as if there were no camera between them at all. NerdNest
