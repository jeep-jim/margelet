🖥 Iran’s top rights: US strike on Qeshm home grave violation of int’l humanitarian law 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s High Council for Human Rights has strongly condemned the latest US attacks on residential areas in Iran, which left civilian casualties, calling the aggression “a clear example of American human rights” and a grave violation of international humanitarian law. In a statement released on Sunday, the council particularly condemned the attacks on Qeshm Island early Thursday, including a strike on a house that killed a child and his parents, and injured two other children. The attacks were part of a series of airstrikes the US military conducted across Iran in recent weeks. ​ The statement said deliberate or indiscriminate attacks on residential areas and civilian objects violate the core principles of international humanitarian law, including the principle of distinction reflected in the 1977 Additional Protocol I to the Geneva Conventions. It stressed that parties to an armed conflict are obligated to distinguish between military targets and civilians and direct attacks only against legitimate military objectives. ​ It added that attacks against civilians and civilian property, as well as indiscriminate or disproportionate strikes causing civilian casualties, are prohibited under international law and constitute serious violations of humanitarian norms. ​ Referring to Article 3 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and Article 6 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, the council said the right to life obliges states to prevent the arbitrary deprivation of life. It said the killing of members of a family inside their home represented a clear breach of that right and a disregard for human dignity. ​ The statement also criticized the silence, inaction, and double standards of many United Nations human rights mechanisms, arguing that such responses undermine the credibility of the international human rights system and contribute to impunity. ​ The council called on the UN Human Rights Council, the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, special rapporteurs, and other relevant international mechanisms to explicitly condemn the attack and take “practical, effective, and tangible” measures to hold the “aggressor state” accountable, end impunity for those responsible, provide redress for victims, and prevent similar attacks in the future. 📲 @Irna_en​
