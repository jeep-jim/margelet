🟠 Why we don't post a Sunday recap Honest framing. Every trading community you follow will publish a Sunday recap today. Last week's wins, this week's outlook, a screenshot with the equity curve highlighted in green. We don't. Not because we can't. Because the recap is the product for them, and the robots are the product for us. A Sunday recap forces you to look at the past seven days as a story with a moral. Markets don't have morals. They have distributions. Any single week, good or bad, tells you almost nothing about whether the system works. The only honest recap is a ninety-day audit, and that's not a Sunday post, that's a spreadsheet. On Sunday, we do less. Same three robots, same criteria, same infrastructure. When Monday opens, nothing about our approach has changed because of what happened last week. That's the difference.
