💰 AI Is Starting to Show Up on Price Tags It’s no longer just a cost behind the scenes — the expense of AI infrastructure is now flowing straight to consumers. As AI innovation speeds up, demand for high-performance memory chips is surging. That’s putting pressure on hardware makers to make tough pricing decisions. Apple has already rolled out major price increases across its MacBook and iPad lines: • MacBook Air: $1,099 → $1,299 • MacBook Pro: $1,699 → $1,999 • iPad Air: $599 → $749 • iPad Pro: $999 → $1,199 Soon after, Microsoft hiked prices on its Xbox consoles by up to $150. The Xbox Series X now starts at $799. What's behind this? Exploding demand for AI-specific memory and semiconductors is driving up component costs. While companies aim to protect profits, everyday users are left covering the gap. ✅@futures Risk everything
