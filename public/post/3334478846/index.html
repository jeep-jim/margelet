🇩🇪🇺🇦 A German landlord says a Ukrainian family she took in out of sympathy stripped her newly built rental down to bare walls before disappearing back to Ukraine. Natalie Schell built a duplex in Wendeburg, Lower Saxony, in 2024. She lives in one half and rented the other, 140 square meters, five rooms, all new, to a Ukrainian family with six children in early 2025 for 1,895 euros a month including heating, Bild reports. While Schell was away, the family gutted the unit: the kitchen, the entire heating system, the toilets, showers, and sinks were all torn out. She puts her losses at more than 100,000 euros. The family is now back in Ukraine.
