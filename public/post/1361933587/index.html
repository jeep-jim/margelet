10 Facts About the World's Largest Performing Fountain 1️⃣Set on a man-made lake at the foot of Burj Khalifa in Downtown Dubai, this is an unforgettable spectacle. 2️⃣The developer, Emaar, announced the fountain's construction in June 2008. 3️⃣The project is by California-based WET Design, the firm behind the musical fountain in front of the Bellagio hotel in Las Vegas. 4️⃣Construction cost – $218 million. The name was chosen through a competition. 5️⃣It opened on May 8, 2009, together with the opening ceremony of Dubai Mall. 6️⃣The fountain is 275m long. This is longer than two football fields. It consists of five circles of different diameters and two arcs. 7️⃣Inside, there are 'water robots' – jets that shoot water under pressure. Moving nozzles create a 'water dance' effect. 8️⃣Water jets reach heights of 150m – equivalent to a 50-storey building. When the fountain is running, up to 83 tonnes of water are in the air at any given second. 9️⃣6,600 lights and 25 colour projectors create 1,000+ shades of water. The fountain's light beams can be seen within a 32km radius. 🔟The fountain performs a variety of songs in different languages: from Sama Dubai in honour of Sheikh Mohammed to global hits.
