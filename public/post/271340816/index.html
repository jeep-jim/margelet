🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 27 July 2026 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation. 📍 The Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation. Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, two marine brigades, and three national guard brigades close to Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Dobropolye, Rubezhnoye, Novotroitskoye, Sergeyevka, Svetloye, and Novonikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic). ▪️The AFU losses amounted to up to 365 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and three motor vehicles. 👉 See Part 2 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
