Still going through my old stuff, this time I didn't loot something from a dead guy, but I remember being a kid and buying old newspapers that caught my eye. Al Ahram newspaper, February 23 1958. Old advertisements, main topic was the unification of Syria and Egypt, voted for with 99.99% and 99.98% in Egypt and Syria respectively. The thing that catches my eye is you can see at the bottom left, a Socialist star and beneath it a moon crescent which is common in Islamic artistry (the crescent, not the star) They really were very experimental with designs back in the day.
