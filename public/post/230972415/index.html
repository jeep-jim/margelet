On 1st July 2026, Miss Nicolle Verma’s 19th birthday was celebrated as Sewa Diwas. On this occasion, food service was organised for needy and helpless attendants of patients at various hospitals in Lucknow. The initiative was successfully organised and led by Foodman Shri Vishal Singh. Miss Nicolle Verma, daughter of Dr. Abhishek Verma, dedicated her special day to the spirit of service, compassion and social responsibility. Through this noble initiative, Foodman Shri Vishal Singh and his team gave a meaningful message that the true celebration of a birthday lies in serving humanity and bringing comfort to those in need.
