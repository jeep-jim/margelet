Движение камеры⚡️ Сегодня я делюсь с вами движением камеры в ИИ-видео. Правильное движение камеры − это эмоция, которую зритель чувствует телом. 📎 Навигация: syntx.ai или @syntxaibot → «Изображения» → «Kling 3.0» 1️⃣ Phantom Glide ➕ Промпт: Smooth continuous forward glide — unhurried but inevitable. The camera moves like air passing a body, like something that has already decided where it is going.The camera pushes forward and skims past the figure - close enough to feel the proximity, close enough that for one frame the person nearly fills the edge of the lens - then slides past and continues into the space beyond. The figure falls behind. The world ahead opens up.The foreground sweeps close to the lens, blurs slightly with proximity, then releases. One world gives way to another.The camera arrives beyond the foreground layer - new space fully revealed ahead - and eases naturally into stillness. The pass is complete. 2️⃣ Ground Level ➕ Промпт: PRIORITY — CAMERA DROPS TO GROUND LEVEL. The shooting point is pressed to the ground — the lens sits as low as physically possible, almost touching the surface. This is the non-negotiable condition of the entire shot. Movement: camera slides along the subject's movement path strictly at ground level or no higher than below-waist height. Speed: strictly match the subject — synchronized with footsteps or wheels. Framing: the ground occupies the majority of the frame and actively passes through it = surface texture, tracks, soil details and ground debris must be clearly readable throughout. End: the shot finishes with the camera held rigidly at ground level — low perspective is maintained and locked until the very last frame. 3️⃣ Truck right ➕ Промпт: Pan right. Movement: the camera rotates horizontally from a single fixed point, sweeping smoothly to the right. Speed: steady and even throughout, with no sudden jolts or acceleration. Framing: the horizon stays perfectly level as fresh scenery continuously enters from the right edge of the frame. End: the motion gradually comes to rest, landing on a well-defined and intentional final composition. 4️⃣ Crane Up ➕ Промпт: crane up. Movement: travel smoothly upward through open space. Speed: slow controlled vertical lift. Framing: keep the subject or location readable as the camera rises. End: finish with the higher scale clearly visible. 5️⃣ Snorricam ➕ Промпт: A warrior woman walks slowly and deliberately forward into battle, shot on a body-mounted Snorricam camera. The camera is physically fixed to her chest and torso, rigidly attached to her body relative to her movements. She looks directly into the camera as she walks. 6️⃣ Crash Zoom ➕ Промпт: Crash zoom in. Movement: snap the lens rapidly toward the main visual target — a small bird perched on a human hand. Speed: very fast and punchy. Framing: keep both the bird and the human face readable through the sudden scale change — feathers, open beak, eyes of the bird and the human face remain in focus. End: land on a bold tighter composition — close-up split between the singing bird on the hand and the human face looking at the bird.
