♦️What Is Ethics in Research & Why Is It Important? بازنشر: ۳۰ تیر ۱۴۰۵ by David B. Resnik, J.D., Ph.D. December 23, 2020 [Updated December 23, 2024] Honesty / صداقت Strive for honesty in all scientific communications. Honestly report data, results, methods and procedures, and publication status. Do not fabricate, falsify, or misrepresent data. Do not deceive colleagues, research sponsors, or the public. Objectivity / عینیت و پرهیز از سوگیری Strive to avoid bias in experimental design, data analysis, data interpretation, peer review, personnel decisions, grant writing, expert testimony, and other aspects of research where objectivity is expected or required. Avoid or minimize bias or self-deception. Disclose personal or financial interests that may affect research. Integrity / سلامت و صحت یا درستی Keep your promises and agreements; act with sincerity; strive for consistency of thought and action. Carefulness / دقت Avoid careless errors and negligence; carefully and critically examine your own work and the work of your peers. Keep good records of research activities, such as data collection, research design, and correspondence with agencies or journals. Openness / گشوده‌گی Share data, results, ideas, tools, resources. Be open to criticism and new ideas. Transparency / شفاقیت Disclose methods, materials, assumptions, analyses, and other information needed to evaluate your research. Accountability / پاسخ‌گویی Take responsibility for your part in research and be prepared to give an account (i.e. an explanation or justification) of what you did on a research project and why. Intellectual Property / رعایت حق مالکیت فکری‌ی دیگران Honor patents, copyrights, and other forms of intellectual property. Do not use unpublished data, methods, or results without permission. Give proper acknowledgement or credit for all contributions to research. Never plagiarize. Confidentiality / قابل اطمینان بودن و قابل اعتماد بودن Protect confidential communications, such as papers or grants submitted for publication, personnel records, trade or military secrets, and patient records. Responsible Publication / انتشار مسؤولانه Publish in order to advance research and scholarship, not to advance just your own career. Avoid wasteful and duplicative publication. Responsible Mentoring / نظارت مسؤولانه Help to educate, mentor, and advise students. Promote their welfare and allow them to make their own decisions. Respect for Colleagues / احترام به هم‌کاران Respect your colleagues and treat them fairly. Social Responsibility / مسئولیت اجتماعی Strive to promote social good and prevent or mitigate social harms through research, public education, and advocacy. Non-Discrimination / تبعیض قایل نشدن Avoid discrimination against colleagues or students on the basis of sex, race, ethnicity, or other factors not related to scientific competence and integrity. Competence / شایسته‌گی Maintain and improve your own professional competence and expertise through lifelong education and learning; take steps to promote competence in science as a whole. Legality / قانون‌مند بودن Know and obey relevant laws and institutional and governmental policies. Animal Care / مراقبت از حیوانات Show proper respect and care for animals when using them in research. Do not conduct unnecessary or poorly designed animal experiments.
