Why Human Health Begins in Nature | Jim Gale & Dr. Heidi Wichmann What if health doesn’t start in the body alone, but in our relationship with nature? In this conversation, Jim Gale and Dr. Heidi Wichmann explore food forests, biodiversity, the microbiome and why human health is deeply shaped by the living systems around us. Inspired by Heidi’s new book " The Earth Within Us", this episode challenges the modern idea that health can be reduced to symptoms, medication and isolated nutrients. Instead, we ask a bigger question: What if nature isn’t separate from health, but the foundation of it?
