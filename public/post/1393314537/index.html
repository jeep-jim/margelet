Pico do Arieiro is the third-highest peak on Madeira Island, rising to 1,818 metres above sea level. Most days, its summit pierces through rolling clouds, offering a surreal, almost otherworldly view. When the skies are clear, you can spot the distant outline of Porto Santo Island, some 48 kilometres to the northeast. With its dramatic peaks and ever-changing skies, Madeira’s rugged interior is a haven for nature enthusiasts and outdoor adventurers. 📍Pico do Arieiro, Madeira, Portugal 🇵🇹 Untamed world
