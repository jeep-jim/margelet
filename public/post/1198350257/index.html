Staff report BLOOMINGTON, Ind. — June 12, 2026 Facebook appeared to experience a widespread service disruption Friday morning, with users in the United States and several other countries reporting blank screens, frozen feeds and difficulty logging into their accounts. The problems began shortly before 9:45 a.m. EDT for some users. Screenshots showed Facebook opening to a blank page or displaying only gray loading placeholders where posts and other content normally appear. Reports posted to Reddit came from users in the United States, Australia, Belgium and the Philippines. Some users said they had been logged out, while others could open Facebook but could not refresh their feeds, view pages or load recent posts. Several also reported problems with Instagram, Messenger and Threads. The volume and geographic spread of the reports indicated the problem was not limited to an individual account, device or internet provider. However, Meta had not publicly identified the cause of the disruption as of Friday morning. Meta’s public status page, which primarily monitors its business and advertising products, was still listing no known problems when checked. Independent outage-monitoring websites also had not yet registered a widespread Facebook outage, possibly because the disruption had only recently begun. There was no immediate evidence that the outage was caused by a cyberattack or that individual accounts had been compromised.
