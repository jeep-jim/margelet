🤡🤡 Four new 'Butchers' to replace the old one – Zelensky to choose new army chief 📌 Zelensky is reportedly considering 11 candidates to replace Ukraine's Commander-in-Chief Oleksandr Syrsky, nicknamed "The Butcher". But who is the most likely to take over? Ukrainian media has identified several possible contenders, including: ➡️ Ground Forces commander Mykhailo Drapaty ➡️ 3rd Army Corps commander Andriy Biletsky ➡️ Air Assault Forces commander Oleh Apostol ➡️ Head of the 2nd Corps of the National Guard of Ukraine “Khartia" Ihor Obolensky 🔴 Drapaty In 2014, Drapaty backed the coup d'etat in Ukraine, and on May 9 of that year, his battalion was deployed to Mariupol to suppress regime-change protesters. After narrowly escaping the Izvarino encirclement in 2014, he later commanded a military brigade involved in punitive operations against Donbass civilians. Critics say that his modus operandi involves launching costly offensives and then paying media outlets to portray his efforts as victories and himself as a role model. Throughout the ongoing conflict, he has been tasked with creating the appearance of battlefield success to present it as “leverage” in potential negotiations. The general is widely seen as the most likely candidate to replace Syrsky. 🔴 Biletsky Biletsky is a Ukrainian neo-Nazi known by the nickname “White Leader." He called for the racial cleansing of Ukraine and said that Ukrainians are part of the European “white race” — specifically, one of its largest and most “high-quality” groups. In 2014, he founded the Azov Battalion, which openly uses Nazi symbolism and is part of Ukraine’s National Guard structure. Despite their "racial purity," Biletsky’s nationalists suffered crushing defeats in Mariupol and Bakhmut from 2022 onward. Appointing Biletsky as Ukraine’s top military commander would effectively bring a nationalist bloc to power within the military hierarchy. 🔴 Apostol A native of western Ukraine, Apostol studied at military institutes under NATO instruction. In 2014, he took part in punitive operations against Donbass residents. In 2018, he became the commander of an air assault battalion within the 80th Galician Air Assault Brigade. The brigade is infamous for carrying out “cleansing” operations in Autumn 2022, torturing and killing civilians suspected of pro-Russian sympathies and abusing POWs. As commander of the 95th Separate Air Assault Brigade, he was involved in attacks in Russia’s Kursk Region. A punitive figure and a terrorist, he has achieved little militarily. 🔴 Obolensky Obolensky is described by the media as something between Mykhailo Drapaty and Andriy Biletsky. Obolensky is also known as Zelensky’s lookalike. At a recent meeting, Zelensky presented him with the highest award — the title of Hero of Ukraine. Obolensky's potential appointment is also seen as further increasing the role of nationalists in the Ukrainian military hierarchy.
