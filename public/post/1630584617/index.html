Middle East Spectator sent me a message and informed me that I never actually gave my opinion on the Iran war since it started, this has led to accusations that I may possibly be Jewish, so I'll like to talk about it. We first have to understand that Iran is a native power in the Middle East, it is not a foreign one, it isn't a colonial one, it's a middle power in the region where it's civilization had existed and clashed with other Middle Eastern civilizations for millennia. It is natural that sometimes people wouldn't get along, but local affairs should be handled by the locals, a struggle between Saudi Arabia and Iran is infinitely more preferable than one that involves foreign powers. There's an ancient saying, me and my brother against my cousin, and me and my cousin against the stranger. I will never support a foreign power that is not local to the region over a local power that I struggle with, simply because he will be closer to me than some distant people oceans and continents anyway, this region should handle its own affairs by itself. The issue is that most of the Arabs forgot this, and this has led to disastrous consequences for our region. That, of course, and the fact that Iran was bombed first, why should I, knowing where I'm from and at, engage in any hostilities against Iran? We've known each other for thousands of years, killed each other at least double that period, but at least there was no Made in USA and a pig dealing oil futures and options in Wall Street clubbing him to death first. Life will always be like that, perhaps one day, it wouldn't be so.
