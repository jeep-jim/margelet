Poetry in motion ✨ Several species of jellyfish from the genus Crossota inhabit the deep waters of Monterey Bay. Researchers at MBARI have observed this pale white jelly, Crossota alba, at depths ranging from 2,000 to 3,200 meters (6,600 to 10,500 feet). Unlike its vibrant relative, Crossota millsae, this species appears ghostly white and often displays a delicate rainbow sheen when lit by ROV lights or under laboratory conditions. These small jellies grow no larger than four centimeters (1.5 inches) in diameter. Untamed world
