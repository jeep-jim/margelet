The Shipwreck Lodge, Namibia Inspired by rusting wrecks on Namibia’s Skeleton Coast, this unique lodge opened in 2018 in one of the country’s most stunning, remote protected areas. Once feared as *The Gates of Hell* by Portuguese sailors, this harsh shore holds over 1,000 shipwrecks, claimed by fog, currents, and shifting sands—yet pulses with eerie beauty. Designed by Nina Maritz Architects, the lodge features 10 chalets styled like stranded ships, blending bold design with isolation. Guests explore wild dunes, spot desert-adapted wildlife—desert lions, giraffes, critically endangered black rhinos—try fishing, or join guided trips to decaying wrecks.
