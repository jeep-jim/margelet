🤡Nuclear rules for Iran, nuclear privileges for Saudi Arabia Demented Don has approved a 30-year deal allowing US companies to help Saudi Arabia develop uranium enrichment capabilities, the WSJ reports. 📑The plan starts with a two-year feasibility study. If enrichment is deemed commercially viable, the technology needed to build the facility could be transferred to Riyadh. 👉Naturally, Washington insists the program is peaceful — the same reassurance it refuses to accept from Iran. 🔴 The double standard is difficult to miss. Iran’s nuclear infrastructure is bombed and sanctioned, while Saudi Arabia is offered US technology to develop the most sensitive part of the nuclear fuel cycle. 🔴 That trust looks even shakier considering Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman openly said in 2018 that Saudi Arabia would build a nuclear weapon if Iran acquired one. 🔴 Riyadh has also rejected a more intrusive IAEA monitoring framework. Instead, the US wants allies to believe its own oversight will be enough — apparently safeguards matter less when the country enriching uranium is a US partner. ❗️And the consequences may spread well beyond Saudi Arabia.
