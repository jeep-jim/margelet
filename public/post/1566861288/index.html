Gili Meno Coast, Indonesia 🇮🇩 Just off the peaceful shores of Gili Meno rests one of the world’s most unique underwater art pieces — Nest. Crafted by British artist Jason deCaires Taylor, the installation features 48 life-sized concrete figures arranged in a circle, submerged 4–5 meters below the surface. But Nest is more than sculpture—it’s a living reef. As corals gradually colonize the figures, marine life is drawn in, helping to regenerate the ocean environment.
