Visa, Stripe, Mastercard, BlackRock, and Coinbase are launching a new stablecoin, OUSD The largest financial and crypto companies have announced the launch of the Open Standard (OUSD) stablecoin. The project involves Visa, Stripe, Mastercard, BlackRock, Coinbase, as well as several dozen other companies. Partners of the project will receive all profits from the reserves of the stablecoin, deducting a small fee for Open Standard's operational expenses. This differs from most existing stablecoins where the issuer (Tether or Circle) keeps the majority of the profit for themselves. OUSD is positioned as an open standard, in which several major players act as both issuers and beneficiaries simultaneously. This is intended to increase trust in the stablecoin among institutional investors and traditional financial companies. - Create an alternative to USDT and USDC with a more distributed revenue model. - Simplify the integration of stablecoins for large companies (Visa, Mastercard, Stripe). - Attract institutional capital through the participation of BlackRock and other traditional players. - Reduce the concentration risks present in centralized stablecoins. Official details about the backing of OUSD (which assets will be in reserves) and the exact mechanics of profit distribution among participants have not been disclosed yet. It is expected that more detailed information will be available in the coming weeks. The project is already being hailed as one of the most serious attempts by the traditional financial world to create its own major stablecoin.
