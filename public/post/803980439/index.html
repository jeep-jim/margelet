🇩🇪 Germany may reintroduce mandatory conscription after its voluntary recruitment drive flopped. The Telegraph reports, citing Thomas Röwekamp, member of the German parliamentary defense committee, that a decision needs to be made before the end of July 2027. The numbers explain the urgency. Since January, the Bundeswehr sent out roughly 300,000 questionnaires to 18 year olds as part of the new "voluntary" service model. After medical screening and interviews, only about 530 actually signed up for at least six months of service. Berlin wants to grow the army from 184,000 to 260,000 active troops by 2035 to meet NATO targets. Public polling backs up the picture. A recent survey found 59 percent of Germans would not take up arms to defend the country if attacked, with just 16 percent saying they definitely would. The voluntary model was always the soft landing before conscription.
