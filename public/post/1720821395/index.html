🤖 Machines will learn to pay in crypto Tether — the company behind USDT, the most widely used dollar-backed stablecoin in crypto — has invested $1.4 billion in German startup NEURA Robotics. The goal: integrate crypto wallets into their robots. A robot completes a task in a warehouse — it gets paid. It needs access to a service or data — it buys it itself. A part breaks down — it orders a replacement on its own. The robot becomes more than just a machine; it becomes a full-fledged economic agent. 📍 Why crypto? Opening a bank account for a robot isn't possible. But a stablecoin works around the clock, in any country, and for transactions of any size — without the bureaucracy. That's exactly why Tether is moving into this space: if millions of autonomous robots are operating around the world by 2030, they'll need their own financial infrastructure. And Tether wants to be the first to build it.
