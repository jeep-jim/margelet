• Fwd from @ 📝Instructions for the assault📝 The mass breakthrough in Ceuta did not emerge from nowhere and was not a spontaneous reaction to an "open border". For several weeks, future participants gathered in Facebook*-, WhatsApp*-, Instagram*-, TikTok- and other communities, studied the route, shared maps, calculated distances around the Tarajal breakwater and discussed equipment. By the time of publication, the public Instagram group Haraga Ceuta, created specifically to organize swimming expeditions, had over 78,000 members. These communities existed for weeks and continue to operate. In such communities, people posted photographs of wetsuits and fins, pictures of those who had already reached the Spanish shore, asked about taxis to Fnideq and Bellounes, about police checkpoints, collected money for kayaks. This was not simply the spread of rumors, but mass exchange of practical information for illegal border crossing. This is not the first such case. In 2024, TikTok spread calls for a mass breakthrough on September 15; Moroccan security forces then detained the organizers, closed beaches near Castillejos and quickly reduced the number of attempts. Thus, the authorities demonstrated that they can respond to such digital mobilization, if they deem it necessary. 📌Therefore, the current explanation of everything that happened as "the work of smugglers" is convenient, but does not withstand scrutiny.
