📡 Iran embassy in Caracas reaffirms ‘deep and historic’ ties with Venezuela 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Embassy of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Caracas has reaffirmed the “deep and historic” relations between Iran and Venezuela, stressing that the longstanding partnership is rooted in mutual respect, solidarity between the two nations, and their shared determination to safeguard independence, national sovereignty, and the right to self-determination. In a statement published in Persian and Spanish on its official Telegram channel and X account on Wednesday, the embassy commemorated the birth anniversary of late Venezuelan leader Hugo Chavez, paying tribute to his legacy and his enduring role in establishing and expanding the “brotherly and exemplary” relations between both revolutionary nations. ​ The embassy said the bilateral relationship has consistently been targeted by enemies seeking to undermine it, particularly through media campaigns. ​ It said the latest example involved “the incomplete and out-of-context reflection of part of an interview” by Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi regarding US military aggression and pressure against independent nations, including Iran and Venezuela. ​ “Some media outlets have sought, by selectively editing the remarks and presenting an interpretation contrary to the speaker’s original intent, to create misunderstanding and disrupt the historic relations between the two countries,” the statement read. ​ The embassy clarified that Araghchi’s remarks “in no way contained disrespect or a negative judgment” toward the Venezuelan people, the country’s legal institutions, officials, or national sovereignty. ​ The Venezuelan government and people, it added, have always enjoyed Iran’s profound respect and the Iranian government has consistently admired the Venezuelan nation’s historic resistance in defense of its independence and sovereignty. ​ The statement further said the strong bond between Iran’s Islamic Revolution and Venezuela’s Bolivarian Revolution, founded on shared ideals of independence, justice, opposition to domination, and mutual respect, would not be harmed by divisive media campaigns. ​ The embassy concluded by reaffirming that more than seven decades of Iran–Venezuela relations have been based on internationally recognized principles, including sovereign equality, political independence, non-interference in internal affairs, mutual respect, and respect for national sovereignty, and would continue to expand on the same foundations. 📲 @Irna_en​
