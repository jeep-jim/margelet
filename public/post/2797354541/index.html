After Toncoin (TON) was rebranded to Gram (GRAM), its price dropped by almost 15%❕ The renaming of the TON ecosystem token took place on June 15 at the initiative of Telegram founder Pavel Durov. On that day, the token price rose to $1.82. GRAM is currently trading at around $1.57. Over the past 24 hours, the token has lost more than 10%. The rebranding was supported by crypto exchanges, although not all of them have updated the ticker yet. Bybit was among the first to rename TON, while Binance announced that the old ticker will remain until June 30, with trading pairs under GRAM set to launch on July 2. GRAM currently ranks 20th on CoinMarketCap with a market capitalization of $4.24 billion.
