⚽ Most people looking at the FIFA World Cup focus on one question: Who will win? Bybit is taking a completely different approach with its new WC26 Goal Difference Futures. Instead of simply predicting a winner, traders can speculate on the expected goal difference between the home team and away team. This is important because the contract price is linked to expected net goals, not winning probability. For example: 🏟 Home team wins 2-1 Goal Difference = +1 Settlement Price = 11 🏟 Match ends 1-1 Goal Difference = 0 Settlement Price = 10 🏟 Home team loses 1-2 Goal Difference = -1 Settlement Price = 9 The settlement formula is: Settlement Price = 10 + Goal Difference One thing many traders misunderstand is that a contract trading at 10.9 DOES NOT mean the team has a 90% chance of winning. Instead, the market is pricing an expected goal difference of approximately +0.9 goals for the home side. Another interesting feature is that these contracts remain tradable before and during the match.
