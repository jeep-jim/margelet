This afternoon and evening, I held my final two speaking events in New Zealand at Calvary Chapel Auckland. Both gatherings were packed, and I thank the Lord that, despite increased back pain, He gave me the strength to teach, answer questions, and spend time meeting and greeting so many wonderful people. Both events were flawlessly organized by Pastor Jordan and his amazing team. The hospitality was exceptional, the worship was powerful, and they took such great care of me - from providing a comfortable place to rest to ensuring excellent security. Thankfully, there were no protesters today, as they were busy holding an event downtown. And that’s a wrap! Within just one week, I had the privilege of holding seven events in five locations across four major cities in New Zealand. Tomorrow I begin the fifth and final leg of my speaking tour - Australia. Once again, thank you for your faithful prayers and support. They enable me to do what the Lord has called me to do. Onwards and upwards! “Therefore, my beloved brethren, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.” - 1 Corinthians 15:58 (NKJV) Awaiting His Return, Amir
