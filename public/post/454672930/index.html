Alexander Buchkuri — "The Verdict" The painting is titled "The Verdict", and its narrative draws from a brutal rural tradition once used to punish wives accused of infidelity or disobedience. When a husband suspected his wife of cheating, he would tie her—naked—to a harnessed horse and parade her through the village, lashing her with a whip.
