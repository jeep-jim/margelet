Neuralink, created by Elon Musk, has developed a wheelchair for disabled individuals that can be controlled using the power of thought through an implanted brain chip. The implant reads signals related to movement direction and translates them into commands for a virtual joystick. Thanks to this, fully paralyzed individuals can control the wheelchair without moving.
