🔻 Iran criticizes US congressional hearing for ignoring human cost of war 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized a US congressional hearing on the performance of the country’s secretary of war during the conflict with Iran, saying American lawmakers failed to address the humanitarian consequences of Washington’s military campaign. In a post on X on Wednesday night, Baqaei described it as a shame that the hearing focused solely on the financial costs of the conflict without raising a single question about the human dimension of the war. ​ “It’s a shame that throughout the US Secretary of War’s congressional hearing, not a single question was asked regarding the “human” aspect of America’s ruthless war against the Iranian nation, fully aware that this was an illegal war of choice accompanied by countless war crimes, Minab and Lamerd being just two of them,” the spokesperson said. ​ On Tuesday, Secretary of War Pete Hegseth testified before the Senate about the financial cost of the war against Iran, as he called for nearly $70 billion in additional funding for the conflict. ​ On February 28, 2026, the first day of the recent US-Israeli war, a missile strike destroyed Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School in Minab, Hormuzgan Province, killing at least 168 people including schoolchildren. The same day, strikes on civilian locations, including a school and a sports hall, killed at least 21 people in the city of Lamerd, Fars Province. Women and children were among the victims of the attack in Lamerd, also located in the south. 📲 @Irna_en​
