Oregon, USA If nature had a kaleidoscope, it would look like Oregon. In just a single day, you can stand beside the ocean, beneath a volcano, beneath a waterfall, at the edge of a canyon, and in the heart of the desert. The state’s western edge is a rugged stretch of beaches and cliffs, forever shaped by the untamed Pacific. To the east lie vast lava plateaus and sunbaked desert lands. Between them rises the Cascade Range — dense forests, serene lakes, winding rivers. The Columbia River Gorge carves through the landscape like a living gallery of waterfalls, where every turn reveals a new spectacle. Oregon is one of the last places where wild camping feels limitless. The air carries the scent of pine, and the sky seems within reach.
