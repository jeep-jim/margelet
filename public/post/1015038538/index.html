📉 Bitcoin is approaching a potential Death Cross 💀 The Death Cross, where the 50-day moving average falls below the 200-day moving average, is traditionally viewed as a bearish signal. In previous Bitcoin cycles, it has often been followed by declines of around 30%. However, there's an interesting pattern: in past cycles, the Death Cross has frequently marked the final major drop before a new bullish trend began. If history repeats itself, some analysts believe $50K could become the ultimate bottom of this correction. As always, past performance doesn't guarantee future results—but it's a pattern worth watching. 📈
