Crypto speak: Bull trap A “bull trap” is a market analysis term that refers to a cryptocurrency’s price action that feigns an impending long-term upward (bullish) trend when, in reality, it’s secretly a short-term correction with more downward pressure. In other words, if you see a crypto’s price pumping and immediately rush to buy it, congratulations, there’s a strong chance you’re entering a bull trap, and your coin is about to get cheaper 🙃 Crypto Mindset✅️
