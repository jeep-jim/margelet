James Fishback is the first candidate to join us, but we will be sitting down with every candidate running for Governor of Florida. We were ready to set sail on Lucky Charm III, but thanks to one homeless man, we had to change course. Fishback shares with me his plans to overturn Florida policies like Red Flag gun laws.
