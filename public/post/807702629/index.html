💵 US Treasuries in Structural Bear Market US Treasuries have delivered an average annual return of -3% after inflation over the past decade — the weakest performance since the 1980s. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 generated +12% annual returns in real terms during the same period, marking the largest gap between stocks and bonds since the 1960s. Prolonged negative real returns on Treasuries have only occurred three times before: in the 1910s, 1940s, and 1970s–80s — each era defined by inflation spikes, sometimes exceeding 10%. Today’s deficit levels and inflation trends show little sign of abating. ✅@futures
