Good afternoon from Yalta where I had the honour to visit the Livadia pal…

Good afternoon from Yalta where I had the honour to visit the Livadia palace, summer residence of Nikolay II and his family, venue of the Yalta conference in February 1945 where a new order was established by the Big Three. It was a diplomatic success for Josef Stalin that after the Tehran conference the Allies met in the Soviet Union, the summer resort of the Russian aristocracy. Stalin wanted to show his partners the destruction of the country done by Nazi Germany. Together they were united in fighting Nazism and demilitarise Germany as the Pravda stated. I had read so much about this place and the minutes of the negotiations that when we entered I felt as if I had been there before. US-President Theodore Roosevelt was hosted by Stalin at the conference venue so to make his life in the wheelchair easier. Winston Churchill had arrived with a flue, none was in good health but they managed to get things done. Roosevelt obtained the establishment of the UN, Churchill represented absent France, and Stalin struck not only a diplomatic success but assured territorial expansion for the Soviet hemisphere. The delegations were small compared to the huge negotiation teams of our time, the press release was precise and concise in contrast to the confusion we can read every few hours about current wars. The British wondered where to get lemon for their afternoon tea, the organisers brought lemon trees...what a protocol. The rooms and gardens were bugged, and Stalin had a certain intelligence advantage, Churchill of course was not amused. And across the palace one can catch also a glimpse of the atmosphere during the summer vacation of Tsar Nikolay II and the family, there are lots of photos on the walls, showing them at the tennis courts with the German cousins...a few years later they would wage war against each other. 1911, 1917, 1945...2026.. I am walking in these impressive gardens so diligently taken care of with a worrisome heart. There were rational persons at the table in 1945, but I miss rationality. #Yalta #LivadiaPalace #diplomacy #war
