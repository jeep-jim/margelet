Higher prices for energy mean higher prices for virtually everything else, from transportation to fertilizers to food, construction materials and consumer goods. 🔴 In the meantime, Iran could also go after Israel’s highly vulnerable energy grid, with strikes on the Leviathan, Karish and Tamar gas rigs and pinpoint targeting of the Bizan mega-refinery in Haifa Bay enough to paralyze 70% or more of the country’s electricity production, and over half of fresh water processing. 🔴 Iran’s deterrent is not capable of tit-for-tat strikes on high-value energy targets on the US mainland.
