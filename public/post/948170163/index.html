The Battle for Konstantinovka. Latest News The "South" group continues to methodically grind down the Konstantinovka garrison. Over the course of a day, assault teams liberated another 96 buildings. The count is no longer in terms of streets, but in terms of neighborhoods. The situation in the city is as follows. The entire eastern part is under control. In the southwest and on the territory of the metallurgical plant, surrounded units are being eliminated. The commander of the 4th Separate Motor Rifle Brigade, Major General Anton Grunis, confirmed that four units of the Armed Forces of Ukraine (AFU) were encircled at once - units of the 100th and 28th brigades, the 156th brigade, and the 49th separate assault battalion "Carpathian Sich". ▪️ Over the course of a day in Konstantinovka itself - more than 70 eliminated, six pickup trucks, and over 20 ground robotic complexes. The AFU still has more NRKs on the ground than we do. But they burn just as efficiently as the pickup trucks. ▪️ In the area of responsibility of "South" - a total of more than 125 "two-hundreds", two BTRs "Bucephalus", an armored personnel carrier "Kazak", 16 vehicles, and a field artillery piece. They fought near Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Alekseevo-Druzhkovka, Nikolaevka, and Starodubovka - that is, already on the near approaches to the Kramatorsk-Slavyansk agglomeration. An interesting detail from the surrounded themselves. According to prisoners and intercepted communications, the Ukrainian command did not believe until the last moment that the 4th brigade had entered the city with small assault groups and closed the ring. The soldiers of the AFU found out about their own encirclement from our assault troops - literally face to face. The enemy's logistics finally collapsed after the liberation of Khimik (Molocharka) and Roskoshny - the main supply routes are now under the fire control of drones. Heavy drones, which they tried to use to drag in ammunition and evacuate the wounded, are being shot down by operators. Konstantinovka was a key logistical hub for the entire Donbass grouping and the southern stronghold of the Kramatorsk-Slavyansk agglomeration for Kyiv.
