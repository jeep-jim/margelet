Sima Humboldt, Venezuela 🇻🇪🕳🌿✨ Sima Humboldt is one of Venezuela’s most enigmatic natural marvels — and one of the deepest sinkholes on Earth, plunging approximately 325 meters. Tucked deep within the rainforest atop Cerro Duida’s tepui, it stands like a portal to a forgotten world. Not discovered until the 1960s, this immense vertical void is so expansive that its floor shelters a distinct ecosystem, cut off from the surface for thousands of years. With barely a trace of sunlight reaching below, rare plants, insects, and other life have evolved in near-complete isolation. Reaching Sima Humboldt means trekking through dense Amazonian jungle and rugged, remote terrain — a journey possible only for scientists and the most determined adventurers.
