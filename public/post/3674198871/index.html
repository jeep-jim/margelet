🇮🇷🇵🇰🇺🇸 The Bab al-Mandab is not closed. It is loaded. Iran used a high-level delegation to Islamabad—headed by the Interior Minister and including the head of NIOC—to deliver a message to Trump via Pakistan: implement the original 14-point MoU in full, with no renegotiation, or face a prolonged war and non-stop disruption of energy and maritime transit. Trump rejected the only off-ramp available to him. Iran would consider accepting a 10-day pause on the current escalation ladder. But for that to happen Trump needs to start delivering the very moment Iran accepts the 10-day pause on all aspects of the 14-point Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). Translation: no new negotiations. This, incidentally, happens to also be the Chinese position. The only thing that matters is to go back to the original MoU and fulfill its commitments. Tehran's calculus is clear: it can continue absorbing pressure while raising the cost of war via control of the Strait of Hormuz and now the Yemeni blockade of Saudi Arabia in the Red Sea. Meanwhile, Gulf states are beginning to hedge away from exclusive security dependence on the US, looking for non-American guarantees—with Pakistan now in a position to decide how far it's prepared to go without sacrificing its carefully built relations with Iran. 💬 Pepe Escobar writes @rocknrollgeopolitics 🔔 Read exclusive analysis 🚀 Subscribe 🌐 Join us on VK
