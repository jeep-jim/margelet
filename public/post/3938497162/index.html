Palestinian Prisoner’s Society: Israeli occupation’s prison system commits medical crimes against Palestinian child detainee, then releases him to evade responsibility. 15-year-old child remains unconscious in hospital, in critical condition. August 2, 2026 The case of 15-year-old Palestinian child detainee Mohammed Mousa Mohammed Suleiman Humaid exposes the systematic medical crimes committed by the Israeli prison system against Palestinian political prisoners. Mohammed, from the town of Tuqu’, Bethlehem, was arrested on February 25, 2026, and held in Ofer Prison. After contracting scabies, prison authorities denied him adequate medical care, causing his condition to deteriorate until the infection spread to his heart and brain. On July 27, Mohammed underwent heart surgery without his parents present and suffered neurological damage. His right foot may require amputation. Israeli authorities released Mohammed on July 30, only after his condition became life-threatening. Today, he remains unconscious in the intensive care unit. The Palestinian Prisoner’s Society holds Israeli occupation authorities fully responsible for his fate and stresses that his case reflects a systematic policy of medical neglect against Palestinian prisoners. More than 100 Palestinian prisoners and detainees have been killed in Israeli custody since the beginning of the genocide. Israel currently holds approximately 350 Palestinian children and around 9,400 Palestinian political prisoners.
