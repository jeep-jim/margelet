💬 ‘BRICS Currency’ formation a realistic expectation: Russian expert 📌 Moscow, IRNA – The Head of the “BRICS Expert Council–Russia” believes that expecting the formation of BRICS currencies in the coming years is a realistic expectation. Talking to IRNA on Tuesday, Victoria Panova said that BRICS actions aimed at altering the current status quo, moving away from dollar dominance, and enhancing the role of national currencies in BRICS trade will undoubtedly expand gradually. ​ The utilization of national BRICS currencies in intra-bloc trade among member countries has already reached 65 percent, the Russian expert noted. ​ At the same time, Panova emphasized that BRICS is by no means establishing an anti-dollar bloc; its objective is not to eliminate the dollar, but rather to stop being held hostage by any single currency or payment infrastructure. ​ Commenting on the prospects of Iran’s membership in the New Development Bank (NDB) of BRICS, she evaluated Iran’s economic capacity in key sectors as practical and logical for the NDB. Panova stated that Iran, as a nation with a strong motivation for financial diversification and reducing dependence on the dollar, aligns well with the new strategy of the New Development Bank. ​ Nevertheless, she specified that the final decision and timeline for Iran’s membership in the New Development Bank depend entirely on the bank’s own formal regulatory mechanisms. 📲 @Irna_en​
