🔍 RESEARCH: Not all crypto credit is created equal. CeFi lending, DeFi liquidity pools, tokenized Treasuries, and RWA-backed private credit—all operate on fundamentally different models of custody, collateral, and enforceability. CeFi centralizes counterparty risk. DeFi relies on on-chain collateral and automated liquidations. Meanwhile, real-world asset (RWA) lending still depends on off-chain underwriting and legal recourse. When a loan defaults, what really matters? The token. The collateral. Or the legal claim behind it? Cointelegraph Research, in collaboration with @eightlends, breaks down how credit risk is structured across the crypto landscape.
