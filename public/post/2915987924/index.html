🔴 Friday became the settlement theater Honest read. The Friday close used to matter because positioning had to be squared before the weekend. That structural pressure is mostly gone. Twenty-four-hour crypto markets, weekend gap hedging via options, and prime brokers offering Sunday liquidity have all quietly removed the Friday must-close-flat premium. What's left is the appearance of a settlement. 👉 Retail platforms still frame Friday P&L as the scorecard 👉 Trading content still films Friday recaps like they're a verdict 👉 Prop firms still evaluate weekly, reinforcing the artificial rhythm So the ritual survives without the mechanism. And traders end up trading the ritual instead of the market. Squaring positions that didn't need squaring. Taking one more setup to make the number round. The pros stopped treating Friday as terminal years ago. The retail feed hasn't updated the memo 🌟.
