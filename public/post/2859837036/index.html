This isn't even close to everything AR can do Over the last few years, augmented reality moved beyond selfie filters. ▫️ Medicine - surgeons see CT scans and tumor boundaries directly in their field of view during operations. ▫️ Real estate - buyers walk through unbuilt developments at full scale on the actual site. ▫️ Education - medical students dissect virtual bodies, and pilots practice emergencies without the risk. ▫️ Industry - Boeing and Amazon use AR for assembly and warehouse work, with instructions appearing right on the part. ▫️ Art - museums come alive, and artists create works that exist beyond the boundaries of the physical world. ▫️ Retail - trying on clothes, makeup, and furniture without the physical product is now the norm at Sephora, Nike, IKEA.
