🔍 RESEARCH: Most RWA conversations center on token issuance. But with private credit, the real challenge lies in enforcement—can investors actually recover funds when a borrower defaults? If recovery hinges solely on a token, the framework is flawed. Credit still operates within legal systems. Eightlends put this to the test. Its parent company, Maclear AG, documented one default: an Italian firm, Vibroedil, which became insolvent in July 2025 after receiving a €150,000 loan. Under Italian pledge law, collateral recovery swiftly returned the full principal to investors. That’s the gap between a token and an enforceable claim.
