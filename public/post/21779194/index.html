🇪🇺 Von der Leyen: EU to Introduce Mandatory Age Verification App for Social Media The President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, has announced that the European Union will introduce a new mandatory age verification app for accessing social networks and digital platforms. The draft law will be presented after the summer. According to von der Leyen, the issue is not whether "children can use social networks," but rather "when social networks gain access to our children." The plan includes a phased approach based on age: - Strict restrictions or a ban for children under 13; - Enhanced parental control for younger ages; - Limited access to "social platforms" for teenagers. The main goal is to protect children from harmful content and "predatory algorithms" that can cause addiction. The app will allow age verification without sharing unnecessary personal data with platforms. Von der Leyen emphasized that major tech companies have "no more excuses" not to implement such protective measures.
