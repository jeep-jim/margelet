🔥 RESEARCH: Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) form the backbone of Europe’s economy — yet they’re consistently overlooked by traditional banks. Since Basel III took effect, SME lending across the EU has dropped by 40–50%. In 2023 alone, it declined another 12%. The consequence? A stubborn €39B annual funding gap — now increasingly filled by non-bank lenders offering floating-rate debt, exposing SMEs to higher financial risks amid rising interest rates. This shortfall reveals an emerging opportunity: onchain lending platforms that enable retail investors to directly fund SME growth.
