Give me a 30-day expense reduction plan without turning my life into a financial prison. 3. Составляем бюджет: Act as a practical financial coach. I want to create an automatic savings system that doesn't rely on my willpower. My monthly net income is: [amount] My fixed expenses are: [amount] Current savings: [amount] Main goal: [emergency fund / trip / investment / housing / debt] Design a savings hierarchy with: first emergency objective, 3 to 6 months of expenses objective, recommended automatic percentage, transfer frequency, ideal account for each goal, rules for not touching the money, and progressive savings increase plan. Include a simulation with small, medium, and aggressive contributions. 6. Управляем финансовой ясностью: Act as a personal financial analyst. I want to build a simple dashboard to understand my financial situation in a single view. My accounts are: [accounts] My debts are: [debts] My financial goals are: [goals] Design a dashboard with: income, expenses, savings, debt, net worth, investments, subscriptions, fees, and progress toward goals.
