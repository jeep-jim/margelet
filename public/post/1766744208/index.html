Almost like in "Dune": in the USA, a jacket has been created for extracting drinking water from the air in extreme conditions. The trick is in detachable inserts with a hydrogel fabric that absorbs moisture from the environment. To extract the liquid, these inserts need to be placed in a special folding device that can be carried in a backpack. It is promised that just one small piece of fabric provides about a liter of water per day. In the future, the technology is also planned to be used in tents and other camping gear.
