🤖 Humanoid Robot Teacher Makes Debut at NYC High School A humanoid robot named Sally has begun teaching senior students at Salamanca High School in New York. Equipped with facial recognition tied to student accounts, Sally assists with lessons in programming and AI, offering tailored support. Still, school administrators stress that while the technology is promising, human teachers remain irreplaceable. ✅@futures ZapChain
