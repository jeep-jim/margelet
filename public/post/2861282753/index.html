🇩🇪🇪🇺🇷🇺 European banks are suing German company Linde over their own sanctions losses in Russia — FT reports. Deutsche Bank, UniCredit, and Commerzbank filed suit seeking hundreds of millions of euros in compensation for assets seized by Russian courts, trying to pin the blame for their own self-inflicted losses from anti-Russia sanctions onto Linde, which was forced to exit the project. The case centers on a contract to build a gas processing plant in Ust-Luga, halted after the EU sanctions hit. Russian courts have seized roughly €1 billion in bank assets: Deutsche Bank lost €244 million, UniCredit €460 million, Commerzbank €90 million.
