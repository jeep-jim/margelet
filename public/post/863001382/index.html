🇪🇺 Experts predict a wave of crypto company acquisitions following the rollout of MiCA in Europe After the EU’s MiCA crypto regulations take effect on July 1, smaller crypto companies may find it easier to sell their businesses or merge with larger players than to meet the new regulatory requirements. Crypto exchanges and startups will now be required to comply with standards similar to those applied to banks and other financial institutions. Companies without a MiCA licence will gradually leave the European market, while their users and assets are expected to move to licensed providers. Self-custody will not disappear, but the broader market is likely to shift towards regulated companies. Europe may follow Switzerland’s path. After the country introduced digital asset legislation, around three-quarters of its largest banks began offering cryptocurrency-related services. Banks are also more likely to use crypto companies’ infrastructure for custody, staking and tokenisation rather than compete with them directly.
