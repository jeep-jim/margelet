🌍 Pakistani education minister praises Iran’s resilience, women’s empowerment 📌 Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan’s Minister of Federal Education and Professional Training, Wajiha Qamar, has praised Iran’s resilience and women’s achievements, saying recent developments had demonstrated how courage, perseverance and national unity could be learned from the Iranian people. Qamar met with Iranian Deputy President for Women and Family Affairs Zahra Behrouz Azar and members of the Iranian delegation on the sidelines of the Ninth Meeting of Women Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) in Islamabad on Sunday. ​ During the meeting, she highlighted the longstanding brotherly ties between Pakistan and the Islamic Republic of Iran, saying, “We witness the outstanding role of Iranian women in society, and recent developments showed us how to learn perseverance, courage and national solidarity from the people of Iran.” ​ Recalling her meetings with Iranian women officials at regional and international forums, as well as Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian’s recent visit to Pakistan, Qamar added, “Iran taught us how to demonstrate courage, perseverance and national solidarity under difficult circumstances.” ​ She also praised Iran’s progress in empowering women and supporting their participation in education and professional fields, saying Pakistan could benefit from Iran’s experience. ​ Expressing solidarity with Iran, she said Pakistanis were mourning the loss and praying for the success and prosperity of Iranian officials. 📲 @Irna_en​
