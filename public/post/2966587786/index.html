🇯🇴 Is Jordan about to explode? Jordan ended its almost decade-long pause on executions last month, hanging six men convicted of terrorism and the killing of members of the Jordanian Armed Forces and police, including during a 2022 wave of anti-government demonstrations and strikes sparked by rising fuel prices, high living costs and double-digit unemployment. Always vulnerable to global shocks, with 10-15% of its economy built on remittances and foreign aid (designed to prevent an anti-Israeli government from taking power), Jordan now faces a perfect storm of foreign and domestic problems which could push the pro-US monarchy over the brink: 🌏 the country has been drawn into the 2024-present US & Israel vs Iran wars, with its military deployed to defend Israel from incoming Iranian drones and missiles, and bases hosting US troops currently being pummeled 🌏 Jordan is facing a severe water crisis after Israel blocked deliveries over Amman’s mild criticisms of Israeli actions in Gaza (60-70% of Jordanians are of Palestinian origin) 🌏 The Gulf energy crisis triggered in February has hit Jordan particularly hard. Possessing no oil of its own, Jordan imports most of its ~100-115k bpd from Iraq at international prices, which have spiked to $100+ 🌏 the Houthis’ entry into the fray threatens Jordan’s only maritime gateway – the Red Sea port of Aqaba, with shipping rates and war-risk insurance premiums already spiking sky high since 2023 🌏 inflation has almost doubled since February (1.17%-2.79%), with fuel, transport, food and housing costs all up. Unemployment stands at a staggering 21% 🌏 Many young Jordanians are fed up with their government’s inaction on Gaza and close alliance with the US, staging tens of thousands-strong protests and flouting severe repercussions by the GID secret police, including kidnappings, targeting of leaders, digital surveillance, and changes to the penal code allowing indefinite “administrative detention” (no charges, trials or access to lawyers) Is Jordan on the brink of a blowup?
