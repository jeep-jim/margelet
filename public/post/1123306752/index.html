The risk sits in the asset price and in the unlock rules. 🤝 Lending. You lend an asset through a protocol and earn interest from a borrower. The risk sits in the protocol and in whether the market can pay you back. 📌 Yield is always payment for a specific risk. The skill is naming which one before you chase the number.
