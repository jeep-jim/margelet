OpenAI launches Health in ChatGPT Health is a new mode in which the chatbot gains access to your medical records and Apple Watch data to answer health-related questions based on your actual metrics. Users can connect Apple Health along with medical records from hospital systems, One Medical, or Function Health. Once linked, ChatGPT can view lab results, medications, recent doctor visits, sleep, and activity data. It can compare a new blood test with previous ones, explain unclear terms from discharge summaries, or even recommend restaurants while taking allergies into account. According to OpenAI’s own statistics, 300 million people already ask ChatGPT about health every week. Among early testers, 70% of these conversations began outside a dedicated medical section. For this reason, the relevant context is now pulled directly into regular chats without the need to switch modes. Dedicated models have been assigned to the feature. Free users get access to GPT-5.5 Instant, which has improved recognition of situations that require a real doctor. Paid users receive GPT-5.6 Sol, which handles complex cases more accurately and explains lab results more effectively. Data from medical records and Apple Health is not used for model training or advertising targeting. It is stored with additional encryption, can be disconnected at any time, and synchronized records are deleted within 30 days. The feature is currently available only in the United States, on web and iOS, for users aged 18 and older on the Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans. It is not available in Codex.
