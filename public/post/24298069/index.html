🇺🇸⚡️ — The U.S. government has ordered Anthropic to suspend access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI models for all foreign nationals worldwide, including foreign employees, citing national security concerns over an alleged jailbreak method. Anthropic said it received the directive on June 12 and was forced to immediately disable both models, though access to its other AI systems remains unaffected. The company said the government has not provided evidence of a serious security failure and that the reported jailbreak appears to involve only limited, previously known vulnerabilities that can also be identified by other publicly available models, including OpenAI’s GPT-5.5. Anthropic defended Fable 5’s security measures, stating it underwent thousands of hours of red-teaming with U.S. and UK government partners, private organizations, and internal teams, and that no universal jailbreak capable of broadly bypassing its safeguards has been discovered. The company argued that perfect jailbreak resistance is likely impossible for any AI model and that its strategy relies on layered defenses, monitoring, and 30-day data retention to detect and mitigate misuse. While complying with the order, Anthropic criticized the government’s decision as lacking transparency and technical justification, warning that applying such a standard across the AI industry could effectively halt the deployment of future frontier models. It said it believes the action is based on a misunderstanding and is working to restore access as quickly as possible.
