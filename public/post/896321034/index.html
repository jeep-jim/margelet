On the occasion of International Yoga Day 2026, Dr. Abhishek Verma and The Verma Family celebrated the day at their residence in New Delhi with a special yoga session, accompanied by over 125 members of their personal staff. Yoga Guru Sohit Yogi and his team conducted an hour-long yoga session for The Verma Family and their personal staff, explaining the importance of adopting yoga in daily life, especially in today’s stressful and fast-paced lifestyle. Following the session, Dr. Abhishek Verma addressed the gathering and highlighted the physical, mental and spiritual benefits of yoga. He thanked Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi for giving yoga global recognition through the United Nations 12 years ago and for transforming India’s ancient wisdom into a worldwide movement for health, discipline and inner strength. Dr. Verma also highlighted the important role of ShivSena in promoting yoga, noting that the Ayush Ministry at the Centre is with ShivSena and that the party is making every possible effort to popularise yoga across the country.
