🤔 Is Strategy preparing to become a Bitcoin seller? Michael Saylor's company has introduced a new Digital Credit Capital Framework, which for the first time explicitly mentions the possibility of selling part of its BTC holdings to fund operations and maintain liquidity. Key details: 🟠 Strategy now holds $2.55 billion in cash reserves. 🟠 The company estimates it could raise another $1.25 billion by selling BTC if necessary. 🟠 Combined, that's enough to cover nearly 26 months of dividend payments without needing additional financing. Another interesting signal: last week, Strategy didn't buy a single Bitcoin. Instead, it raised $1.15 billion through MSTR share sales. The market actually liked the update — both MSTR and STRC jumped around 6% after the announcement. For years, "never sell your Bitcoin" was part of the Strategy story. Now, the company is at least acknowledging that selling BTC could become an option if conditions require it. 👀
