While many traders are still expecting one final capitulation, Bitcoin has climbed back above $66K for the first time since mid-June. 👀 The main reason so many remain bearish is simple: previous cycles saw deeper drawdowns. But Bitcoin's corrections have been shrinking with each cycle, making blind comparisons to past markets increasingly unreliable. 🐋 Meanwhile, whales continue placing buy orders between $60K and $48K.
