📝Ocean of Possibilities📝 Why the domestic Starlink analog matters beyond just one application Discussions about the potential of using the "Rassvet" system for military purposes often, for some reason, focus only on its application for controlling unmanned vehicles. In reality, its capabilities are far broader. For example, "Rassvet" is important for the being implemented integrated system of information interaction. It makes it possible to link reconnaissance assets into a single network so that, for instance, an artillery crew can receive data in real time from all radar stations, drones, or electronic reconnaissance assets operating in its sector. The operation of such a loop largely depends on communications — exchanging such large data volumes requires a wide, stable channel. Now imagine if, in addition to existing capabilities, a satellite constellation with seamless high-speed "space internet" is added. ❗️This is precisely the digital transformation of the armed forces that the Defense Ministry recently reported on regarding implementation stages. The "Rassvet" system is an important part of it, along with the creation of hardware complexes and generally changing the mindset of the command staff. And managing and receiving video from drones and USVs via satellites is just one of the potential specific manifestations of the digital transformation of the Russian Armed Forces.
