🏠 4% mortgage rates: one of the key advantages of investing in Dubai real estate When choosing a property, investors typically focus on location, rental yields, and capital appreciation potential. However, there is another factor that directly impacts overall profitability — mortgage financing conditions. According to March–May 2026 market data, investors from India and Egypt accounted for 20.6% and 12.6% of all real estate transactions in Dubai, respectively, making them among the most active international buyers in the UAE property market. We decided to compare mortgage financing conditions in these countries and see how interest rates differ. 🇮🇳 India Average mortgage rates currently range from 7% to 9% per year. This results in higher monthly payments and significantly greater total interest costs over the life of the loan. 🇪🇬 Egypt For most buyers, mortgage rates range between 8% and 12% per year. At these levels, financing costs substantially increase the investor’s financial burden and reduce overall investment efficiency. 🇦🇪 UAE Today, mortgage programs in the UAE are available from 3.99% per year. For investors, this means the opportunity to acquire property with a lower financial burden, preserve capital for additional investments, and improve overall returns. 📊 Mortgage rate comparison ▪️ UAE — from 3.99% ▪️ India — 7–9% ▪️ Egypt — 8–12% This is one of the reasons why international investors view Dubai not only as a market with strong growth potential, but also as one of the most attractive jurisdictions for real estate financing. An additional advantage is that, in many cases, rental income can cover a significant portion of the mortgage payment — and sometimes even the entire installment.
