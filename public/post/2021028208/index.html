Punkaharju Ridge, Finland 🇫🇮🌲💧 Running 7 kilometers between the pristine waters of Lake Puruvesi and Lake Pihlajavesi, the Punkaharju Ridge is a stunning relic of the Ice Age. This slender, forested ribbon rises like a natural bridge between two mirror-like lakes, offering jaw-dropping vistas at every turn. Formed from an ancient esker system, it has drawn travelers since the 1800s—earning admiration even from Tsar Nicholas I. Now a protected landscape, it’s where towering pine forests embrace glistening turquoise lakes, creating the perfect escape for hiking, cycling, or simply pausing to soak in the crisp, clean air of the Finnish wilderness.
