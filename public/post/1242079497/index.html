✔️✔️ Evolution of Early Societies: Paleolithic to Chalcolithic 📍 Paleolithic Age (c. 2 Million BCE – 10,000 BCE) ✅ Society was nomadic and dependent on hunting & gathering. ✅ Lived in small egalitarian groups; no private property or social hierarchy. ✅ Tools made of quartzite; crude and unpolished. ✅ Lower Paleolithic – Hand axes & choppers. ✅ Middle Paleolithic – Flake tools. ✅ Upper Paleolithic – Bone tools and sharper blades. ✅ Sites found mainly in rock shelters and river valleys. 📍 Mesolithic Age (c. 10,000 BCE – 6,000 BCE) ✅ Climate became warmer after the Ice Age. ✅ Development of microliths (small stone tools). ✅ Beginning of animal domestication. ✅ Evidence from Bagor (Rajasthan) and Adamgarh (MP). 📍 Neolithic Age (c. 6,000 BCE – 1,000 BCE) ✅ Marked the Agricultural Revolution. ✅ Shift from nomadic to settled life. ✅ Agriculture (wheat, barley) and animal husbandry expanded. ✅ Mehrgarh – Earliest evidence of agriculture and cotton. ✅ Burzahom – Famous for pit dwellings and dog burials. ✅ Polished stone tools and pottery emerged. ✅ Rise of village settlements and early leadership. 📍 Chalcolithic Age (c. 3,000 BCE – 500 BCE) ✅ Transition from stone tools to copper-stone tools. ✅ Predominantly rural farming communities. ✅ Major cultures: ✅ Ahar-Banas (Rajasthan) ✅ Malwa (Madhya Pradesh) ✅ Jorwe (Maharashtra) ✅ Known for Black-and-Red Ware Pottery. ✅ Evidence of social stratification from burials. ✅ Sites like Inamgaon and Daimabad show emerging class divisions. ✅ Laid the foundation for the rise of the Harappan Civilization. 📍 Quick Revision ✅ Paleolithic → Hunting & Gathering ✅ Mesolithic → Microliths & Animal Domestication ✅ Neolithic → Agriculture & Settled Life ✅ Chalcolithic → Copper + Stone Tools & Regional Cultures #UPSC_HISTORY
