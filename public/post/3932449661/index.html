Dr. Abhishek Verma, Chief National Coordinator, ShivSena NDA Alliance & Elections, met Shri Raj Bhushan Choudhary, Minister of State for Jal Shakti, Government of India, to discuss strengthening coordination and cooperation between the Bharatiya Janata Party and ShivSena within the National Democratic Alliance (NDA).
