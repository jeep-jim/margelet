🔴 The Monday over-prepare trap Honest framing. Sunday night you read three threads, marked four levels, watched two recaps. Monday morning you feel ready. Ready for what, exactly. The trap isn't laziness, it's the opposite. Over-preparation builds a position before the market gives you one. By Monday open you're not reading price, you're defending the homework. The trader who studied the most all weekend usually trades the worst on Monday. The fix is small: have a plan, but treat it as disposable. The first 90 minutes should be allowed to make most of your weekend notes useless. That's a feature 🌟.
