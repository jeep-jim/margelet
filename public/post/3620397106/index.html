📝Emergency Mode in Crimea📝 The heads of Crimea and Sevastopol Sergey Aksyonov and Mikhail Razvozhaev announced the introduction of an emergency situation regime of regional character. 🔻Why was this done and what is this regime? During the emergency situation regime, temporary restrictions on the movement of people and vehicles can be introduced, special access procedures to the territory can be established, and additional forces and resources can be mobilized to eliminate consequences. Temporary seizure of property from citizens and organizations is also possible with subsequent compensation. The emergency situation regime differs from the emergency rule regime. Emergency rule represents a more severe measure introduced in conditions of armed conflict or mass unrest, and implies broader authority powers, including restrictions on citizens' rights. 🖍The situation on the peninsula in recent weeks has called for such measures: there are fuel problems, power supply is seriously failing, which causes water supply problems. And against this backdrop, questions are already arising about the work of service and management companies. 🚩The introduced measures will simplify the work of regional authorities from a legal standpoint. This will increase both the speed of response and the flexibility of administrative resources in conditions of growing crisis. ❗️The fuel issue alone requires very close attention: when there is no gasoline, and resellers are trading it at prices of 500+ rubles per liter.
