Good morning from Ketchikan, Alaska. Yesterday we marked America’s 250th birthday. While fireworks lit the skies across America, hundreds of thousands of mourners packed Tehran’s Imam Khomeini Mosalla Grand Mosque for the funeral of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. An emcee at the podium called for Trump’s death, and the crowd instantly erupted: “Death to America! Death to Israel!” In Israel we do not wonder whether those chants are serious. We have seized thousands of documents in Gaza and Lebanon proving Iran and its proxies planned October 7. The only surprise for Hezbollah and Iran was the uncoordinated timing - hence Nasrallah’s hesitation to launch a full ground invasion of the upper Galilee that same morning. It was the enemy’s miscalculation, not the readiness of the IDF, that prevented the war from being far more devastating. If you see in all this only world leaders, terror groups, and miscalculations, think again. The God of Israel has more than once allowed invasions - only to destroy the enemy in ways that left no room for “miscalculation” theories. The key difference was that the nation cried out to God, from the least to the leaders, and the Keeper of Israel showed up. (2 Chronicles 20) Even when God permitted an invasion that actually succeeded as judgment for unbelief, He spoke through His prophet: “For thus says the Lord: After seventy years are completed at Babylon, I will visit you and perform My good word toward you, and cause you to return to this place. For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.” (Jeremiah 29:10-11) Twenty-six hundred years later, God has not changed. His word stands, His heart remains the same, and His plans endure. As He declared through Malachi: “For I am the Lord, I do not change; therefore you are not consumed, O sons of Jacob.” (Malachi 3:6) It is not pleasant to walk in a world growing in hatred, yet the core issue is not Jew-hatred but a God-hating world. Hatred of Israel flows from not knowing the God of Israel - or from hating Him. Psalm 83 captures it perfectly in its opening verses: “Do not keep silent, O God! Do not hold Your peace, And do not be still, O God! For behold, Your enemies make a tumult; And those who hate You have lifted up their head. They have taken crafty counsel against Your people, And consulted together against Your sheltered ones. They have said, ‘Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation, That the name of Israel may be remembered no more.’” (Psalm 83:1-4) Our ministry exists to teach the world about the God of Israel, the Messiah, and the hope of salvation - so that haters of God and those who do not yet know Him will run to His open arms. When they do, they often fall in love with Israel, grasp its place in God’s master plan, and begin to pray for the Jewish people to turn to their Messiah so the veil is lifted and they receive eternal life. (2 Corinthians 3:15-16) I am excited to share this with you and to see understanding dawn in your heart as you partner with us in this work. Thank you for your prayers and support. All details on upcoming events, my new books, tour and cruise dates, and ways to partner with us can be found on our website. www.beholdisrael.org Awaiting His return, Amir
