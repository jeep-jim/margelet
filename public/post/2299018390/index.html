AR and the end of the gallery bottleneck We published a new piece on Medium today - about what augmented reality actually changes for artists. The short version: the gallery system has been the only path between an artist and an audience for 200 years. AR makes it optional. An artist can now anchor their work to any physical location in the world - and leave it there permanently. Anyone who walks past with the right app finds it. No approval, no waitlist, no geography. A physical show runs a few weeks. An AR object can exist for years. We think the artists who start building in this medium now are going to have a significant advantage. The space is still wide open.
