🇮🇷🇮🇶 Ghalibaf and Zaidi: WE SHARE A COMMON ENEMY. Iraq’s Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi met with Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf in Tehran, where both leaders reaffirmed that Iraq and Iran are partners who share a “common destiny.” Ghalibaf stated that the Zionist regime remains the number one enemy of Islamic countries and emphasized that cooperation between Iran and Iraq can resolve many of the region’s challenges. Al-Zaidi echoed that sentiment, describing the relationship between Baghdad and Tehran as a strategic partnership that extends far beyond diplomacy. Perhaps most notably, the Iraqi Prime Minister praised the massive crowds that gathered in Iraq to attend the funeral ceremonies of Iran’s martyred leaders, saying that despite the intense heat, the turnout was extraordinary and sent a powerful message to the world. This meeting comes amid expanding Iran-Iraq cooperation in transportation, trade, and regional security. The two countries share more than 1,600 kilometers of border, deeply interconnected energy and economic ties, and are increasingly positioning themselves as key pillars of regional integration in West Asia.
