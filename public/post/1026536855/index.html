🏦 THE PAYMENT IS BECOMING PART OF THE REQUEST. For most of the internet’s history, software could request information. But it could not independently pay for it. A person still had to: • create an account • enter payment details • select a subscription • approve the purchase • manage access credentials Agentic payments change that process. Coinbase’s x402 protocol allows a digital service to respond to a software request with a payment requirement. The agent can then: receive the price make a stablecoin payment access the service continue the workflow without stopping for a separate checkout process. Coinbase reports that x402 processed more than 160 million agentic payments over the past year. It also says more than 90% of onchain agentic stablecoin transaction volume is currently processed through Base. This matters because an AI agent may need to purchase: • market data • computing power • search results • analytics • API access • execution services The transaction is no longer separate from the task. It becomes part of the task itself. But faster purchasing does not guarantee better decisions. An agent can buy useful data. It can also pay for incomplete, outdated or unreliable information. Machine payments solve access. They do not solve judgment. The future of agentic finance will depend on both: what an agent can purchase and how it evaluates what it receives. 🔗 metatronics.global Automated systems, stablecoins and digital assets involve technical, market and operational risk.
