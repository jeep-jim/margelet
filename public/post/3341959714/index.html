🎙 IRGC declares strikes on US military facilities in Kuwait during Operation Nasr-2 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) announces that it has carried out a new wave of missile and drone strikes targeting US military facilities in Kuwait, saying several strategic installations were destroyed as part of the ongoing Operation Nasr-2 (Victory-2). In separate statements released early Wednesday, the IRGC said its Aerospace Force and Ground Force launched the fourth and sixth waves of the operation in retaliation for recent US attacks on targets in Iran. ​ According to the statements, the strikes targeted the KJL facility, the primary logistics and supply hub for US forces across West Asia, in Mina Abdullah, Kuwait, which the IRGC said was set on fire and destroyed. ​ It also asserted that missile and drone attacks struck a US satellite communications center, missile defense and air defense radar systems, a Patriot air defense complex, military logistics facilities, and HIMARS missile launch platforms stationed in Kuwait. ​ The IRGC described the measures as a response to US military operations against Iranian territory. ​ In one of the statements, the IRGC directly addressed the people of Kuwait, stressing that Iran had no hostility toward the Kuwaiti nation and that the attacks were aimed solely at American military forces operating from bases in the country. ​ It urged Kuwaitis to seek the removal of US troops from their territory, arguing that foreign military bases had become the source of attacks against Iran. ​ The IRGC also reiterated that its retaliatory operations would continue and announced that the Strait of Hormuz would remain closed until US aggression comes to an end. 📲 @Irna_en​
