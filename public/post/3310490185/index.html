🌍 Stablecoins are becoming more than a faster payment rail. A new BIS paper examined stablecoin flows and traditional dollarisation across more than 130 economies. The researchers found that both tend to grow under similar conditions: currency pressure, banking stress and sovereign crises. Both can also become difficult to reverse once established. The key difference is access. Traditional dollar deposits move through the banking system. Stablecoins can provide access to dollar liquidity outside much of that infrastructure and appear less affected by capital-flow restrictions. That changes how the asset should be understood. A stablecoin is not only a place to wait between trades. It can also be: • a payment rail • a store of dollar value • an alternative to local banking infrastructure • a channel through which capital crosses borders The price may remain close to $1. The wider risk still depends on the issuer, reserves, liquidity, jurisdiction and the system surrounding the token. The token may be stable. Its role in the financial system is not simple. 🔗 Source: BIS Working Paper No. 1370, July 21, 2026
