📡 IRGC warns regional people to distance themselves from US military hideouts 📌 Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) warned the people of regional countries hosting American bases to immediately take a minimum distance of 500 meters from any covert residences or hideouts of US military personnel. The IRGC statement emphasized that should the enemy continue its war crimes and aggressive strikes against Iran’s infrastructure and its people’s assets, it would not hesitate swift retaliation against the criminals. ​ “As numerous officers and personnel of the occupying US military have abandoned their primary bases out of fear of targeted retaliation by our forces, they have instead relocated to buildings across the cities to direct their operations from within cities, the statement said. ​ “Consequently, we warn the general public in countries where US troops are located to immediately maintain a minimum distance of 500 meters from covert residential quarters and the Americans’ hideouts to guarantee their own safety.” ​ The statement also urged the regional people to report new movements or relocations of the occupying US military personnel directly to the IRGC Public Relations department via their Telegram account at @sepahnewsiradmin, or provide the information through sepahnews.ir website. 📲 @Irna_en​
