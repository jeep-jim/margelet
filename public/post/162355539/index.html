Masai Mara, Kenya 🇰🇪🦁🌍 The Masai Mara stands as one of Africa’s most iconic wildlife sanctuaries, nestled in Kenya’s southwestern Narok County. Named for the Maasai people—the region’s traditional stewards—and the winding Mara River that cuts through the savannah, this reserve is a breathtaking haven of life. Across its sweeping golden plains, lions prowl, elephants wander, cheetahs sprint, and giraffes tower—free and unfettered amidst an extraordinary web of biodiversity. Yet the Mara’s most renowned marvel is the Great Migration—one of nature’s most awe-inspiring events. Every September and October, more than a million wildebeest surge across the Mara River in a thunderous search for fresh grass, joined by zebras and gazelles.
