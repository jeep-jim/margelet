🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 20 July 202 Part 2 (👉 See Part 1) 📍 The Vostok Group of Forces continued advancing into the depth of enemy defence. Manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, an assault brigade, and four assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit close to Vasilkovka, Chaplino, Velikomikhaylovka, Prosyanaya in Dnepropetrovsk region, Zalivnoye, Sorochino, Zorevka, and Shirokoye in Zaporozhye region. ▪️The AFU losses amounted to up to 505 troops, 11 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one Bastion MLRS combat vehicle. 📍 The Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted fire damage on formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade near Lezhino, Yurkovka, and Orekhov (Zaporozhye region). ▪️More than 60 servicemen, 21 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised. ✈️ Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces have inflicted damage on the assembly and storage areas of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, a logistics centre, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly and storage facilities of uncrewed surface vehicles as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas. ▫️ Air defence systems shot down eight guided aerial bombs, four U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 917 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles. ▫️ The Black Sea Fleet eliminated two uncrewed surface vehicles of the AFU in the Black Sea. 📊 In total, since the beginning of the special military operation the enemy has lost: ▫️673 aircraft, ▫️284 helicopters, ▫️185,525 unmanned aerial vehicles, ▫️666 anti-aircraft missile systems, ▫️30,222 tanks and other armoured fighting vehicles, ▫️1,761 MLRS combat vehicles, ▫️35,829 field artillery guns and mortars, ▫️66,970 units of support military vehicles. 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
