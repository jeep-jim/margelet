📊 June US consumer prices fell 0.4% month on month. Annual inflation slowed from 4.2% to 3.5%, while core inflation held at 2.6%. Markets received the number as relief. The useful part is understanding what created it. ⚡️ Energy prices fell 5.7% during June. Gasoline fell 9.7%. That means a large part of the improvement came from a component that can reverse faster than monetary policy can respond. Today brings the second test: June producer prices. CPI shows what consumers paid. PPI shows how price pressure is moving through the production system before it reaches them. If producer prices confirm the slowdown, the case for sustained inflation relief becomes stronger. If they do not, one soft consumer print remains exactly that: one print. For traders, the rule is simple: A headline changes the market. A sequence changes the regime. 🔗 Sources and links: US CPI release, US PPI release, BLS release calendar
