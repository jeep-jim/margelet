🇮🇷🇬🇧🇫🇷 Iran has rejected a joint UK-France statement on the Strait of Hormuz, warning against any foreign military presence in the strategic waterway. Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said the Strait of Hormuz "is not a stage for extra-regional powers to display military force" and stressed that its security should remain the responsibility of the countries bordering the strait. His remarks came after the UK and France said they were prepared to deploy a wider multinational military mission to support freedom of navigation in the region. Iran warned that any external military activity in the Strait of Hormuz could have serious consequences and said those creating tensions would bear responsibility.
