A phishing victim lost nearly $1 million in USDT⚠️ An unknown Ethereum user lost 999,999 USDT after approving a malicious token approval request in their wallet, according to analysts at Scam Sniffer. First, the attacker attempted to drain exactly 1 million USDT from the wallet using the Multicall function. Then, through several transfers, they withdrew the entire available balance. In total, the funds were stolen across three transactions: 639,999 USDT, 159,999 USDT, and 200,000 USDT. The addresses that received the tokens have already been flagged by blockchain explorers as being linked to phishing attacks under the label Fake_Phishing. Scam Sniffer analysts reminded users to carefully review every signature request and avoid approving transactions in a rush. In May, the Ethereum Foundation introduced a concept for a new standard designed to protect users from “blind signing” transactions, but it has not yet been implemented.
