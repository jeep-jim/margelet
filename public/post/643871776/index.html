The Dawn of a New Era The Lord is doing an incredible work, and after a deep season of prayer and soul-searching, I am thrilled to share a major update about the future of this channel. God has been opening massive doors for ministry, and the opportunities He has entrusted to our team have grown significantly. To fully step into this calling, I am shifting my primary focus away from the daily news cycle so that I can pour my time and energy exactly where the Lord is leading. For a long time, I have carried a deep passion for the news, especially regarding the ongoing war. But living in the 24/7 news cycle can easily pull our focus toward anger, sadness, or fear. While those feelings are a real part of our human experience, I want the lasting legacy of this channel to be one of absolute triumph and faith! Instead of tracking anxiety, I want to point you to hope. Instead of daily speculation, I want to anchor you in God’s promises. Instead of chasing constantly changing headlines, we are going to fix our eyes on His unchanging Word. Scripture reminds us how precious our days are: “See then that you walk circumspectly, not as fools but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil.” Ephesians 5:15-16 (NKJV) The absolute best way to redeem the time right now is to devote this platform more fully to teaching God’s Word, proclaiming the incredible hope of His soon return, and advocating for Israel through a powerful biblical lens. What’s Next for This Channel? This Telegram channel is getting an upgrade. We are transforming this space into an even stronger, more vibrant platform for everything Behold Israel stands for. In the coming days, you will see a new name and logo reflect this focus. Our team is firing on all cylinders to bring you daily content designed to equip, encourage, and strengthen you in your walk with the Lord. Where to Find News Updates Don't worry, I won't be disappearing from the news world entirely! Once a week, I will still join my good friend Barry Stagner on MidEast & Beyond. Together, we will continue to break down the most significant global and Middle Eastern developments through the clear lens of Bible prophecy. The words of the apostle Paul are burning in my heart as we step into this next season: “But none of these things move me; nor do I count my life dear to myself, so that I may finish my race with joy, and the ministry which I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify to the gospel of the grace of God.” Acts 20:24 (NKJV) Thank you from the bottom of my heart for allowing me to be a part of your daily lives for so many years. The best is yet to come! Let’s continue this journey together with joy - not with our eyes fixed on the headlines, but on Yeshua, the Author and Finisher of our faith. Awaiting His Return with Joy, Amir
