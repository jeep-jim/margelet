The Lord continues to bless my time here in New Zealand! After the challenges in Tauranga, where a group of aggressive pro-Palestinian protesters tried to disrupt our services with loud shouting, banging on windows, pouring fake blood at the entrance, and attempting to intimidate both the church staff and attendees, it was a joy to continue the journey in Hamilton. The protesters showed up there as well. However, unlike the previous evening, the steady rain quickly sent them running for cover. Their demonstration for the false genocide narrative was short-lived, while inside, believers from all across New Zealand’s North Island gathered for a wonderful evening of worship, sweet fellowship, and a deep study of God’s Word. Once again, the Lord proved that no amount of intimidation - or even bad weather - can stop His people from gathering around the truth of His Word. One funny moment from the evening was walking into the foyer and seeing my own photos hanging on the wall. Why was that so amusing? Because the church meets in a funeral home! As I was teaching the living in a place normally reserved for the dead, I looked across the foyer and there were my pictures already hanging on the wall. I couldn’t help but laugh. I may well have been the first living speaker to have his photos displayed in a funeral home while he was still very much alive. Only God can turn a funeral home into a place where people come to hear the message of eternal life! A heartfelt thank you to pastor Jake, your fearless wife Tekla and everyone who worked so hard behind the scenes to make this evening possible. Your faithful service, warm hospitality, and love for the Lord and His people were evident in every detail. Thank you all for your fervent prayers and faithful support. They mean more than you know. Onwards and upwards! Awaiting His Return, Amir
