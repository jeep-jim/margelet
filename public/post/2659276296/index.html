⚡️🇹🇭🇨🇳⚔️🇯🇵 📈 Aapico Hitech, Thailand's largest listed parts supplier CEO Yeap Swee Chuan, has came out with a thundering revelation, where he predicts that China will "win the war" on EVs, citing lower costs and better technology as a reason. Yeap told Nikkei Asia in an exclusive interview, "In the long term, survival is going to require greater collaboration with the Chinese. It's going to be hard to stop them." Thailand's automotive industry has been long plagued by domestic factors such as lack of political stability, limited government support for businesses and slow economic growth, with Toyota's Asia Region CEO Masahiko Maeda being particularly bearish on this aspect, which accounts for 12% of thailand's GDP. Yeap expects continuous retreat of figures in automotive production, citing change of export patterns and domestic household debt strains. This evaluation is supported by figures provided by the Thailand Automotive Institute, Reflecting the decline from 2.46million (2013) to 1.45million (2025). Yeap also believes the rapid reordering of this industry driven by Chinese EVs would lead to a "new type of globalization", where sector giants would have to re-adjust and smaller companies will supply parts to manufacturers around the world. The main contender to the automotive spot is the Japanese, which accounts for 26% of new global car sales.
