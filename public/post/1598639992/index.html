🎮 Downloaded a Steam Game, Lost Your Crypto 👮‍♀️ The FBI has arrested a 21-year-old hacker who uploaded fake games to Steam containing hidden crypto-draining malware. Over two years, he allegedly: • Infected 8,000+ computers. • Gained access to 80 crypto wallets. • Stole at least $220,000 in crypto. Ironically, he was caught after converting the stolen funds into Uber Eats gift cards. Investigators reportedly traced the food delivery address—and showed up with a search warrant instead of lunch. 🍔 The fake games looked legitimate and even launched normally, making them difficult to spot. The case is a reminder that installing unknown software can put your wallet at risk, especially if your seed phrase or wallet files are stored on the same device. 🛡 Treat every unfamiliar download as a potential security risk—even if it comes from a trusted platform.
