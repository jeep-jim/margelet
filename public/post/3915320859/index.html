🔻 Iran, Iraq sign four agreements, MoUs to expand bilateral cooperation 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Senior Iranian and Iraqi officials have signed four agreements and memorandums of understanding (MoUs) aimed at upgrading bilateral relations and expanding cooperation between the two countries. The documents were signed on Thursday in the presence of Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Iraqi Prime Minister Ali Faleh al-Zaidi during the Iraqi premier’s official visit to Tehran. ​ The four documents cover road freight transportation, the construction of the Khosravi–Khanaqin–Baghdad railway, the establishment of a sister-city relationship between Tehran and Baghdad, and cooperation in public administration, training and human resources management. ​ The agreements were signed by senior officials from the two countries at the Sa’dabad Cultural-Historical Complex in Tehran. 📲 @Irna_en​
