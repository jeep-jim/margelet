TYPE OF ENTRIES 🕯 Entry at Open: Enter the market at the start of the next candle. In the example, this means entering at the open of the red (bearish) candle following a green (bullish) one. Entry at Break: Enter the market when the price breaks the low of the previous candle. The example shows entering as soon as the price drops below the low of the green candle, indicating a possible bearish continuation. It's useful for traders looking to understand and improve their entry strategies in the forex market. Good morning! @nursejannah ⛅️
