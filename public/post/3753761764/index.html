🔴 The Thursday urgency trap Honest framing. Thursday has a specific pressure that Monday doesn't. By day four, the week has a shape. You know roughly how it went. And that shape creates an urge: make something happen before Friday closes the book. That urge is not a signal. It's arithmetic anxiety wearing a strategy costume. The week doesn't owe you a setup. Thursday is not a deadline. Retail traders lose money on Thursdays not because liquidity is bad, but because they stop waiting and start reaching. The fix is not a better entry model. The fix is recognizing when you're trading the calendar instead of the market 🌟.
