⚡️ Scientific, technological cooperation key to Iran-Iraq strategic ties: Minister 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Information and Communications Technology, Sattar Hashemi, has emphasized Iran’s readiness to share expertise in electronic health, space technology, and communications research, calling scientific and technological cooperation the cornerstone for broader strategic ties between Tehran and Baghdad. Hashemi met with Abdul Hussein al-Moussawi, the Iraqi Health Minister and the Acting Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, on Wednesday. ​ During the meeting, the two officials emphasized that enhancing scientific and technological cooperation is a top priority for the two countries. ​ Referring to the deep historical and cultural bonds, the two sides considered these commonalities a suitable platform for expanding interactions in new fields of knowledge and technology. ​ Highlighting the capabilities and advancements of the Islamic Republic in the space industry, Hashemi said, “In recent years, Iran has made significant progress in telecommunications and remote sensing satellites and is prepared to enhance these capabilities within the framework of joint cooperation with Iraq”. ​ He also cited the establishment of a joint research and development center, cooperation in satellite imagery, development of ground stations, and expansion of cooperation in the space industry as key areas of collaboration between the two countries. ​ For his part, Al-Moussawi emphasized the importance of Iran’s experiences in health, education, and technology, highlighting the Iraqi government’s commitment to fostering and enhancing scientific and technological relations with the Islamic Republic of Iran. 📲 @Irna_en​
