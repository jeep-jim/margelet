We sat down again after two years to understand how he didn't just "stay relevant," but managed to maintain both his scale and his pace. Today, Vova is known all over the world. His videos consistently generate hundreds of millions of views, his content gets recognized by the biggest names in the industry, and people on the level of Joe Rogan and Arnold Schwarzenegger follow his media. In the podcast, we talked about how not to lose your mind when you become world-famous and how not to lose your hunger for results, how to get rid of the small-town mentality that holds you back from growing, and what creators should do at a time when AI starts creating digital clones of them that compete with the original. This is a conversation about why the scale of your success is always secondary to discipline and the willingness to be just the right kind of obsessed with what you do.
