Explain how to request removal from Google, Bing, and other search engines when eligible. Review my social profiles for exposed personal information and location clues. Help me remove metadata from photos, videos, PDFs, and documents before posting. Check whether my email addresses have appeared in known data breaches and explain the safest next steps. Create an ongoing monitoring system so I can catch new exposures. Then create a prioritized checklist beginning with the highest-risk exposures. Be honest about what cannot be fully erased. Do not recommend fake identities, impersonation, deceptive information, illegal access, harassment, or attempts to evade law enforcement. At the end, create a tracker with these columns: Platform or website | Personal information exposed | Removal method | Request date | Follow-up date | Status | Confirmation saved Сохраняем и используем.
