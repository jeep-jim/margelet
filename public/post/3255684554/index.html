❕ Most trading problems aren't strategy-related. Traders blame setups, but losses stem from emotions after normal losing trades. Frustration leads to revenge trading, bigger positions, broken rules—erasing weeks of gains in hours. Try this: review recent trading history. Split losing days into two groups: 📊 Those following your plan (normal losses), and 🛍 Those breaking rules (emotional trading). Remove the second group—many find their edge was solid; the real issue is emotional control. Your journal should track: 🪙 When do you break rules? 🪙 After how many losses does judgment decline? 🪙 Do you trade worse at certain times? 🪙 What mistake costs most monthly? Answers beat new indicators. Top traders survive not by perfection, but by spotting emotional shifts early—and stopping. ✅@futures #TradingPsychology #RiskManagement #EmotionsInTrading Crypto flash
