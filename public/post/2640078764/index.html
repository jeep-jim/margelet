➡️ When does a stablecoin become cash? A stablecoin can trade at one dollar without being treated as cash inside the financial system. That distinction is becoming one of the next major regulatory questions. In written input to the US Securities and Exchange Commission, Ripple asked for qualified payment stablecoins to be recognised consistently as allowable assets, cash equivalents and eligible reserve assets in broker-dealer rules. The request sounds technical. Its effects are not. Classification determines: - whether a financial firm can hold the asset as collateral - how much capital must sit against it - how customer assets are protected - whether the token can move efficiently through regulated infrastructure Stablecoin adoption will not be decided by transaction speed alone. It will also be decided by the balance-sheet category regulators place around it. The token can move in seconds. The legal classification decides where it is allowed to go. ➡️ SEC Crypto Task Force Written Input
