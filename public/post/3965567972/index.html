⚡⚽🇪🇺 All 55 UEFA member countries voted in favor of boycotting the World Cup if Gianni Infantino moves forward with the project to open a subsidiary company of FIFA to private investors. The FFE will handle the sale of all rights connected to FIFA (sponsorships, tickets, licenses, television packages) and the operational management of tournaments.
