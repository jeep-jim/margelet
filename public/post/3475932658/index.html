The following post may make some of you a bit uncomfortable. However, some things need to be said. Over the past seven years, since I began writing books, I have received thousands of emails from people all over the world sharing how the Lord used them to draw them to Himself, strengthen their faith, clarify difficult biblical truths, and rescue them from confusion that had left them vulnerable to spiritual deception. I have never been in the habit of posting those emails because they are deeply personal and belong to the people who wrote them. Now comes the part that some people seem to struggle with: promoting the books. The same people who gladly pay for milk, bread, cheese, vegetables, fruit, newspapers, magazines, streaming services, and countless other products rarely question why those things are advertised. Yet when it comes to books that have helped lead many to faith in the Messiah and strengthen countless believers in their walk with Him, some suddenly become critical. “Why are you promoting your books?” “Why are you talking about your books?” “Why are you encouraging people to buy your books?” “Why are you writing books at all?” The answer is simple. Writing is a calling. Teaching is a calling. These books do not simply sit on shelves. They often become the very theme of conferences and Bible teaching events around the world where thousands gather to study God’s Word together. We gladly teach at those conferences without ever asking the hosting churches or ministries to cover any of our travel or ministry expenses. We consider it a privilege to serve the body of Christ. Not one of my books has ever been written for the purpose of making money. Every one of them was written because I believe the Lord entrusted me with a message that needs to be shared while there is still time. Many people also ask why I encourage pre-orders. The answer is equally simple. Strong pre-orders send a signal to booksellers - especially secular bookstores - that a title is in demand and deserves shelf space in their brick-and-mortar stores as well as their online catalogs. That gives the book the opportunity to reach people who would likely never step into a Christian bookstore or attend a Bible conference. So when I encourage pre-orders, it isn’t about chasing bestseller lists or sales numbers. It is about creating the greatest possible opportunity for God’s truth to reach people who desperately need it. Rather than choosing to be offended or pointing a finger, I would ask you to pray that The Elijah Mandate will find its way onto the shelves of bookstores across the world and into the hands of people who need its message. Pray that the Lord will use it to awaken believers, strengthen His church, and draw many to the saving knowledge of the Messiah. I knew The Elijah Mandate would face opposition because of its urgent message and its relevance for the days in which we live. What I did not anticipate was how much of that opposition would come from within the camp, as the enemy distracts people from the message by causing them to focus on the messenger. Time is short. Let’s not waste it on petty things. Let us redeem every opportunity the Lord gives us to proclaim His truth and shine His light in an increasingly dark world. “And let us not grow weary while doing good, for in due season we shall reap if we do not lose heart.” Galatians 6:9 (NKJV) Awaiting His Return, Amir https://www.theelijahmandatebook.com
