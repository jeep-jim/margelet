NEW: 🚨 BITCOIN MINING DIFFICULTY DROPS 10.09% — THE SECOND-SHARPEST DECLINE OF 2026 At block 953,568, Bitcoin's mining difficulty decreased by 10.09%, marking the second-largest drop this year. The adjustment follows Bitcoin’s ~15% price pullback in early June, which pressured miner profit margins and led some operations to power down older, less efficient ASIC machines. This reduction eases the computational burden for active miners, improving block discovery odds and boosting short-term profitability—key for maintaining network stability during volatile periods. The shift also coincides with a trend among mining firms migrating infrastructure toward AI and high-performance computing, diverting power and hardware resources away from Bitcoin mining.
