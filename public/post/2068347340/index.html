DIY – Beauty Water for Healthy, Glowing Skin • Take 50g of rose petals and jasmine flowers. • Arrange them around a large saucepan. • Place a small bowl in the center of the pan. • Pour just enough water to cover the flowers. • Cover the saucepan with a lid and place a few ice cubes on top. • Simmer on low heat for 15 minutes. • Strain the infused water from the pan. • Transfer into a glass bottle — your beauty water is ready. How to Use: Use this as the first step in your skincare routine. Soak a cotton pad and gently wipe your face — you’ll notice impurities (a blackish residue) on the cotton. Then wash your face and pat dry. Best used before showering or at night. @RevealedEye Unfiltered lens
