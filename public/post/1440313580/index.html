🇷🇺⚔️🇺🇦🌾📉 Russian strikes on ports could cut Ukrainian grain exports by nearly a third, Reuters reports. Due to intensified attacks on ports, ships, railways, and energy infrastructure, exports through Odessa could fall from the current 6 million to 4 million tons per month, according to Taras Vysotsky, Deputy Minister of Economy of Ukraine. The problem is exacerbated by the fact that more than 90% of Ukrainian exports go through Black Sea ports. Redirecting cargo to the Danube is only partially possible – no more than 1 million tons per month – and such logistics are significantly more expensive. It is reported that losses to port terminals since the start of the war have already reached $1.5 billion.
