Saudi Arabia is expanding its military activity across the Middle East, having carried out airstrikes against Axis of Resistance Iraqi fighters alongside the US. 🔴 As a pretext, Saudi Arabia accused Iraqi militias of launching drone attacks against its oil facilities over the past two days – claims denied by Iraq’s Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF), an umbrella organization of Iraqi militias, while Yemen's Ansar Allah claimed responsibility for the attacks 🔴 As a result of US-Saudi airstrikes, at least 20 Iraqi fighters were killed and 32 wounded across seven provinces ➡️ The PMF declared they will stay on high alert ➡️ Harakat al-Nujaba, an armed group in the PMF, demanded the expulsion of US forces from Iraq, called for an end to cooperation with Saudi Arabia, and threatened retaliatory attacks against US and Saudi interests ➡️ Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi’s visit to Saudi Arabia scheduled for July 30 has been postponed, according to reports 🔴 US neocon think tank CSIS claims Saudi Arabia's move may signal deeper involvement to come, including potential willingness to allow the US to use bases in Saudi Arabia, for attacks on Iran and/or for operations in the Strait of Hormuz 🔴 Saudi Arabia’s latest involvement in the conflict began with strikes on Sanaa and Hodeidah in Yemen, followed by retaliatory moves from Ansar Allah, including a blockade of Saudi shipping in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
