In a statement released on Sunday, the Army said the attacks began early in the day in response to the United States’ violations of international treaties and attacks on civilian areas. ​ According to the statement, Iranian drones targeted a US ammunition depot at Camp Buehring (formerly Camp Udairi) as well as equipment and personnel hangars at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait. The Army said both sites had been struck for the second time. ​ The statement reiterated that Iran’s military campaign is aimed at defending the country’s national identity, centuries-old history, and its people, who, in line with Islamic principles, neither commit oppression nor submit to it. ​ It added that, guided by “this divine teaching,” Iranian Army forces remain steadfast in defending the nation against the enemies of Iran and humanity. ​ Earlier in the day, the Army said that it had targeted the ammunition depots of the terrorist American army at Camp Udairi and the Patriot radar and aerial radar of the US military at the Ali Al-Salem base in Kuwait with heavy volleys of destructive drones. 📲 @Irna_en​
