Fermarci è impossibile - La lotta No Tav in Val di Susa "It's Impossible…

Fermarci è impossibile - La lotta No Tav in Val di Susa "It's Impossible to Stop Us" is a documentary that recounts through images and direct testimonies the resistance and determination of the No Tav movement in the Val di Susa region. The film traces the turbulent years of the popular struggle, from the first surveys in 2010 to the major mobilizations of the summer of 2013, focusing on the historic day of July 3, 2011, and the clashes around the construction site of the Free Republic of Maddalena in Chiomonte. A fundamental work to understand the historical memory of a territory that does not bow to environmental devastation and the logic of useless and imposed large-scale projects.
