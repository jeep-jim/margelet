📄 VP Aref calls for full immunity in cybersecurity, scientific progress 📌 Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad-Reza Aref has emphasized that Iran must urgently pursue full cybersecurity immunity, alongside scientific and technological progress, to achieve a leading position in the region and strengthen its defense capabilities. Speaking in a meeting on Monday, Aref said that the goal of achieving first place in the region in the field of science and technology development is outlined in the development vision document, adding that although successes have been achieved in this regard, not all goals have yet been realized. ​ He emphasized that prioritizing the development of modern technologies in both the government and private sectors, particularly over the past two years, is essential to realizing the vision document and attaining a leading position in the region, as well as becoming globally influential in science and technology. ​ Referring to the country’s advances since the 12-day war, he said, “These successes are the result of the efforts of our young, knowledgeable, and motivated elites and scientists.” ​ Aref described the Zionist regime as an “illegitimate entity” in the region, emphasizing, “This situation will not be resolved with an agreement or a ceasefire. In addition to diplomatic efforts, we must seriously pursue progress in science and technology to enhance our defense capabilities.” ​ Reiterating the importance of complete immunity in cybersecurity, he noted, “Serious measures must be taken in this area. Iran’s position on cybersecurity is defensive; however, we must also be prepared to strike the enemy”. 📲 @Irna_en​
