🖥 1K Arbaeen Mawkebs operate in southwestern Iran 📌 Khorramshahr, IRNA — Khuzestan’s Arbaeen headquarters says more than one thousand Mawkebs, temporary stations to voluntarily serve pilgrims, are operating at the Shalamcheh and Chazabeh border crossings in southwestern Iran. Mahmoud Mousavi, head of Khuzestan’s Arbaeen headquarters, said on Wednesday that the deployment of Mawkebs began at the start of Safar, the second month of the Islamic year that coincides with July 16, 2026. ​ According to Mousavi, nearly 1,500 Mawkebs have been dispatched from the province, and over 1000 Mawkebs are currently stationed at the two border crossings of Shalamcheh and Chazabeh. ​ He noted that 350 suitable facilities have been prepared to host 50,000 pilgrims per night. ​ Arbaeen takes place on August 4 this year. Each year, millions of people from various countries travel to Iraq to mark the religious occasion. 📲 @Irna_en​
