Core Proposal: Judy Shelton advocates issuing "Treasury Trust Bonds" (TTBs) — long-term (e.g., 50-year), zero-coupon U.S. Treasury securities offering holders the choice at maturity of redemption in face-value dollars or a pre-specified amount of gold, creating optional gold convertibility without a full gold standard. Structure and Purpose: Backed by U.S. gold reserves (valued at the official $42/oz rate but market ~$3,300+), these bonds aim to restore dollar credibility, impose fiscal/monetary discipline via market signals, attract long-term investors, and potentially lower borrowing costs by signaling sound money commitment. Timing and Impact: Shelton ties the symbolic first issuance to July 4, 2026 (U.S. 250th anniversary), maturing on the 300th in 2076; it serves as a market-friendly bridge to re-link dollar trust to gold without overhauling the entire system🎆
