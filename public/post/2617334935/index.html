⏰ OKUMA MOLASI ⏰ Whereas infectious diseases are caused by pathogenic microorganisms and can often spread between individuals, noncommunicable diseases such as diabetes, cardiovascular disorders, and many cancers typically develop through a combination of genetic, environmental, and behavioral factors. These conditions tend to progress slowly and often require long term management rather than short term treatment. As life expectancy increases worldwide, noncommunicable diseases account for a growing proportion of healthcare expenditure and mortality. Consequently, preventive measures such as healthy lifestyles, early screening, and risk factor reduction have become central components of public health policy.
