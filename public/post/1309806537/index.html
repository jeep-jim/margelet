Dr. Abhishek Verma, Chief National Coordinator, ShivSena NDA Alliance & Elections, attended today’s 9th Foundation Day event in Delhi of “Rajneeti Ki Paathshala” as the Chief Guest. The event was organised by Dr. Ajay Pandey, Founder of Rajneeti Ki Paathshala, and was graced by distinguished speakers and guests including Dr. A. P. Singh, Senior Counsel, Government of India, Shri Pradeep Gandhi, Former MP, Kumari Mumtaz Patel, daughter of Late Ahmed Patel, Shri Sandeep Choudhary, ShivSena Delhi President, Shri Abhay Dwivedi, ShivSena Uttar Pradesh President, along with eminent writers, professors, judges and social representatives. In his address, Dr. Verma spoke about political education, ideological clarity and disciplined public service. He said that politics must be seen as a responsibility towards society, democracy and the nation, not merely as a pursuit of power.
