🇮🇶 Iraq eyes alternative oil route to bypass Strait of Hormuz The idea is to build a pipeline from Iraq's Basra through Syria's Baniyas and Turkey's Ceyhan, with the possibility of expanding the project to Jordan’s Red Sea port of Aqaba, the Daily Sabah reports. ➡️ Iraq has faced oil export pressure since the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, through which around 20% of the world’s oil normally passes. ➡️ Iraqi officials admit that transporting oil by land would be less efficient and more expensive than through the sea, as pipelines are unlikely to fully replace the Strait, but given the country’s dependence on oil exports — it accounts for about 90% of Iraq’s revenues — it has to find other options. ➡️ Currently Iraq uses a pipeline to Turkey that runs through the Kurdistan Regional Government region, and plans to build the second one in order to bypass it.
