WhiteBIT overhauls its VIP Program — now one metric is enough WhiteBIT has redesigned its VIP Program so members qualify through any single criterion — no longer two at once. Four paths to VIP status: - Average Balance — no trading required - Spot Volume — including Convert, sub-accounts and bots - Futures Volume — derivatives activity - Crypto Lending — funds in active 30+ day plans Status is assigned automatically, upgrades within 24 hours. Downgrades only happen if all four metrics drop and grace periods pass — so one slow month won't cost a member their tier. VIP status from other exchanges can be transferred via verification too. Why it matters The biggest shift is crypto lending — previously excluded from balance calculations, it now qualifies on its own, so funds can earn yield and build VIP status at the same time. From level one, members also get a dedicated personal manager via Telegram, email, WhatsApp, or call. VIP unlocks lower fees, higher limits, and priority service — now built around how members actually use the platform.
