🇮🇷☪️ The graves of former Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and his…

🇮🇷☪️ The graves of former Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and his martyred family members have now been opened to the public, inside the Imam Reza Shrine complex in Mashhad, where he was buried yesterday following his funeral prayers. The shrine itself is one of the most important religious sites in Shia Islam — roughly comparable in significance to a major cathedral or basilica in Christianity. It houses the tomb of Ali al-Ridha (Imam Reza), the eighth Imam of Twelver Shia Islam and a descendant of the Prophet Muhammad, venerated for his piety and scholarship. Nearly 30 million pilgrims visit the shrine each year, making it the most-visited religious site in the Islamic world. Over the centuries the complex has grown to include mosques, museums, a library, and a university.
