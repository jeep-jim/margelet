🔥🔍🔍🔍 Stanford study uncovers widespread manipulation in 5-minute Bitcoin markets A new study by researchers from Stanford University and Singapore Management University has revealed evidence of traders exploiting short-term Bitcoin markets on Polymarket. By artificially influencing spot prices just moments before settlement, these traders profited at the expense of retail participants. The analysis identified 821 suspected manipulators who collectively made around $8.2 million. Their strategy? Executing tightly clustered trades in the final seconds before settlement—briefly shifting Bitcoin’s price, which then snapped back immediately after. The researchers suggest that extending contract durations to 15 minutes and moving away from reliance on a single Chainlink-based settlement price could significantly reduce such manipulation. 🔥 @Bitcoin_Insights TECH
