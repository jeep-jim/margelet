⚡🇺🇸 🪙 Onchain investors are piling into tokenized US Treasuries. But, why? The growth is being driven by a surge in demand to earn a yield onchain by using US Treasury Bills as collateral. Using onchain platforms like Jupiter, these positions can even be "looped" through a lending program.
