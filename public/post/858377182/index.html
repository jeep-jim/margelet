Fwd from @ #Overview #Summary for the morning of June 21, 2026 ▪️ This week, an obvious element of the situation became an escalation of the conflict by Ukraine. The enemy concentrated efforts on long-range strikes against Moscow and southern Russia, as well as strikes at sensitive depth into our rear areas. The measures taken to protect approach routes to the capital proved insufficient, and the adversary managed to overload our air defense system with the quantity of UAVs, which led to demonstrative strikes on refineries in the capital. The public reaction was to redirect the stream of hatred toward abstract individuals filming the consequences of raids on their phones and posting footage to channels, although it is already clear that the lion's share of intelligence is obtained by the adversary through covert forms and methods, including technical ones. In any case, the enemy managed to increase panic demand for fuel in the country amid constant strikes on refineries, which required a response from a number of regional heads. Another target of the enemy's information-warfare operation in the summer period is the tourism industry, on which the budgets of southern regions depend. The developed set of measures to increase the number of mobile air defense systems, air defense stations, and organize patrols along the same "Novorossiya" highway with our counter-drone systems is already being implemented, but without resources from the federal center, complete localization of the threat appears difficult, since regions independently do not yet produce drones and air defense missiles. ▪️ Without loud statements after the strike on Moscow, footage of which was broadcast at a weapons exhibition in Paris, the Russian Armed Forces organized serious strikes against Zaporizhia and the Dnieper Hydroelectric Station in particular. The destruction of the enemy's railway rolling stock continues, strikes on fuel tankers in Odesa Region have been noted, in maritime spaces drones flew against the Ukrainian maritime corridor with commercial vessels. That is, systematic measures to destroy any economic activity of the adversary began to be implemented in the fifth year of the war, when certain budgetary difficulties for Russia in a war of attrition became apparent. ▪️ On the front, our forces are intensifying offensive operations in the Donbas. As a result of heavy fighting, the key to Slovyansk has been taken – the settlement of Rai-Aleksandrivka, a successful assault on Krasny Lyman and Kostyantynivka is underway. The Northern Grouping of Forces reports successful assault operations in Kazachya Lopan in Kharkiv Region. The adversary is forced to evacuate the population from another district of Dnipropetrovsk Region, infrastructure services are ceasing operations in the AFU-occupied part of Zaporizhia Region. At the same time, a miracle weapon against enemy tactical-level drones has still not been found, which determines the course of hostilities: infiltration of small assault groups with support from fire and reconnaissance assets. ▪️ The international backdrop does not allow Moscow's Foreign Ministry and foreign policy actors to speak of the success of the negotiation track.
