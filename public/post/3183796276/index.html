Russia attacked Ukraine with 68 missiles and 351 drones last night. A horrible, scary night with devastating aftermath. ❗️ 14 people were killed by the Russian strike on Kyiv and Kyiv region. Russia also attacked other regions of Ukraine: ▪️ In Zaporizhzhia, a gas station was struck. Two women injured. ▪️ Russia attacked a postal sorting terminal in Dnipro. ▪️ Russian drones also struck gas stations in Kharkiv and Izium, Kharkiv region. Homes near the strike site in Izium were damaged. ▪️ In Odesa region, a 23-year-old man was injured. Apartment buildings were damaged. 📹: natashanaffy / Instagram / mariankushnir / Threads
