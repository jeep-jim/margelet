Russia also attacked other regions of Ukraine: ▪️ In Zaporizhzhia, a gas station was struck. Five people were injured, including an 11-year-old boy. ▪️ In the morning, a Russian drone hit a civilian car in Kherson. Two men killed. Two women injured. ▪️ Russia attacked a postal sorting terminal in Dnipro. ▪️ Russian drones also struck gas stations in Kharkiv and Izium, Kharkiv region. Homes near the strike site in Izium were damaged. ▪️ In Odesa region, a 23-year-old man was injured. Apartment buildings were damaged. 📹: natashanaffy / Instagram / mariankushnir / Threads
