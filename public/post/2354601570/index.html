💬 Official: Huge funeral crowds reflect strength and loyalty to Leadership 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The unprecedented public turnout at the funeral processions for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution reflects the Iranian people’s strength and devotion to velayat-e faqih (the guardianship of the Islamic jurist), says the head of the Ideological-Political Organization of Iran’s Police Force. “We give our very existence for our martyred Imam, just as we sacrifice our existence for Imam Hossein (AS) and for keeping Ashura alive,” Ali Shirazi made the remarks on Thursday at the farewell ceremony for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution in Mashhad, adding that the massive turnout signals the people’s love for Islam, the Islamic Republic and Islamic leadership. ​ Just as the people enthusiastically supported Ayatollah Khamenei after Imam Khomeini, they will certainly stand fully behind the current Leader, Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei, as well, he said. ​ Turning to the scale of the mourning processions, Shirazi said unprecedented crowds turned out for the farewell ceremonies for the martyred Leader in Tehran and Qom, as well as in Iraq. ​ At the farewell ceremonies for our martyred Imam in Tehran, Qom and Iraq, people turned out in unprecedented numbers and created an epic scene, just as they held enthusiastic gatherings in the streets for 130 nights after his martyrdom to demand retribution for his blood, he said. ​ The final ceremonies and burial for the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution are being held Thursday in Mashhad, where his body will be laid to rest at the shrine of Imam Reza (PBUH). ​ The Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei and several family members martyred on February 28, 2026, in a terror attack by the United States and the Israeli regime on his residence, downtown Tehran. 📲 @Irna_en​
