Crowley Lake, CA, features surreal stone columns formed by a volcanic eruption 700,000 years ago, revealed after the 1941 dam construction. These tall pillars change with water levels and are best explored via 4WD or kayak. Rent from Caldera Kayak in Mammoth Lakes for an intimate view. The site blends geology and recreation—discover arches, caves, and great fishing. Pelican Point Grill offers comfort food and vegetarian choices.
