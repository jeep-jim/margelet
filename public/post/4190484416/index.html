Add a small white caption strip under every frame with Shot, Action/Detail and Audio/SFX. Black background, white condensed title, bronze accents, realistic food textures, natural daylight, warm firelight, shallow depth of field, crisp 4K documentary photography, perfectly aligned grid, readable text, no watermark 2.
