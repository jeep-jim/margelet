The emerging Iran deal reportedly includes a 300 billion dollar private i…

The emerging Iran deal reportedly includes a 300 billion dollar private investment fund, with the majority of commitments already secured, according to the Jerusalem Post. A source familiar with the terms said the fund is structured as a private investment vehicle - not a reconstruction or reparations program - and will not include any government money or grants. The scale of the figure is notable. At 300 billion dollars, it would represent one of the largest single investment vehicles tied to a diplomatic agreement in recent memory, though the absence of government funding raises questions about how binding or durable those commitments would prove in practice. No further details on the fund's governance structure or participating investors have been reported.
