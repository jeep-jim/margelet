Polymarket has been sued over a dispute involving Strategy’s Bitcoin sale ✔️ Two traders have filed a lawsuit against Polymarket, accusing the platform of violating its own rules when resolving a market on whether Strategy had sold Bitcoin. The dispute centered on a prediction market asking whether Strategy would sell any Bitcoin before May 31. The company reported that it had sold 32 BTC between May 26 and May 31, but Polymarket resolved the market as “No,” arguing that the transaction was officially disclosed only on June 1, after the market deadline. The plaintiffs claim that this condition was not included in the market rules and that Polymarket effectively changed the criteria for determining the outcome. One of the traders had previously said he bought “Yes” shares worth $500,000 and, after the market was resolved, publicly accused the platform of being a scam. The traders are seeking compensation for the winning “Yes” positions, as well as reimbursement of legal costs.
