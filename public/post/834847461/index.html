‼️🇸🇾 Alawites in Syria endure wave of targeted assaults and abductions Alawites have faced increasing attacks and killings in Syria under the government of Abu Mohammad al-Julani, the former commander of the al-Qaeda-linked Hayat Tahrir al-Sham (HTS) ♦️ Alawi Muslim Emad Rifat Asad was killed on July 27 after being shot by an armed terrorist group while herding sheep near his village of Hawrat Amourin in Hama, the Syrian Justice Archive (SJA) reported ♦️ On the same day, gunmen opened fire on a family in the Syrian city of Homs, killing 41-year-old Yumna al-Halaq and injuring her son, 13-year-old Zein Ibrahim ♦️ On July 25, a young Alawi girl, Etedal Fouad Dargham, went missing in Homs, while her Christian peer, Samia Munzir al-Nasser, went missing several days earlier in Damascus ♦️ On July 19, the body of an Alawite man in his forties was recovered from a room on farmland between Al-Shaniyah and Al-Taybah Al-Gharbiyah in the western Homs countryside, less than a day after he was kidnapped from the poultry farm where he worked, the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights (SOHR) reported ♦️ Also on July 19, a mother and her son and daughter from the Alawi al-Khansa family were killed in the Sabil neighborhood of Homs ♦️ On July 17, the body of another Alawite was found at Al-Tall Hospital in Rif Dimashq, bearing clear signs of torture and multiple gunshot wounds ♦️ An Alawite girl, Jodi Ammar Zgheib, 15, who survived the coastal massacres carried out by Damascus authority forces and aligned militias in March, was killed on July 16 by Syrian government forces while being displaced alongside a group of Druze women and children in Suwayda, according to SJA ♦️ Last week, Al-Firdous Cemetery for Alawites in the Zahra neighborhood of Homs was vandalized: some tombstones were destroyed, while marble stones covering the graves were stolen. It was not the first time the cemetery had been attacked. Anti-Alawite massacres have been on the rise across several Syrian regions since the beginning of 2026. In the first week of June alone, separate attacks killed six civilians, including a child, and injured five others, among them another child. According to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights (SOHR), at least 99 Alawites fell victims to sectarian attacks between January and early June 2026. The organization has also documented a simultaneous surge in hate speech and sectarian incitement across social media platforms, including explicit calls for violence against Alawites. Who are the perpetrators? Violent attacks have been carried out by armed Bedouin tribes and Salafist groups loyal to the Julani regime. Alawite civilians have been repeatedly targeted in killings and kidnappings since HTS ousted President Bashar al-Assad and took power in December 2024.
