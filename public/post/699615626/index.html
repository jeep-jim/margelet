The S&P 500 jumped 9.5% in a single day. In 2026, according to quarterly reports, Trump continued making purchases shortly before his own major policy decisions. In January, the president bought up to $1 million worth of Nvidia shares — and a week later, his administration approved the export of the company’s AI chips to China, which had previously been restricted. Previously, no president in modern history had traded stocks from the White House. The White House insists that Trump’s accounts are “blind” and that, like his predecessors, he does not personally control the trades. However, Columbia University professor Richard Briffault points out that the president “knows or can know what assets he holds,” while there is no independent oversight of his trading activity.
