🛢🔍 Israel enters energy war in the Mediterranean The Eastern Mediterranean is heating up — and not just from the sun. Israeli Energy Minister Eli Cohen has declared that the planned gas pipeline to Cyprus will be built, dismissing any opposition: 💬 "We know how to defend ourselves, and no one can dictate terms to us. We have proven over the past two and a half years that those who threaten the State of Israel do not end well." The project: 🌏 A 200 km pipeline from Israel's Karish and Tanin gas fields (2.4 TCF reserves) to Cyprus 🌏 Estimated capacity: 0.5–1 billion cubic meters of gas annually 🌏 Developer: Energean, which committed $1.6B to the fields The geopolitical context: Turkish President Erdogan has been excluded — Israel, Greece, and Cyprus have "no interest" in Turkish participation. While Turkey has long sought a role in any Mediterranean gas pipeline project, Israel increasingly views Turkey as a main strategic threat in the region. This isn't just about gas; it's about leverage. Israel is building an energy corridor that bypasses Turkey, effectively excluding it from the Eastern Mediterranean's energy future.
