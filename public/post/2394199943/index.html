🔘 The Sell Button Illusion Private assets are entering digital platforms faster than ever. However, clicking “Sell” does not always mean instant liquidity. ⁉️ What actually happens after an investor clicks “Sell”? 👉 In public markets, the process is usually clear. 👉 In private markets, it can be much more complicated: ⏳ Settlements may take longer 📊 Valuations may not update instantly 📄 Documents may still require manual checks 🧩 Records may sit across disconnected systems In his latest article for Traders Magazine, Arthur Azizov, CEO and Founder of B2BROKER Group, highlights the gap between the smooth front-end experience investors expect and the complex liquidity reality behind private assets. In the end, it breaks trust. If that trust problem were to scale, it would be far more expensive to reverse than any back-office upgrade would have been. 💡 The Takeaway Modern brokerages need more than an attractive interface. They need transparent processes, stronger infrastructure, and a trading experience that reflects how private assets actually work. Because in private markets, liquidity should not be implied. It should be clearly understood. 👉 Read Arthur Azizov’s article in Traders Magazine
