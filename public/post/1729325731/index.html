🖥 Italian foreign minister: Iran-US MoU important step for regional stability 📌 London, IRNA – Italian Foreign Minister, Antonio Tajani, has described the Iran-US memorandum of understanding as an important step for regional stability, expressing Rome’s readiness to facilitate negotiations between the two sides. Tajani made the remarks after a telephone conversation with his Iranian counterpart, Abbas Araghchi, on Wednesday evening. ​ The Italian foreign minister wrote in a post on his X account that it is now of fundamental importance to maintain the agreement and move both sides towards a final understanding. ​ He added, “We will follow up and facilitate the 60-day negotiations through which the US and Iran must reach a final agreement.” ​ The Italian foreign minister also emphasized the full opening of the Strait of Hormuz. ​ Tajani went on to say that during this call with Araghchi, he also discussed developments in Lebanon and shared Italy’s position on the need to end military operations. 📲 @Irna_en​
