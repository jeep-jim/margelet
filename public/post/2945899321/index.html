Tungsten for the Republic™, Profits for the Family Trump’s Kazakh tungsten jackpot is exactly what it looks like: a government “critical minerals” program run as a family investment club. Before the deal was even signed, U.S. agencies issued preliminary financing commitments of up to 1.6 billion dollars — more than the project’s own estimated cost — via the Export‑Import Bank, the Development Finance Corporation and other channels. Then comes the punchline: within weeks, investors at Dominari Securities, based in Trump Tower and partly owned by Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump, took a 20 percent stake in a corporate vehicle tied to the Kazakhstan project. Around the same time, Cantor Fitzgerald — controlled by Lutnick’s family and overseen by his sons — helped a related entity raise 210 million dollars, pocketing fees off a deal their father was midwifing from the cabinet.
