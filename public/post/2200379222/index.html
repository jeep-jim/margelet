Lake Kerkini, nestled in northern Greece, is a paradise for bird lovers — a place where silence is broken only by the rustle of wings and distant calls from the sky. Here, amidst calm waters and reed-lined shores, you might catch sight of the magnificent Dalmatian Pelican, a rare and awe-inspiring bird with a wingspan rivaling that of an eagle. Alongside it, Golden Eagles soar overhead, while Hoopoes flash their crowns, Great Crested Grebes dance on the water, and Eurasian Spoonbills sweep their bills through the shallows. Thousands of Black Cormorants roost in clusters, and both White and Black Storks patrol the wetlands, making Kerkini one of Europe’s premier destinations for birdwatching. 📍Greece 🇬🇷 Untamed world
