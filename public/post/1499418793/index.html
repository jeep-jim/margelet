The updated Sl8 Wallet functionality is now available. Completely redesigned asset screen. Tap on any asset to open a brand-new interface with a 24-hour price chart and detailed market information, including the current price, 24-hour high and low, trading volume, market capitalization, circulating supply, bid/ask spread, liquidity, and more. Everything is neatly organized into tabs, while Send and Swap are always within easy reach. The chart will begin displaying correctly 24 hours after the update. Precious metals in the RWA (Real-World Assets) section. You can now buy gold, silver, and other assets using USDC, XLM, or other tokens and, if you wish, send them to staking with a single tap. Synced address book. Your saved addresses are now linked to your account and automatically synchronized across all your devices. You can add, edit, and rename them. Hide unwanted tokens. Assets you no longer use can now be removed from the list. At the same time, the system will not allow you to hide tokens that are currently in staking or a liquidity pool. Updated liquidity pool deposit screen. The process of adding liquidity has become simpler and more intuitive, with additional options for configuring the acceptable price slippage during a transaction.
