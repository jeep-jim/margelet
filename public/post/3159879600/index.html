Cascade Mountains, USA The Cascade Range isn’t just a geological seam from Canada to Oregon—it’s a living pulse, a testament to nature’s enduring power. In Washington, the peaks rise fiercely: glaciers carve ancient paths, volcanoes sleep under mist, forests thrive, and alpine lakes mirror the sky. Mt. Rainier stands ice-clad—an eternal sentinel. Nearby, Mt. Baker’s snowcapped cone pierces the heavens. Hundreds of trails weave through, leading not just across wilderness, but deep into it. Winter brings silence—snow-laden pines, hushed peaks. Summer wakes the range: blooming meadows, dark skies, stars blazing as they have for centuries. The Cascades aren’t measured in height, but in stillness. In breath.
