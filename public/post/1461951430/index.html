Gharibabadi made the remarks in a post on his X account after Macron (the French president) said that France is cooperating with its partners in the demining of the Strait of Hormuz. ​ He said that according to the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding, demining will be carried out solely by Iran and not by any other country, and Iran will not allow such a thing in principle. ​ Gharibabadi pointed out that the situation is sensitive and complex, adding that Iran strongly advises France not to complicate the situation further with its provocations. 📲 @Irna_en​
