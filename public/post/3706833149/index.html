🔥👁 Iran’s attack on Amazon infrastructure in Bahrain raises questions about Pentagon AI ops 📌 The IRGC Aerospace Force has launched a barrage of cruise missiles against Amazon's central data infrastructure in Bahrain, leaving the facility destroyed. Attacks on Amazon & other Silicon Valley giants It's not the first time that Iran has targeted the Amazon infrastructure: ➡️ In March, drone strikes on Amazon Web Services data centers in the UAE caused outages for apps and digital services, with a Bahrain facility also sustaining damage ➡️ In April, the IRGC warned of potential attacks against Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta, Oracle, Intel, HP, IBM, Cisco, Dell, Palantir and Nvidia assets in the Middle East ➡️ On April 1 an Iranian drone hit an Amazon data center in Bahrain, according to the Financial Times ➡️ On April 2, Iranian forces attacked an Oracle facility in Dubai Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud hub 🌏 The AWS Middle East (Bahrain) Region, opened in 2019, is a major cloud infrastructure hub that underpins digital services across the Gulf, providing large-scale computing capacity, data storage, and cloud resources for governments, financial institutions, and enterprises. 🌏 Iranian strikes targeting Amazon appears to be escalating in both tempo and destructive push, according to independent intelligence publication House of Saud. Whereas Iran began with drone attacks on AWS facilities in March, it escalated to missile strikes on adjacent infrastructure in April, and has reached the use of cruise missiles in July allegedly intended to destroy targets rather than simply disrupt operations, the publication suggests. 👉 Bahraini officials and Amazon have not commented on the strikes so far. Why was AWS targeted? 🌏 As the US military are increasingly employing artificial intelligence capabilities for their lethal military operations, Middle East-based data centers and cloud services could provide them with additional computing capacities. Iranian forces may see such infrastructure as linked to military activities and could be targeting facilities they believe support attacks against Iran, the Conversation presumes. The media outlet added, however, that it remains unclear whether this particular data center in Bahrain — or the facilities targeted in previous incidents — were used by the US military. 🌏 Remarkably, AI models developed by three companies working with the Pentagon — Anthropic, Palantir, and xAI — and used in earlier strikes against Iran have close partnerships with Amazon. 🌏 Amazon Web Services maintains dedicated classified cloud infrastructure, including a Top Secret environment for the US Intelligence Community and Defense Department and a Secret-level cloud platform supporting sensitive missions, Forbes reported in March, insisting, however, that Amazon’s facilities in the UAE and Bahrain serve only commercial customers. 🌏 Nevertheless, in theory, the Pentagon could potentially leverage the underlying hardware for its computational purposes. 👍 Boost us | Chat | @geopolitics_prime
