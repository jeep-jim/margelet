📝Large-scale raid — limited damage📝 Sergey Sobyanin published statistics of July raids on the Moscow Region. Over the month, 6,225 drones were launched toward the capital, of which 780 were destroyed directly on approach to Moscow. ➡️Only part of the drones reach the Moscow Region. Air defense forces destroy the bulk of targets over border and neighboring regions. This is largely explained by the high density of air defense systems in the Moscow direction: as they approach the capital, interception capabilities increase significantly. ➡️It is impossible to completely avoid the consequences of such attacks. Among the most notable incidents in recent months are the strike on the Moscow Refinery in Kapotnya, attacks on Wildberries facilities, and temporary restrictions on Moscow airports' operations. However, against the backdrop of thousands of launched drones, such cases remain more of an exception than the rule. ❗️July statistics show that the AFU's bet on mass drone launches is not yet yielding the expected results.
