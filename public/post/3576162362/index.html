💬 President Pezeshkian hails Iranian physics Olympiad team’s global triumph 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian hails the country’s national physics team for ranking first in the world in the experimental section at the 56th International Physics Olympiad in Colombia. In a message on Sunday, President Pezeshkian said the team’s success in Colombia, where it won three gold medals and two silver medals and ranked first in the world in the competition’s experimental section, added another golden achievement to the country’s scientific record. ​ The president said the accomplishment reflects the scientific capability, outstanding talent and admirable perseverance of Iran’s youth. ​ “I sincerely congratulate the dear students, their respected families, hardworking teachers and the country’s scientific community. ​ I believe that building a better future requires greater attention to capable human resources, quality education and fair opportunities for talent to flourish,” Pezeshkian said. ​ He added that children of Iran have once again proven that knowledge, effort and hope pave the way for successful and honorable participation on the world stage, raising the flag of Iran proudly atop the peaks of science and knowledge. ​ Pezeshkian wished continued health and pride for the team members and for all capable young people across the country. ​ The International Physics Olympiad (IPhO) 2026 was held in Bucaramanga, Colombia, from July 4 to 12, 2026. 📲 @Irna_en​
