One day… Life will become very quiet. Quieter than you ever imagined. The doors that were always open for you will slowly close. The people who kept believing in you, encouraging you, waiting for you… won’t stay forever. And on that day, you won’t cry because life was unfair. You’ll cry because you never realised how deeply you were loved while you still had the chance. We often think we’ll have more time. More time to say thank you. More time to listen. More time to value people. More time to become grateful. But life has never promised later. It has only given us today. The greatest tragedy in life isn’t that love doesn’t exist. It’s that we become so used to receiving it that we stop noticing it. We forget the people who silently chose us… Again and again… Without asking for applause. One day you’ll remember the little things you never valued. The advice you were tired of hearing. The person who never stopped believing in you. The sacrifices you never saw. The prayers someone whispered for you while you were busy chasing your own dreams. And your heart will break… Not because those moments were small. But because they were priceless… And you realised their worth only after they were gone. Don’t let gratitude arrive with regret. Don’t wait for an empty chair to remember who used to sit there. Don’t wait for silence to realise how beautiful someone’s voice was. Don’t wait for distance to understand who was always walking beside you. Life has a painful way of teaching us the value of things… It first gives us everything… Then quietly asks, Did you even notice? And sometimes… The answer comes with tears.
