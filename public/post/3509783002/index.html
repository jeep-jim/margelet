The prelims results have been declared. I’m sure this is not going to be…

The prelims results have been declared. I’m sure this is not going to be an easy night for most of us. Take some time to process things. Rest assured, i’d still like to remind you that the paper was absolutely random and not a true test of hardwork, regardless of wether you’re clearing or not. All of you have put in a lot of hardwork for this and due to circumstances beyond your control your hardwork was not only not rewarded, but not tested. However, this is the situation we are in now and this is our reality. I’d strongly recommend that you sit with yourself and think about the path ahead. Whatever our path ahead, we will do our best to become the best version of ourselves. All of that doesn’t have to happen today. Today should just be about you and your mental health. None of this is easy, but i’m here with you. Best, Shivin.
