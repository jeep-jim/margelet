THE NEXT USER MAY NOT BE HUMAN 🤖 For years, financial platforms were built for people. A person opened an app. A person selected an asset. A person confirmed the transaction. That model is beginning to change. AI agents can now receive wallets, pay for digital services, trade assets and complete multi-step financial workflows within limits defined by their owners. Coinbase reports that its x402 protocol processed more than 160 million agentic payments over the past year. It has also introduced tools that allow AI agents to trade, pay and execute workflows through a connected Coinbase account. This creates a new type of market participant: software that can analyse and act without waiting for every individual human command. But autonomy without control is not intelligence. Any financial agent needs: • spending limits • defined permissions • approved assets and services • complete transaction records • the ability to stop execution immediately The important question is not: “Can an AI agent move money?” It is: “Under which rules is it allowed to act?” This is the core principle of systematic finance. Automation should not operate without boundaries. It should execute faster inside a clearly defined structure. The future may belong to autonomous financial agents. Trust will still depend on the rules controlling them. 🔗 https://metatronics.global Digital asset activity involves risk. Automated systems can make errors and cannot guarantee financial results.
