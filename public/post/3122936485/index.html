📄 Iran calls for postal, e-commerce cooperation with Turkiye 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Minister of Information and Communications Technology and Chief Executive of the National Post Company, Mohammad Ahmadi, has emphasized enhanced postal cooperation, cross-border e-commerce, and smart service exchanges to pave the way for an Iran-Turkiye agreement. Ahmadi met with Hicabi Kirlangıc, Turkiye’s Ambassador to Tehran, on Tuesday. ​ During the meeting, Ahmadi highlighted the historical and cultural ties between Iran and Turkiye, announcing readiness to enhance cooperation in the digital economy and cross-border e-commerce. ​ He said that Iran is ready to share its “Noapa Post” smart-service innovations with Turkiye. ​ Ahmadi proposed signing a cooperation agreement between the postal companies of both countries, which could facilitate the development of cultural collaborations, the exchange of experiences, and an improvement in the quality of postal services. ​ For his part, Kirlangic highlighted the strategic importance of both countries as gateways to the world, adding that Turkiye is fully prepared to provide a platform for enhancing postal cooperation and further developing these relations alongside other areas. 📲 @Irna_en​
