AR glasses are no longer exotic: XREAL at $299 and Snap's first consumer glasses The last two months brought two pieces of news that are interesting to look at together. XREAL released the XBX a01+ for $299 - 1600 nits, 62 grams, 50° field of view. It has already launched in China, and sales began in the US in July. Snap showed off its first truly consumer AR glasses, Specs, at AWE 2026 - but at a price of $2,195 with a $200 deposit, noticeably higher than competitors like Meta Ray-Ban. Two signals that set the direction for the industry: on one hand, AR hardware is becoming affordable (XREAL), and on the other, a major player is seriously entering the consumer market with full AR for the first time. This kind of movement usually precedes the moment when a direction goes mainstream - even though Snap itself is currently targeting not the broad public, but enthusiasts and developers.
