📜 Enoch Scroll Preparing for the Storm There is something fascinating about watching the weather. The meteorologist begins tracking a disturbance hundreds of miles away. At first, it is only a possibility. Then the models begin to agree. The warnings become more urgent. What do we do? We prepare. We fill our gas tanks. We stock our pantries. We buy bottled water. We gather batteries and flashlights. We charge our phones. We secure our homes. We check on family and neighbors. Why? Because wise people prepare before the storm arrives. Lately, I cannot help but feel that same stirring in my spirit. Not because I claim to know what tomorrow holds. Only God knows that. But because Scripture teaches us to remain watchful, discerning, and prepared. God has always warned His people—not to create fear, but to invite faith. He warned Noah before the flood, and Noah built. He warned Joseph of the coming famine, and Joseph stored grain. He warned Israel through the prophets so hearts could return to Him. Preparation has never been about panic. It has always been about obedience. So perhaps the question is not... "Is a storm coming?" Storms have always been part of life. The better question is... "How am I preparing?" Am I filling my heart with God's Word the way I would fill my pantry before a hurricane? Am I strengthening my faith before it is tested? Am I forgiving while there is still time? Am I extending kindness in a world growing colder? Am I encouraging the weary? Am I teaching my children and grandchildren where true hope is found? Am I becoming more like Christ each day? If tomorrow is peaceful, then praise God. If tomorrow brings hardship, then let us be found standing on the Rock. Jesus said the wise man built his house upon the rock before the rains descended—not after. Faith is not something we scramble to build in the middle of the storm. It is forged in the quiet moments before the clouds ever gather. May we be people who prepare our hearts as diligently as we prepare our homes. For the greatest shelter has never been walls... It has always been the presence of God. "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty." — Proverbs 22:3 🤍 Prayer Heavenly Father, Teach us to prepare our hearts with the same wisdom that we prepare our homes. Fill us with discernment rather than fear, with faith rather than anxiety, and with compassion rather than judgment. Help us to remain steadfast in Your Word, rooted in truth, and anchored in Your promises. May our lives reflect Your love in a world searching for hope. Give us eyes to see those who are struggling, hands willing to serve, and hearts that forgive quickly. If calm days remain, let us use them wisely. If storms should come, may we stand unshaken because our foundation is Christ alone. May we become lights that guide others safely home—not through our own strength, but through the power of Your Holy Spirit. Prepare us, Lord. Prepare our families. Prepare Your Church. And may we never be so distracted by the things of this world that we neglect the condition of our souls. For You alone are our refuge, our fortress, and our peace. In the mighty name of Jesus, Amen. 🤍
