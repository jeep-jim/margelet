The Ionosphere lies ~50 miles up—still within atmosphere, not space. That’s where helium balloons float, suspended, not orbiting. No vacuum, just upper air. To explore planets or galaxies, we need a breakthrough. Electromagnetic propulsion could be it—if it exists, we may never know.
