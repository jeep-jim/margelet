Google is testing a new captcha with a camera... ➠ reCAPTCHA against AI bots: instead of searching for traffic lights and bicycles, users are asked to turn on the camera and wave their hand. ➠ The camera captures a short video of the gesture, and the system reads not the actual identity, but the movement of the hand: 21 key points of the hand - joints, fingers, and their positions in the frame. ➠ The company claims that the system only analyzes the hand movement, doesn't record sound, doesn't link the video to an individual, and deletes the recording immediately after verification. ➠ Currently, this captcha is only available to a limited group of users. ➠ But on Twitter (X) there is already a debate about privacy: people disapprove the idea that to verify "I'm not a robot," you need to grant the website access to the camera.
