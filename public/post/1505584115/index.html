Long-Term investing strategy When the crypto market is falling, buying feels scary. When it's going up, it feels like you've already missed your chance. As a result, most people either buy based on emotion or keep waiting for the perfect entry point — and end up doing nothing at all. But there's a simple way to avoid this problem: invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals. This strategy is called DCA (Dollar-Cost Averaging). You simply choose the asset, the amount you want to invest, and how often you want to buy. For example: $50 worth of Bitcoin every week. When Bitcoin is cheaper, your $50 buys more. When it's more expensive, it buys less. Over time, your average purchase price naturally evens out, and over the course of a few years, it often ends up being better than the average market participant's. Bybit even has a built-in Auto-Invest bot designed specifically for this strategy. You set it up once — choose your currency, the coin, the investment amount, and the schedule. After that, your purchases happen automatically, whether it's every day or once a month. The key is to choose a coin you genuinely believe in for the long term.
