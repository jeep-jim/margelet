⚡🇧🇷 Brazilian President Lula calls for a long-term national defense strategy to protect Brazil’s territory, oil reserves, pre-salt fields and critical minerals: “If we have no defense, we cannot protect what belongs to us. One day, someone may decide to invade us, and we will be unprepared. Who is going to protect our oil? Who is going to protect the pre-salt fields?” Lula argued that Brazil must modernize its Armed Forces, strengthen its domestic defense industry and invest in science and technology rather than remain dependent on the military capabilities of foreign powers. “You think I want to fight China? I’m not crazy. You think I want to fight the United States? I’m not crazy. I don’t have the aircraft they have. I don’t have the tanks they have. I don’t have the bombs they have. I don’t have the money they have, and I still don’t have the scientific and technological knowledge they have. Instead of fighting them, I want to defeat them by studying more, investing more and conducting more research than they do.” Lula made the remarks during the 78th Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Society for the Advancement of Science in Niterói. @medmannews
