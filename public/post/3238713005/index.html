Cala Goloritzé beach, Italy 🇮🇹 Cala Goloritzé is a stunning beach nestled in the town of Baunei, on the southern shore of the Gulf of Orosei in Ogliastra, Sardinia. Formed by a landslide in 1962, this is one of Sardinia’s most striking coastal wonders. It’s renowned for its dramatic 143-meter-high limestone pinnacle rising above the cove. Another iconic feature is the natural arch carved by the sea on the bay’s right side. The shore itself is made up of smooth white pebbles and soft sand. ✨ Absolutely breathtaking. ✨ Wow Factor
