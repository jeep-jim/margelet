New technologies in rear service 'From the beginning of the special military operation, the volume of armaments and military and special purpose equipment supplies has increased fivefold and the number of means of destruction has increased twelvefold. It should be noted that the Russian defence industry, under the harshest Western sanctions, provides Russian troops with everything necessary. Comprehensive work with the industry has made it possible to increase the supply of combat units with weapons and military hardware of the combat service support to 97%,' said Deputy Defence Minister Colonel General Aleksandr Sanchik. 🏭 Three repair plants were incorporated in the Ministry of Defence to increase capacity. This has resulted in a 15% reduction in the time for the repair of equipment and a 15% reduction in the cost of capital repairs. 🎮The deputy head of the military department noted that forward units are now supplied with cargo quadcopters, ground robots, and light utility vehicles: motor vehicles, buggies, and motorbikes. Since the beginning of 2026, the volume of cargo delivered by UAVs has more than doubled. This year alone, assault detachments received more than 15,000 pieces of motor vehicles. 'In order to ensure traffic safety, more than 1,800 km of anti-drone corridors, including more than 800 km this year, have been equipped on the main logistics routes. The range of food supplies in MREs has been increased, and work on improving combat gear has been continued, taking into account the experience of combat operations. This year, 2.5 million tonnes of materiel were delivered to maintain the combat readiness of the troops,' said Colonel General Aleksandr Sanchik. 🤝 At the Combat Service Support Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, the Deputy Minister of Defence congratulated the servicemen on their professional holiday and thanked them for their hard work, courage and selflessness in the combat actions. 🔹Russian Defence Ministry
