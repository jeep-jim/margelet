🇬🇧UK lawmakers examine bank restrictions on crypto firms 🔵On July 21, the UK Crypto and Digital Assets All-Party Parliamentary Group (APPG) launched an inquiry into whether crypto firms and consumers face disproportionate barriers to banking services, including account access and crypto-related transaction restrictions. 🔵Written submissions from banks, payment providers, crypto firms, regulators, and other stakeholders are open until August 31, after which the APPG plans to publish findings and recommendations. 🔵A UKCBC survey found that 40% of transactions to crypto platforms were blocked or delayed, while 70% of surveyed exchanges said the restrictions reduced their willingness to invest, expand, or hire in the UK. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Debanking remains one of the most serious operational barriers for crypto firms worldwide, often limiting market access even where businesses are ready to operate within formal regulatory frameworks. Crypto firms serving or targeting UK customers now have a rare chance to help address the issue by submitting evidence of banking refusals, account closures, transaction blocks, and similar cases before August 31. Well-documented submissions may become an important basis for moving the industry closer to risk-based banking access rather than broad restrictions against the crypto sector as a whole. 🗂️ Ready-Made Solutions | 👩‍💻Tailored Legal Solution
