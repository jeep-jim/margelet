Ever since October 7, 2023, I shifted to reporting the news 24/7. That season culminated during the major milestones of this ongoing war - the hostage crisis, the pager operation, the fall of the Assad regime, and the two wars with Iran. The sheer amount of evil I was exposed to - most of which I deliberately chose not to share - combined with increasing sleep deprivation and chronic back pain, eventually forced me to make a very difficult decision. I had to ask myself one simple question: What is my true calling? The answer was clear: to teach the Word of God. As long as I could faithfully teach the Scriptures while also keeping you informed with the news, I gladly did both. Over the past several weeks, and particularly in recent days, as I witnessed the complete madness, deception, and confusion coming from every direction, I realized the time had come to make the painful decision to step away from daily news reporting. I knew there would be backlash, and sadly, I was right. My direct messages on Instagram quickly became vicious, mean-spirited, and hostile. One woman told me she was going to throw my books in the trash instead of giving them to her brother to read. Others warned that this decision would financially hurt the ministry. Looking back now, I can honestly acknowledge that I am dealing with post-traumatic stress from everything I was exposed to while covering these events. When you’re in that state, reporting the news becomes nearly impossible. Your emotions begin to overwhelm your thoughts and judgment, and before you even realize it, your personal frustration and grief over what is happening in the world begin to shape the tone of your reporting. Instead of informing, equipping, and encouraging, I found myself increasingly doing the opposite. I am deeply grateful that I was able to serve in that capacity during one of the darkest periods in Israel’s history. But I am even more grateful that God has allowed me to recognize my own weakness and vulnerability, and to return wholeheartedly to the place where I know I belong - studying and teaching His Word. News will never lead anyone to the saving knowledge of the Messiah, nor will it ultimately explain the world we live in. The Word of God will. And as for me, the verse the Lord has placed on my heart is this: “And He said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for My strength is made perfect in weakness.’ Therefore most gladly I will rather boast in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.” 2 Corinthians 12:9 (NKJV) Awaiting His Return, Amir
