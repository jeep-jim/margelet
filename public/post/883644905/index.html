🇮🇹 Sicily's mafia is going shopping in the same black market the Ukraine war created — and prosecutors admit they're not ready for it. Palermo Chief Prosecutor Maurizio de Lucia told parliament's anti-mafia commission that the Mafia is actively seeking to buy drone weapons like those used in the Ukraine war. Investigators have already traced one weapons shipment reaching Italy via Puglia, though it actually originated in the Balkans. More alarming: de Lucia said there are signs of purchases of drone-launched munitions — weapons Italian authorities openly admit they're unprepared to defend against. He called it an attempt to rebuild a high-quality arsenal, both in Palermo and elsewhere in the country. Some reporting adds a funding angle too: the mafia's drone shopping spree is allegedly bankrolled by drug-trafficking proceeds tied to cooperation with South American cartels — tying Europe's oldest organized crime networks straight into the same war-economy black markets fueling drone proliferation from Ukraine to the Balkans to Latin America. 🔴 @DDGeopolitics
