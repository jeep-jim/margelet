🇰🇷🇯🇵South Korea and Japan move digital assets into the core of financial regulation 🇰🇷In an official proposal released on July 14, South Korea announced plans to test tokenized government bonds linked to the Bank of Korea’s wholesale CBDC infrastructure in 2027, alongside new token securities rules recognizing distributed ledgers as valid securities registries. ↳ Moreover, in a press release dated July 15, the Ministry of Economy and Finance shared they plan to bring crypto and intellectual property under a new asset management framework, with potential tokenization of government bonds and state-owned real estate. 🇯🇵Meanwhile, Japan passed a major crypto overhaul under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, introducing insider trading rules, stronger oversight for crypto businesses, and tougher penalties for unregistered operators, local media reported on July 15. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Digital assets are moving into formal financial and public-sector infrastructure rather than remaining a parallel crypto market.
