🤨 Technology 6G will be able to track people through walls... German regulators believe that future 6G networks are planned to be turned into a massive radar. Base stations and smartphones will be able to analyze reflected radio waves (using ISAC technology) and determine without cameras: ➟ breathing and heart rate ➟ gestures and body position ➟ gait and movements ➟ potentially - a specific person It will be enough for a person to simply be within the signal range. Similar technology is already being tested via regular Wi-Fi. The open system RuView uses a router as a transmitter and 3-6 ESP32-S3 boards as receivers. Developers claim to be able to detect a person through a wall at a distance of up to 5 meters, and with multiple sensors - up to 8 meters. The system attempts to track breathing within a range of 6-30 breaths per minute and a pulse rate of 40 to 120 beats. Currently, RuView remains an experimental project, and reliable identity recognition is weak. Crypto Headlines
