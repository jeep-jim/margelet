This combination has become decisive on the Ukrainian front — and the US Army still hasn't mastered applying it the way real combat requires. 🚩Driscoll formulated a key point: US Army personnel will work at these facilities directly alongside equipment developers. In American defense bureaucracy, where the path from prototype to adoption traditionally takes years, this is quite a radical approach. 📌The US actively studies all SMO innovations, collects data, and publishes analyses. And now they're transitioning from observation to integrating this experience into real combat training.
