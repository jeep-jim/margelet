🇨🇼Curaçao introduces supplier registration and crypto compliance rules, reshaping B2C and B2B operations 🔵On June 24, the Curaçao Gaming Authority (CGA) confirmed that foreign providers of critical gaming services – including RNG game developers, live-casino studios, sportsbook software suppliers, odds providers, and aggregators –will need to register with the CGA by December 24, 2026, to continue serving Curaçao-licensed operators. ↳ Suppliers established in Curaçao will need both a valid CGA supplier license and registration, while B2C licensees will be prohibited from engaging unregistered critical suppliers once the transition period expires. 🔵Separately, under the CGA’s new crypto policy for B2C online gaming licensees issued on June 24, operators may now accept crypto solely for gambling-related purposes and may not act as exchanges or VASPs, while being subject to wallet screening, transaction monitoring, and other related requirements. ↳ Licensees must immediately block sanctioned wallets and mixer-linked funds, submit compliant crypto policies within three months, complete risk assessments and relevant training within six months, and achieve full operational compliance by June 2027. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Curaçao continues to shift from a historically flexible licensing hub toward a more structured compliance regime aligned with international AML/CFT expectations. Therefore, Curaçao-licensed operators and their critical suppliers should complete the relevant registrations, license applications, crypto-policy submissions, risk assessments, wallet-segregation measures, and blockchain-monitoring controls within the applicable CGA timeframes – by September 2026, December 2026, and June 2027 – to avoid disruptions to supplier relationships, crypto operations, or licensing status. 🗂️ Ready-Made Solutions | 👩‍💻Tailored Legal Solution
