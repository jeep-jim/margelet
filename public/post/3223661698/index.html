Green Carbon Exchange Projects (GCXP) is an ERC-20 utility token built on the Ethereum blockchain that aims to improve accessibility, transparency, and efficiency within the global carbon credit ecosystem. GCXP is designed to connect individuals, businesses, and environmental organizations with tokenized carbon credit opportunities through blockchain technology. The project focuses on simplifying participation in voluntary carbon markets by providing a digital infrastructure where verified carbon credit certificates can be accessed, managed, and transferred more efficiently. By leveraging Ethereum’s security and decentralization, GCXP seeks to increase transparency, traceability, and trust in environmental asset transactions. The GCXP ecosystem includes token utility for participation within its platform, staking functionality, and community engagement features while supporting long-term development of real-world environmental applications. The platform’s objective is to bridge blockchain technology with sustainable finance by creating a transparent digital marketplace for carbon-related assets. GCXP is committed to regulatory compliance, user security, and continuous platform development. The project emphasizes responsible environmental innovation, blockchain transparency, and expanding access to the growing global carbon credit market.
