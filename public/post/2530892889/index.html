The Austrian project Allerseelen started working on a cover version of a song by The Moon lay hidden beneath a Cloud. Tristan: "Wo sind wir?" Isolde: "Hart am Ziel." (Richard Wagner: Tristan und Isolde) Allerseelen already recorded in 2026 a cover version of the second song on the first Der Blutharsch CD / LP (1996). For this remake which in some way is as well cover as remix, Gerhard Hallstatt chose the title "Fantasma. Tragicommedia". Fantasma is a word that appears like a mantra in this cover version - but not in the original track by Der Blutharsch. Allerseelen: Fantasma. Tragicommedia (Der Blutharsch Cover) https://allerseelen.bandcamp.com/track/fantasma-tragicommedia-der-blutharsch-cover Photo: "Tristan: Wo sind wir? Isolde: Hart am Ziel."
