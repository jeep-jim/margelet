💬 IRGC strike obliterates 3 US F-35s, kills troops in Jordan 📌 Tehran, IRNA — The Aerospace Force of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced it has destroyed three American F-35 fighter jets and killed US military personnel in a retaliatory missile strike on Al Azraq Air Base in Jordan. In a statement on Thursday, the IRGC announced that, instead of participating in a fair military confrontation, the “helpless American enemy” utilized “occupied bases” in Jordan to launch bunker-buster bombs at two residential houses on Iran’s Qeshm Island, which resulted in the martyrdom of one child and his parents, while the couple’s two other children have been wounded. ​ The IRGC announced that, in response to a recent crime and to assist in liberating the Islamic land of Jordan from the American occupiers, it targeted the parking ramp and maintenance hangar used by American F-35 fighter jets at Al Azraq Air Base with several ballistic missiles. ​ It further noted that the strike completely destroyed three F-35 fighter aircraft and caused significant damage to three others. Additionally, the statement indicated that several enemy officers, as well as technical and maintenance personnel, were killed in the attack. ​ Earlier on Thursday, the US launched missile strikes on several locations in southern Iran, including Qeshm Island as well as parts of Bushehr, Fars, and Khuzestan provinces. 📲 @Irna_en​
