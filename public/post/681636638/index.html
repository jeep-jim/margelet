Last night was a tremendous blessing here in Tauranga - though not without its challenges. It began with my flight from Auckland, which was delayed and on the brink of cancellation due to thick fog. Then, as we gathered at Shoreline Calvary, a group of 30-35 loud and aggressive pro-Palestinian protesters attempted to disrupt both services. They banged on the windows, poured fake “blood” across the entrance, and used loud sound and flashing lights in an effort to silence me and intimidate the church staff and attendees. As they screamed, “Free Palestine!” and “From the river to the sea!” they once again revealed the same demonic spirit that openly calls for the destruction of the Jewish state. But God… Pastor Doug, his wonderful wife Destiny, and their phenomenal team at Shoreline Calvary stood firm. We had two consecutive full-house services filled with heartfelt worship, sweet fellowship, and a deep study of God’s Word. What the enemy intended for intimidation, God turned into encouragement and blessing. I thank the Lord for yet another fruitful stop here in New Zealand. Today we travel to Hamilton for a sweet gathering with the Calvary Chapel family. I am fully convinced that He who began this good work will be faithful to continue blessing this tour tonight and in the days ahead. Meanwhile, in the Middle East, Israel is bracing for another round of confrontation with Iran. It looks like my journey back home may be yet another adventure… But God! Thank you all for your fervent prayers and faithful support! Onwards and upwards! Awaiting His Return, Amir Tsarfati
