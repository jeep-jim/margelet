⚡️ Iranian missiles destroy US firm Amazon’s data center in Bahrain 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says its Aerospace Force has hit and destroyed the central data infrastructure of the American company Amazon in Bahrain with missiles.
