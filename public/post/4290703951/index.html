How to check which services you accessed through Apple ID and Google, and not lose access to them From July 6, Russian websites and applications are required to remove the "Sign in with Google/Apple" buttons. The accounts themselves are not deleted, but access to them can be lost. To prevent this from happening, you need to find the profiles created with Apple ID and Google, and add new ways to log in to them: 🍏 For Apple ID On iPhone, iPad, or Mac, go to "Settings" → your name → "Sign in with Apple". Here you can see all the applications and services that you accessed through Apple ID, as well as the substitute emails from the "Hide email" feature - save these addresses, you can use them to log in as well. 🔎 For Google Account From any device and browser, go to myaccount.google.com/connections. Here you can see all the applications and services where login was set up through Google. Now log in to the necessary services through Apple/Google and add alternative login methods, such as by phone.
