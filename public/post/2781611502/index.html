A wallet reportedly linked to a16z sold $6.5 million worth of HYPE ❗️ A wallet that on-chain analysts associate with Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), one of the world’s largest venture capital firms, sold 105,400 HYPE tokens for approximately $6.48 million at an average price of $61.49. Another 190,000 HYPE were transferred through a series of addresses leading to exchanges, including Bybit. It remains unclear whether those tokens were sold. In total, the address moved 315,000 HYPE in a single day. At the time of publication, it still held around 45,100 tokens. HYPE dropped nearly 10% in less than 24 hours, trading below $59. Back in late May, on-chain analysts reported that wallets linked to a16z had accumulated approximately 9.18 million HYPE since August 2025, spending around $356 million at an average price of roughly $38.77 per token. If these estimates are accurate, the firm is the largest HYPE holder not directly affiliated with the Hyperliquid team or ecosystem.
