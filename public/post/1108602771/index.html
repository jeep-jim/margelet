Targeting this flow threatens to thrust the world’s poor into hunger. ➡️ The Hormuz crisis stripped the world of 20% of its oil supplies. About 20-25% of Russia’s seaborne oil and petroleum product shipments come out of the Black Sea. Even partially affecting this flow while Hormuz remains shut could trigger an unprecedented, catastrophic shock to global energy and fertilizer supplies and possibly become the push needed to plunge the planet into a new Great Depression. ➡️ Besides shipping, Ukrainian attacks have targeted infrastructure linked to Blue Stream and TurkStream—Turkey-bound Russian natural gas pipelines running through the Black Sea and currently serving as the last lifeline for Russian pipeline natural gas to Turkey, Eastern Europe, and the Balkans. 🔴 Blowback There’s no question that Ukraine’s maritime drone terror campaign will sting Russia, with a 20-25% loss of seaborne oil exports threatening to shave 2-3% off GDP if expanded and sustained. But the world economy and Ukraine’s own NATO sponsors will be hurt far more, especially if the Hormuz crisis continues.
