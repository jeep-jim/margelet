🌍 Enthusiastic turnout for martyred Leader’s funeral reflects awakening of nations: NGO 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The Association for Defending Victims of Terrorism (ADVT), a non-governmental organization in Iran, says the enthusiastic turnout of the people to bid farewell to the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution was a reflection of the growing awakening of nations regarding oppressive and hegemonic powers. The public relations department of ADVT, in a note on Sunday, while reviewing the historic public participation in the farewell and funeral ceremonies of Martyr Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, said millions of mourners inside and outside of Iran had a unanimous voice against the criminal and unjust nature of the current world order. ​ The NGO reflected on the history of divisive US and Israeli plots against Iran, saying both Tel Aviv and Washington have been hatching conspiracies right after the victory of the Islamic Revolution, to restore the authority they enjoyed during the Pahlavi era but to no avail. ​ It noted that the US also pursued its hostility through proxies in the region, such as pushing then Iraqi dictator Saddam to attack Iran and using terrorist groups like MKO, which were among the United States' plans to weaken the Islamic Republic and contain it in the region and beyond. ​ According to the NGO, the measures, ranging from coup d'etat incitements to military aggression and the use of terrorism, as well as economic sanctions, were within the framework of policies aimed at hampering the Iranian nation from its progress. ​ The ADVT underscored that all these measures, though they caused damage to the Iranian nation, could not force it to surrender to the excessive demands of the West; rather, they led to the emergence of powerful resistance forces against the United States and its proxies, namely the Zionist regime. ​ Referring to the joint US-Israeli war of aggression on Iran over the last year, the NGO said it can be analyzed within the atmosphere of desperation from the hegemonic system since they had an illusion that with the assassination of high-ranking Iranian officials, the country, as a center of resistance in the West Asia region, would collapse. ​ In this atmosphere, the NGO continued, the people of Iran and the region clearly understood that all claims regarding the defense of democracy and human rights by the hegemonic system in the world are nothing more than a deception. ​ A deception that had protected the West for years with a double standard toward terrorism and crimes against humanity was exposed with the daylight US bombing of the Shajareh Tayyiba school in Minab that left 168 school kids martyred, it added. 📲 @Irna_en​
