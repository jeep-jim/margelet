On top of that, CEO Oliver Blume plans to cut up to 50,000 more jobs and slash investment by 50 billion euros by 2030, with some models reportedly shifting production to Hungary and Slovakia. 🇩🇪🇶🇦⚡ Volkswagen’s crisis is now being pulled into a geopolitical storm. According to Bild, Qatar’s sovereign wealth fund, which is Volkswagen’s third-largest shareholder, reportedly blocked a plan linked to producing components for Israel’s Iron Dome system at VW facilities. The report also says Qatar’s influence affected other deal-making, including Hapag-Lloyd’s suspended move toward Israeli shipping company ZIM. 💬 Is this the end of "Made in Germany" as we knew it?
