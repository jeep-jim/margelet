🧐 Ethereum co-founder and Consensys CEO Joseph Lubin believes that tens of thousands of companies will join the Ethereum ecosystem over the next 2–3 years, moving part of their operations on-chain. According to Lubin, this could significantly increase network activity and L1 revenue, while reducing the circulating supply of ETH through staking and token burns - ultimately driving the asset’s value higher. He compared this shift to the mass adoption of the internet by businesses and said that, over time, most of the world’s 200–300 million companies will transition to Web3.
