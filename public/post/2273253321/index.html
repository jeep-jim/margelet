🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation from 4 to 10 July 2026 ❗️ From 4 to 10 of July, in response to the terrorist attacks launched by Ukraine against civilian objects on the territory of the Russian Federation, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out massive and six group strikes with high-precision weapons and strike drones. 💥 The following targets were hit: enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry, military airfields, ammunition depots, logistics centres, fuel and power, transport and port infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, production and storage areas of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles and their components as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries. 🚩 Over the past week, the Sever Group of Forces have taken control over Petro-Ivanovka (Kharkov region). 💥 Strikes were delivered at manpower and materiel of five mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, an airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, four territorial defence brigades, a national guard brigade, and two border detachments of the Ukrainian border service. ▪️In total, the enemy lost more than 1,380 troops, two tanks, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 80 motor vehicles, and seven field artillery guns in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces. 🚩 As a result of the resolute actions of the Zapad Group of Forces, control has been established over Druzhelyubovka, Novy Mir, Cherneshchina, and Shiykovka (Kharkov region). ▪️Strikes were delivered at formations of six mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and three territorial defence brigades. ➡️ Over the past week, in Krasny Liman (Donetsk People's Republic), units of the 25th Army consolidation troops demined territories, socially significant facilities as well as searched for and neutralised AFU lone fighters. Assault detachments of the 67th Motorised Rifle Division exploited success westwards of the town. ▪️Over the past week, more than 110 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 26 motor vehicles, and 31 unmanned ground vehicles have been neutralised close to Krasny Liman (Donetsk People's Republic). ▪️ In total, more than 1,490 troops, 23 armoured fighting vehicles, 106 motor vehicles, nine field artillery guns, three MLRS fighting vehicles, and five electronic warfare stations were neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Zapad Group of Forces. 🚩 Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces liberated Konstantinovka (Donetsk People's Republic) as a result of resolute offensive actions. 💥 Manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, an assault brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an air assault brigade, the Guard Brigade of the General Staff, an assault regiment of the AFU, and territorial defence brigade have been hit. ▪️ In total, more than 1,310 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, 130 motor vehicles, 21 field artillery guns, and four electronic warfare stations have been neutralised by the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces. 👉 See Part 2 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
