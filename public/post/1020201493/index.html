🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 6–12 June 2026 Part 2 (👉 See Part 1) 🚩 Over the past 24 hours, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated the settlement of Priyut in the Donetsk People's Republic. 💥 During the week, strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, an airmobile brigade, an airborne brigade, a UAV brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, three marine brigades, five national guard brigades, and the Azov special operations forces brigade. ▪️The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,135 troops, three tanks, 17 armoured fighting vehicles, 59 motor vehicles, and eight field artillery guns. ↗️Over the past week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces have continued intensive offensive actions. Losses were inflicted on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, two assault brigades, four air assault brigades, a UAV brigade, five assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade. ▪️The AFU losses in the area of responsibility of the Vostok Group of Forces amounted to more than 2,850 servicemen, 24 armoured fighting vehicles, 48 motor vehicles, and nine field artillery guns. 💥 The Dnepr Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, a UAV brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades. ▪️Over the past week, the enemy has lost more than 320 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 113 motor vehicles, and 18 electronic warfare stations in this direction. ▫️ Air defence systems shot down 74 guided aerial bombs, 11 U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, four Flamingo ground-based cruise missiles, three Neptune long-range guided missiles, and 4,776 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles. 📊 In total, since the beginning of the special military operation the enemy has lost: ▫️ 671 aircraft, ▫️ 284 helicopters, ▫️ 161,178 unmanned aerial vehicles, ▫️ 671 anti-aircraft missile systems, ▫️ 29,732 tanks and other armoured fighting vehicles, ▫️ 1,735 MLRS combat vehicles, ▫️ 35,321 field artillery guns and mortars, ▫️ 63,899 units of support military vehicles. 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
