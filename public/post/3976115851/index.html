🤖 Humanoid Robot Teacher Makes Debut at NYC School A humanoid robot named Sally is stepping into the classroom at Salamanca High School in New York, where she’ll begin teaching senior students. Equipped with personalized learning capabilities, Sally identifies students through individual accounts and supports them in courses like programming and AI. Still, school administrators stress that while the technology is promising, it will never replace the role of human educators. ✅@futures DegenMine
