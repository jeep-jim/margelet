🏠 Everyone is talking about a slowdown in Dubai’s real estate market. But is that really the case? Recently, headlines claiming that Dubai’s real estate market is “cooling down” have become increasingly common. However, if you look beyond the headlines and compare them with what we see in our day-to-day work with clients, the picture is quite different. Yes, the market has changed. But this is not a downturn. 🏠 At LIVRICHY, we work with investors and property buyers every day, giving us a clear view of the current market dynamics. A year ago, many clients were making purchasing decisions within just a few days. Today, buyers take a much more thoughtful and strategic approach. They compare projects, analyze locations, carefully evaluate developers, calculate expected returns, and ask far more questions before making a purchase. And that’s a very healthy trend. The market is becoming more mature. The sense of urgency has faded, but demand for truly high-quality projects remains strong. This is supported not only by our own observations but also by official data from the Dubai Land Department. In the first quarter of 2026, the total value of real estate transactions in Dubai reached AED 252 billion, representing a 31% increase compared to the same period a year earlier. During the quarter, 60,303 transactions were recorded, while the total investment volume reached AED 173 billion. These figures clearly show that investor interest in Dubai’s real estate market remains strong. What has changed is the way investment decisions are made—they are now more informed and carefully considered. In our view, today’s winners are not those who buy simply to avoid missing out, but those who choose properties with strong fundamentals: an excellent location, a reputable developer, a well-designed concept, and significant long-term growth potential. That is precisely why professional guidance has become even more valuable. During periods of rapid market growth, the market often forgave mistakes. Today, however, selecting the right property has a direct impact on the future performance of an investment. At LIVRICHY, we believe the current market phase is not a reason to postpone investing. On the contrary, it is a time for smarter decisions—when experience, in-depth market analysis, and careful property selection become the key competitive advantages.
