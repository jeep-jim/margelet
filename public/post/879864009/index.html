💥Catherine Herridge says the question is no longer what the intelligence records reveal about the 2020 election interference. The question now is whether anyone will be held accountable. Herridge detailed the “buffet of choices” the administration has available when it comes to criminal statutes to pursue charges against the intelligence officials involved. HERRIDGE: “I think this is one of the most frustrating things for folks at home and, you know, all over social media is they’re like, what am I going to see a perp walk?
