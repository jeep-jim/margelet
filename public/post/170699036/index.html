🤖 AI could reshape the internet—and crypto may be one of the biggest winners According to StarkWare CEO Eli Ben-Sasson, the traditional ad-supported web is under threat as AI agents increasingly consume content instead of humans. If the second model prevails, crypto could become the financial layer of the AI-driven internet. The main challenge? Building blockchain infrastructure capable of processing millions of transactions per second. The next wave of crypto adoption may come not from humans—but from AI agents. 🤖💸
