Core Lore of Nordic AliensIn ufology and contactee accounts (dating back to the 1950s with figures like George Adamski) 🛸🛸🛸 Nordics are described as: Appearance: Tall (6–7+ feet), strikingly human-like with long blonde/platinum or white hair, blue/green eyes, fair/pale skin (sometimes with a porcelain, blue-grey, or pastel tone). They often appear youthful, graceful, and "ethereal" or superhumanly attractive. Personality/Abilities: Generally portrayed as benevolent, wise, paternal, telepathic, and concerned with humanity's spiritual evolution, ecology, and avoiding nuclear disaster. They contrast sharply with more malevolent types like Greys (abductions, experiments) or Reptilians (control, domination). Origins: Most commonly linked to the Pleiades star cluster. Some accounts tie them to ancient myths (e.g., resembling Norse gods/elves or "Aryan"-like beings in older contactee stories). They are said to use advanced tech but emphasize mysticism, peace, and observation of humanity. Classic contact stories include warnings about Earth's future and invitations aboard ships. Today Vs. Reptilians: A common duality—Nordics as "good guys" or light beings opposing shape-shifting reptilian overlords (the serpent imagery in the X post fits here perfectly). Ongoing cosmic/esoteric battle, with world leaders (including President Trump) aligned with one side or brokering deals.
