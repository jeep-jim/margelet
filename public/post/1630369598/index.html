🔍 RESEARCH: Taking private credit onchain isn’t just about access—it’s about alignment. The real challenge lies in synchronizing three key layers: retail participation, rigorous underwriting, and legally enforceable claims. Innovative hybrid models make this work by keeping capital and ownership onchain, while anchoring borrower verification, physical collateral, and default procedures within regulated legal systems. This is especially critical for SMEs, whose loans are typically secured by real-world assets—like equipment, vehicles, property, or inventory—rather than crypto. 8lends exemplifies this approach. It acts as the onchain interface for retail investors, handling distribution and settlement, while Maclear AG—a FINMA-regulated financial intermediary—manages loan underwriting, collateral registration, and enforcement.
