💬 Real Madrid vs Fiorentina: Team news, predicted lineup and stats 📌 Real Madrid take on Fiorentina in Austria in an exciting preseason clash. Here's the latest team news, injury updates, predicted lineups, and how to watch the match live. Real Madrid are taking their preseason preparations away from Spain and heading into central Europe for the next phase of their summer plans. Los Blancos are set to face Serie A outfit Fiorentina in Austria this Saturday. Under the guidance of José Mourinho, Madrid have started their summer schedule strongly, casting aside Alcorcón and Leganés. Meanwhile, Fiorentina are looking to rebuild under new manager Fabio Grosso after a turbulent domestic campaign last year. With key signings looking to integrate and squad rotations underway following a busy World Cup, this friendly offers a crucial tactical test for both managers. ​ José Mourinho has named a 21-man squad for the trip to Austria, notably missing several stars recovering from deep World Cup runs, including new addition Marc Cucurella. The injury news remains a concern at the back, with Éder Militão and Thibaut Courtois nursing fitness setbacks, while Raúl Asencio is injured and reportedly out of favor. Rodrygo remains sidelined and is not expected back until the end of the calendar year. However, new signing Denzel Dumfries joins the squad, and 21-year-old Carlos Espí—who netted 11 La Liga goals for Levante last season—looks set to lead the line. ​ La Viola arrives in Austria with a relatively healthy squad, looking to integrate defensive reinforcements Álex Jiménez and Radu Drăgușin into the starting XI. Croatian international Marin Pongrančić, who missed the English leg of the tour, is expected to join the team and could feature off the bench. In midfield, Nicolò Fagioli will pull the strings as he looks to rebuild his promising career following a developmental stall. ​ Los Blancos have been in blistering form, winning four of their last five fixtures across all competitions and friendlies, showing serious attacking firepower. ​ Fiorentina have experienced mixed results recently, blending solid defensive displays with occasional lapses, wrapping up their recent London tour with a tight victory. ​ This matchup serves as a vital tactical laboratory for José Mourinho and Fabio Grosso. With a significant portion of Real Madrid’s senior squad resting after their international duties, Mourinho is leaning heavily on a blend of youth and hungry first-team regulars. The double-pivot partnership of Camavinga and Cestero will have to work hard to dictate the tempo of the game, especially against a technical Fiorentina midfield spearheaded by Nicolò Fagioli. Madrid’s main threat will likely come from the half-spaces, where Arda Güler and Federico Valverde can exploit the gaps behind Fiorentina’s defensive line, feeding the physical presence of young striker Carlos Espí. ​ For Fiorentina, Grosso is looking to establish defensive stability after conceding three goals against QPR. The central defensive partnership of Drăgușin and Ranieri will face a direct test against Madrid’s fluid front four. The Italian side will look to utilize the pace of Moise Kean and Albert Gudmundsson on the counter-attack, attempting to isolate Madrid’s full-backs, Alexander-Arnold and Carreras.
