📡 IRGC says it struck US military sites in Bahrain, Jordan 📌 Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says it has carried out fresh retaliatory strikes against US military facilities in Bahrain and Jordan, targeting key command centers, military infrastructure, aircraft shelters and strategic drones. In statements issued early Wednesday, the IRGC said its Aerospace Force struck the US military base at al-Azraq in Jordan in response to renewed US acts of aggression against Iran. ​ According to the statement, the operation destroyed shelters housing US F-15, F-16, and F-35 fighter jets, as well as several MQ-9 strategic drones stationed at the base. ​ Addressing the people of Jordan, the IRGC said that a significant portion of US military operations against Iran had been launched from American bases on Jordanian territory. ​ The IRGC called on Jordanians to end the presence of US forces in their country and prevent its territory from being used as a launchpad for attacks against Islamic nations and the Palestinian people. It also urged them to seize every opportunity to “destroy American institutions and expel the occupying US army from Jordan.” ​ In a separate statement, the IRGC said its Navy had targeted the US Fifth Fleet’s facilities in Bahrain, striking the NSI management center, the command and control center, large warehouses containing military parts and equipment, and fuel storage facilities. ​ The IRGC said the strikes in Bahrain were in response to the United States’ deploying its naval forces in the Indian Ocean and blocking maritime routes under the pretext of controlling the Strait of Hormuz, which had disrupted the region’s oil and gas exports. ​ “The enemy should know that now that its pirates have blocked the Indian Ocean route for the export of the region’s oil and gas to the world—a move that endangers the interests of America’s economic rivals—it must expect other oil and gas export routes that serve the interests of the United States and its allies to be closed as well,” the IRGC warned, saying: “The region’s oil and gas exports will either be available to everyone or to no one.” 📲 @Irna_en​
