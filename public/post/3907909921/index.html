🇬🇮Gibraltar sets the first clear rulebook for prediction markets 🔵On July 13, Gibraltar published a dedicated prediction markets framework under the new Gambling Act 2025, becoming the first jurisdiction today to treat the vertical as requiring its own standalone regulations. 🔵Prediction market operators must have event contracts approved or certified by the Gambling Authority and ensure they are clear, objectively settleable, resistant to manipulation, and not contrary to public interest. 🔵Gibraltar may restrict contracts linked to criminal conduct, death, serious injury, terrorism, war, or armed conflict, while EU regulators continue to treat certain binary event contracts as financial instruments or banned binary options. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Gibraltar’s move creates a practical licensing route for prediction markets at a time when many jurisdictions are still treating the sector as a regulatory grey zone. Businesses planning to launch or scale prediction market products should not rely on a general gambling setup alone but should prepare a separate licensing strategy, AML and sanctions controls, client-asset safeguards, settlement rules, and market-manipulation procedures before entering this vertical. 🗂️ Ready-Made Solutions | 👩‍💻Tailored Legal Solution
