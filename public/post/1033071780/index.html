Psychopharmacology is important for NEET PG because it is a frequently tested, high-yield topic integrating Pharmacology and Psychiatry. Questions commonly focus on drug of choice, mechanisms, adverse effects, interactions and toxicity of antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilizers and anxiolytics. A single clinical vignette may simultaneously assess the diagnosis, appropriate drug, mechanism of action, adverse effects and management of toxicity, making psychopharmacology an essential scoring area for NEET PG.
