Euro Digital: Europe is preparing money that will work even without the internet Euro digital has taken a new step forward in Brussels. The idea is for any citizen to be able to pay with a digital version of the euro, issued by the European Central Bank (ECB), including without an internet connection. Payments can be made with a phone, card, online, and offline - and rely less on Visa, Mastercard, and private banks. ### What is already raising questions: - Limits will be set on how much digital euros can be stored. - Many stores may be required to accept it. - Banks fear that some of the money will be taken from their accounts. - Millions of citizens are concerned about privacy of data. The ECB promises that digital euro will not replace cash, and offline payments will have an increased level of confidentiality (similar to cash). However, the discussion in Europe has long gone beyond just the technology. This is a question of power: do we need public digital money or are we opening the door to too much control over how we pay?
