CRITICAL: Snyder's era taught nothing—the problems with "Supergirl" began long before its premiere. According to THR, even right after filming wrapped in May 2025, there was growing concern within DC Studios and director Craig Gillespie’s (Cruella) inner circle that the movie simply wasn't working. Fall and December test screenings only confirmed those doubts. After that, the studio effectively took post-production into its own hands. James Gunn brought in his own editor and became deeply involved in reshaping the film, leading to a split vision—Gillespie’s original cut versus the studio’s version. The director’s cut ran 11 minutes longer and offered deeper insight into the main villain, but it was DC’s version that tested better and ultimately made it to theaters. Despite strong early buzz for Milly Alcock’s performance, the film flopped at the box office—earnings still far from recouping its budget. NerdNest
