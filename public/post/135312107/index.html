Best Parks in Dubai Entry to most locations is chargeable and costs $1–$2. Almost all leisure areas are open from 08:00 am to 11:00 pm, with some operating 24/7. 1️⃣Al Ittihad Park 📍Palm Jumeirah The park is renowned for its diverse flora – over 60 species of trees and plants. Its grounds feature one of the most scenic running tracks in Dubai, a shopping gallery, kids' playgrounds, and sports areas. 2️⃣Al Mamzar Beach 📍Deira One of Dubai's first parks equipped with smart technologies. A coastal location with beaches, swimming pools, an amphitheatre, cafés, and BBQ areas. Sports lovers can enjoy bike rentals, water attractions, and a skate park. 3️⃣Burj Park 📍Downtown Dubai A small artificial island at the foot of Burj Khalifa with the best views of the musical fountain. Here you can stroll along the promenade, check out art installations, ride a traditional abra, or rent a bike. 4️⃣Dubai Creek Park 📍Umm Hurair A huge park with scenic views of the creek. The dolphinarium, planetarium, botanical gardens, kids' city, and boat rides are just some of the attractions. 5️⃣Mushrif Park 📍Al Khawaneej Road One of the most interesting spots in the emirate. On site: an equestrian club, planetarium, pools, botanical gardens, and the International Village theme park with traditional houses from different countries. 6️⃣Safa Park 📍Al Wasl This location boasts impressive views of Burj Khalifa. Over 80% of the area is made up of lawns, walkways, and gardens, home to 200+ bird species. Attractions include a kids' railway, an amphitheatre, and football fields. 7️⃣Zabeel Park 📍Zabeel Area One of Dubai's largest parks: its size is comparable to 45 football fields. It is home to the famous Dubai Frame, a dinosaur park, the Dubai Garden Glow light installation park, a lake, and a mini-golf course. —— High-quality articles about the UAE's real estate every week.
