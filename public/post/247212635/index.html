Oil prices fell below 80 dollars a barrel for the first time since the Ir…

Oil prices fell below 80 dollars a barrel for the first time since the Iran war began, according to MarketWatch - a notable move given that the Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed to normal tanker traffic. The drop runs against the intuitive logic of a supply shock. Shipping data show only a fraction of normal tanker volumes transiting the strait, yet prices are declining rather than holding elevated. The most likely explanation is demand-side pressure: markets are pricing in a slowdown in global economic activity that outweighs the supply disruption, or traders are anticipating a near-term resolution to the blockage. How long that calculus holds depends on whether the strait reopens before the demand hit becomes visible in hard data. _________________ Follow @The_News
