During talks with his Qatari counterpart Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad Al Thani, both sides reaffirmed their commitment to deepening strategic partnership in defense and security. 🌏 Qatar is holding preliminary consultations on joining the Strategic Mutual Defense Agreement (SDMA), signed between Islamabad and Riyadh in September 2025, according to international media reports. The pact's key provision mirrors NATO's Article 5: aggression against one party is considered an attack on all. Kuwait has already given official consent to join. Regarding Turkey, rumors of Ankara's interest are circulating, but no official confirmation has been received. 🌏 The practical contours of a potential pact are taking shape. Cooperation in air defense, counter-UAV operations, cybersecurity, and intelligence sharing is also under discussion. 🌏 Pakistan's Defense Minister Khawaja Asif called the accession of Qatar and Turkey a "welcome development" for "regional stability and collective security."
