🤖 Wall Street is no longer discussing AI agents as a future concept. The agents have started work. Morgan Stanley is preparing digital assistants for round-the-clock client interaction. UBS uses agents to automate parts of the advisory workflow. BNY gives digital employees specific tasks, system access and human managers. Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan are developing agentic systems for trading, transaction accounting and treasury functions. A June KPMG survey found that 51% of banks are already piloting AI agents. The important detail is not the number of deployments. It is where institutions stop them. Critical actions still carry human oversight. Access is limited. Permissions are defined. The agent can operate, but not without boundaries. This is the direction financial automation is taking: not unlimited autonomy, but bounded autonomy. The model supplies capability. The architecture decides what that capability is allowed to do. Source: Reuters, July 13, 2026
