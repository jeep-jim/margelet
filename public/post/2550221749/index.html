Dr Saurav Bhishma and Shri Priyanshu Jain of Sahkar Bharati, an organisation of the RSS, called upon Dr Abhishek Verma, Chief National Coordinator, ShivSena NDA Alliance & Elections, today. The discussions focused on strengthening national integration, expanding the cooperative movement across India and connecting grassroots economic empowerment with ShivSena’s nationwide outreach. Dr Verma emphasised that cooperation rooted in nationalism, self-reliance and social responsibility can become a powerful instrument for realising the vision of “Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat” and building an Atmanirbhar Bharat under the leadership of Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi. ShivSena remains committed to advancing the ideals of Hindutva, national unity and inclusive development under the leadership of Shri Eknath Shinde, Deputy Chief Minister of Maharashtra and President of ShivSena, and the guidance of Dr Shrikant Shinde, MP.
