📌🇱🇧 Why is Iran so strongly supporting Lebanon? Recent developments indicate that Lebanon has become a central component of Iran's broader deterrence framework, the Cradle reports. 🔴 For years, Iran operated under a framework of strategic patience, responding primarily to direct attacks on its territory or core interests while absorbing pressure elsewhere 🔴 However, this approach increasingly led to miscalculations by adversaries, who interpreted restraint as permission to test new fronts. What Changed ♦️ Iranian decision-makers now view the security of key regional partners as a constituent element of Iran's own national security perimeter, rather than as a separate file that can be negotiated in isolation ♦️ The commitment to defending allies is seen as crucial to maintaining the credibility of Iranian guarantees throughout the region ♦️ Iran has demonstrated an ability to absorb strikes and continue functioning as a regional actor, even against significant military pressure Why Lebanon is so important ➡️ Hezbollah's presence on Israel's northern border provides Iran with strategic depth, diverting enemy resources and creating a constant security challenge for Israel ➡️ Lebanon gives Iran influence on the Mediterranean Sea, allowing Iran to project power far beyond its own borders ➡️ Hezbollah is the "jewel in the crown" of Iran's regional network.
