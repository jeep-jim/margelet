🇪🇺EU considers new rules for DeFi and staking while BIS questions stablecoins’ long-term role 🪙In a June 26 report on digital assets, the European Parliament’s ECON Committee urged the European Commission to assess whether DeFi, staking, crypto lending and borrowing, and NFTs should fall under additional EU-level regulation. ↳ The report also calls for uniform MiCA implementation across the EU, warns against national rules that could fragment the EU market, and supports euro-denominated stablecoins and tokenization as tools to strengthen Europe’s payments sector and the euro’s international role. 🇨🇭Separately, in its 2026 Annual Economic Report released on June 28, the BIS warned that the rapid growth of stablecoins could fragment the global monetary system, weaken monetary sovereignty, and reduce the role of commercial bank deposits in credit provision. ↳ Rather than rejecting tokenization, the BIS argues for regulated tokenized central bank money, commercial bank deposits, and financial assets operating within clear legal and institutional frameworks. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: MiCA is becoming the baseline, while activities at its edges may soon face closer scrutiny and additional rules.
