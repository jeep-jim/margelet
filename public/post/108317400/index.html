🔴 The Friday tidy-up trade Honest framing. There's a specific trade that only exists on Friday afternoon. The one you take to make the week feel finished. The setup is usually marginal. The size is usually a touch bigger than it should be. The reasoning is always retrospective: "if I close this green, the week makes sense." A week doesn't need a closing scene. That's a story instinct, not a trading one. The robots end the week wherever the week ends. Sometimes that's a clean number. Sometimes it isn't. Neither one changes what happens on Monday. The tidy-up trade is the most expensive entry of the week, because the only thing it's hedging is your discomfort with an unfinished line 🌟.
