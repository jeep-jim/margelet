🌍 Exclusive | China backs return of Strait of Hormuz to normal conditions: Envoy 📌 Tehran, IRNA – China’s ambassador to Tehran says Beijing supports restoring normal conditions in the Strait of Hormuz and calls on the international community to respect Iran’s legitimate rights as a coastal state. Zhong Peiwu, China’s ambassador to the Islamic Republic of Iran, said Wednesday that Beijing views the Strait of Hormuz as a vital international waterway and supports a return to normal conditions there. ​ Speaking to IRNA, the ambassador said Iran, as a coastal state along the strait, holds legitimate rights that the international community must respect. ​ “Beijing supports the return of the situation in the Strait of Hormuz to normal conditions,” Zhong said. ​ “Iran, as a coastal state of the Strait of Hormuz, has its legitimate rights, and it is necessary for the international community to respect these legitimate rights,” he added. ​ The ambassador was responding to a question from IRNA on China’s position regarding the current situation in the strait and the future of its administration by Iran and Oman. 📲 @Irna_en​
