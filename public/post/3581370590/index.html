Dr. Abhishek Verma, Chief National Coordinator, ShivSena (NDA) Alliance & Elections, attended the Khel Karate National Championship 2026 at Talkatora Stadium, New Delhi, as the Chief Guest. Addressing the athletes, coaches, referees, parents and organisers, Dr. Verma said that karate and other martial arts are essential for building a disciplined, confident and self-reliant generation. He emphasised that a strong sporting culture also contributes to a stronger nation. He stated that ShivSena and the NDA remain committed to promoting sports, youth development and grassroots talent, ensuring that young Indians receive greater opportunities to excel at the national and international levels.
