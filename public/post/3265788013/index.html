🌎 CRYPTO HAS THE TECHNOLOGY. NOW IT NEEDS THE RULES. The US Senate is approaching a critical deadline for the Crypto CLARITY Act. Support is expanding beyond the crypto industry. Reports indicate that a group of Democratic senators may be prepared to support the legislation, while major financial institutions and asset managers are publicly calling for regulatory clarity. Why does this matter? Because the crypto market has spent years operating with uncertainty around: • which assets are securities • which regulator has jurisdiction • how exchanges should operate • what protections apply to users • how digital-asset businesses can comply Technology can develop quickly. Institutions cannot scale around rules that remain unclear. A clear regulatory framework could help define: who supervises the market which obligations platforms must follow how assets are classified what standards apply to custody and trading But the current situation is still procedural. The bill has not yet become law, and claims that the White House is reviewing a final approved text remain unconfirmed. That distinction matters. Markets often move on headlines before the legal process is complete. Regulatory momentum is real. Final approval is not. The industry does not need another rumour. It needs rules that can actually be implemented. 🔗 https://metatronics.global/ Digital assets involve regulatory, market and operational risk.
