• 📝More strikes needed📝 Another night in the capital of so-called Ukraine was loud. Russian forces struck transport and logistics facilities and enterprises in Kyiv and surrounding areas. These facilities could have been used for delivering weapons and dual-use cargo — in particular, UAV components. According to the Defense Ministry, the capital of so-called Ukraine saw strikes on the MLP-Chayka logistics center, innovation terminal, and Nova Poshta logistics center, Trans-Logistics transport and logistics center, Epicenter logistics center. The territory of the Novus transport center also burned down almost completely. ➡️In Kyiv Region, several targets in Brovary came under fire. Impacts struck the Rozetka Terminal logistics center, most of which subsequently burned down. In Kvitne, the RLC logistics center sustained damage. ➡️Additionally, the strike hit the production facility of Epicentr Ceramic Corporation in the Kalinivka area. As a result of the strike, at least part of the building collapsed, causing a fire and equipment damage. This is the only enterprise producing ceramic tiles on the territory of so-called Ukraine and supplying it to 16 countries. Its destruction will impact the economic potential of the region and deprive Kyiv authorities of part of their tax revenues. 📌 Representatives of Kyiv authorities characterized the attacks on logistics centers as "acts of terrorism," although since late July the AFU has been deliberately attacking Wildberries warehouses throughout Russia. Its facilities, unlike the objects of Ukrainian companies, are certainly not used for military transport. ❗️Such a reaction from the enemy is unsurprising. While Ukrainian formations pursue the goal of stressing the civilian population and causing economic damage in their attacks, the AFU largely relies on the capabilities of transport companies to organize covert military cargo supply chains. This means that continued systematic strikes against them will become a quite asymmetric and effective response to attempts at terrorism against Russian business.
