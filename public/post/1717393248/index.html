Authentic Southern Iranian music from Bushehr and the Persian Gulf. Deep sorrow and mourning. Inspired by traditional Bushehri elegies and sea culture. No vocals, no lyrics. Lead with soulful ney and emotional jofteh ney, accompanied by soft oud. Very slow and restrained dammam rhythm with gentle senj accents, subtle dohol, and sparse Persian violin. Melodies should use Persian microtones and feel ancient, raw, and deeply human. Atmosphere of lonely shores, sea wind, and silent nights. Cinematic but rooted in authentic Bushehri folk music, avoiding pop or electronic sounds. Heartbreaking, intimate, respectful, and mournful. End with only ney, sea waves, and fading dammam echoes. @Farda_Ai
