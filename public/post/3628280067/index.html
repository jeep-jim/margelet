🌐📹Google tightens the rules around gambling, prediction markets, and platform liability 🌐As per the new rules released on July 1, Google will ban Chrome Web Store extensions that facilitate or promote real-money prediction markets from 1 August 2026, while simulated products may remain available only if it is stated that no real-money winnings are possible. ↳ Additionally, Google will tighten gambling advertising certification from September 14, 2026, requiring advertisers to maintain “good policy health,” prove domain ownership and control, and avoid repeated policy breaches that could lead to certification refusal or revocation as per the July 13 announcement. 📹Meanwhile, Europe’s highest court ruled that YouTube may lose liability protection for gambling-related creator content where Google's revenue-sharing partnerships give the platform specific knowledge of the channel’s gambling focus and advertising activity, Reuters reported on July 16. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Gambling and prediction-market businesses are increasingly being targeted not only at the licensing or enforcement level but also across the wider infrastructure that enables market access. Consequently, regulated gambling operators, prediction market platforms, and affiliates should ensure that every user-acquisition channel they use – from Chrome extensions to Google Ads and YouTube partnerships – is backed by clear licensing, domain control, advertising approvals, and platform-specific compliance records. 🗂️ Ready-Made Solutions | 👩‍💻Tailored Legal Solution
