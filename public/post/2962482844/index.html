🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 2 August 2026 Part 2 (👉 See Part 1) ↗️The Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance into the depth of the enemy's defence. Russian elements inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades and two assault regiments of the AFU near Prosyanaya, Ivanovka, Vasilkovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Zorevka, Nikolskoye, and Mirovka (Zaporozhye region). ▪️The enemy lost more than 355 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, ten motor vehicles, one MLRS combat vehicle, two artillery guns, one counter-fire radar station, and one electronic warfare station. 💥 The Dnepr Group's units inflicted losses on formations of a mechanised brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Preobrazhenka, Orekhov of Zaporozhye region and the city of Kherson. ▪️Up to 30 troops and 17 motor vehicles of the AFU were neutralised. ✈️ Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on power and transport infrastructure, and logistics centres used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, as well as temporary deployment areas of the Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 156 areas. ▫️Air defence systems shot down six guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 1,158 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles. 📊 In total, since the beginning of the special military operation, the enemy has lost: ▫️673 aircraft, ▫️284 helicopters, ▫️195,419 unmanned aerial vehicles, ▫️669 anti-aircraft missile systems, ▫️30,400 tanks and other armoured fighting vehicles, ▫️1,768 MLRS combat vehicles, ▫️35,991 field artillery guns and mortars, ▫️68,114 units of support military vehicles. 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
