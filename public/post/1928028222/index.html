A mother hedgehog carrying her hoglet 🦔 Young hedgehogs, called hoglets, typically begin leaving the nest at about 3–4 weeks old to join their mother on foraging excursions. If the nest is disturbed or a hoglet strays too far before they're ready, mum steps in—gently picking them up and moving them to safety, one by one. After roughly 10 days of learning the ropes alongside her, the young ones set off to live independently. Learn more about hedgehog life cycles #HedgehogCare #WildlifeMoments #NatureInFocus FeralRun
