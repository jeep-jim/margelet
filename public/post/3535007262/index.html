Samsung has revealed its first smart glasses with AI - they run on Google Gemini. The new product was developed in collaboration with Google and Gentle Monster. The glasses can make calls, take photos, play music, translate speech in real-time, provide directions, and answer user queries through Gemini. However, there is no display in front of the eyes - interaction with the device will have to be done through voice, camera, and sound. They promise approximately 9 hours of battery life on a single charge. The release is expected this fall.
