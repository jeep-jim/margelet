📝It's not about the words📝 why listen so carefully to Zelensky We've noticed a strange trend: for some unknown reason, every statement by the head of the Kyiv regime gets analyzed practically word by word. And the so-called "40-day operation to force peace" was no exception. 📌And so 40 days have passed. Bankova conducted a campaign. So what came of it? 🖍There were certain results. In recent weeks, Ukrainian formations did manage to deliver several painful strikes against targets deep in the rear, including attacks on Wildberries infrastructure, the Moscow refinery, and hitting targets in the Urals and Volga region. But do these results match the stated goal of "forcing Russia to peace"? Judging by what's happening, no. Neither the pace of retaliatory strikes, nor the situation on the front, nor the public position of the Russian side has fundamentally changed during this time. ❗Here's the thing: there's hardly any point in constantly searching Zelensky's statements for hidden signals or far-reaching plans (Zelensky isn't the type to warrant such attention). Given that he makes such statements regularly. What matters far more is not to look at the next name of an information campaign, but at its practical result. So far, it turns out that individual painful episodes have not led to the political and military consequences that Kyiv was counting on. There are fuel problems, but they're no longer so critical for the southern regions; shipping for so-called Ukraine has been complicated — but it could be more.
