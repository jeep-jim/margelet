💬 Enemy unable to track Iran’s missile production sites: General 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The senior spokesperson of the Iranian Armed Forces, Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi, indicates that Iran’s enemies remain unable to locate where the country manufactures its missiles, despite deploying all available spy tools. General Shekarchi made the remarks at an explanatory session for activists of popular nightly gatherings, held at the Islamic Development Coordination Council. ​ Speaking on the “second imposed war,” Shekarchi said the enemy had believed it could topple the Islamic Republic’s military within one week and partition Iran the following week. ​ Turning to the performance review conducted within the Armed Forces following Operation True Promise 1 and 2 and the Eqtedar (Authority) military exercises, Shekarchi said a target bank of sensitive enemy positions had subsequently been identified and developed. ​ He said the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, had fully prepared for the third imposed war, including arrangements to compensate for his own and other commanders’ martyrdom. ​ “The Americans deployed every weapon they had set aside for a third world war against Iran, but they did not succeed,” Shekarchi said. ​ He added that a data center established in the United Arab Emirates more than 20 years ago, with the support of American, European, and Zionist regime actors, had been aimed at controlling the entire West Asia region and parts of the wider world. ​ “Twenty years of their effort were destroyed in this war,” he said. ​ Shekarchi said the enemy had lost approximately 14 main strategic bases and three auxiliary bases in the region, totaling 17 major installations, representing more than five decades of investment. ​ “The enemies are confused with all their intelligence-gathering tools and do not know where we produce missiles,” he said. ​ He said the strategic objective of the Armed Forces is the expulsion of the United States military from the West Asia region, and that the Zionist regime would cease to exist once that goal is achieved. 📲 @Irna_en​
