🚀 Russian Ballistic Missile Reportedly Destroys Drone Factory in Kyiv A drone manufacturing facility in Kyiv was destroyed in a Russian ballistic missile strike on Friday. According to reports, the factory was owned by Terminal Autonomy, a U.S.-registered company incorporated in Delaware, marking what has been described as the first reported strike on a U.S.-owned company during the conflict. Terminal Autonomy develops AI-assisted deep-strike drones equipped with guidance systems designed to remain operational despite electronic jamming. The company says its loitering munitions and precision weapons are already being used on the front line. The company and Ukraine's Ministry of Defense did not comment on the reports. Former U.S. intelligence official Anthony Vinci said ownership of a manufacturing facility is unlikely to influence Russian targeting decisions if the site is considered to have military significance. The strike comes amid continued debate over expanding defense production in Ukraine. Earlier this month, U.S. President Donald Trump said the United States would authorize licensed production of Patriot air-defense missiles in Ukraine. Vinci suggested that facilities manufacturing U.S.-designed military systems could also become potential targets.
