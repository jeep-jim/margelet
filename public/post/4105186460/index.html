Zuckerberg's smart glasses have found a serious competitor. Xgimi introduced MemoMind One - glasses with a small screen in front of the eyes, but without built-in cameras. They can show notifications and navigation, translate speech in real-time, work as a teleprompter, and help communicate with AI. The main idea is to keep all the advantages of smart glasses but without capturing the surroundings. However, the ideal is still far away: many functions only work when paired with a smartphone, and some features may be hidden behind a subscription.
