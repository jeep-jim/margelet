Bioko is an island located 32 km (20 mi) off the west coast of Africa, forming the northernmost part of Equatorial Guinea 🇬🇶 Situated on the African continental shelf, Bioko is separated from the mainland by a 32 km (20 mi) stretch of water, only 60 meters deep. During the Pleistocene epoch, the island was connected to the African mainland. It wasn't until about 10,000 years ago—near the end of the Last Glacial Period—that Bioko became an island. ✨ Fascinating! ✨ Wow Factor
