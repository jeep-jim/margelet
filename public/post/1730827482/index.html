⏰ SABAH OKUMASI ⏰ Although antibiotics have substantially reduced mortality from bacterial infections, their widespread use has altered microbial ecosystems in unexpected ways. In addition to eliminating harmful bacteria, antibiotics may also affect beneficial microorganisms that contribute to digestion, immune regulation, and protection against pathogens. Such disruptions can sometimes increase susceptibility to secondary infections or other health complications. Consequently, healthcare professionals increasingly emphasize the importance of prescribing antibiotics only when necessary and selecting treatments that minimize unintended effects on the body’s microbial communities.
