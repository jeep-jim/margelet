❣️ MiCA's grandfathering period ended on July 1. The score in the EU: 244 crypto firms approved, 1,738 ordered to wind down operations. That is not a headline about tokens. It is a headline about venues. Liquidity, custody and withdrawal routes across an entire regulated market got redrawn in a single week. A frozen account holds a frozen position. If your exchange loses its license while you hold size there, your risk management happens in a support queue. For a trader the lesson is venue risk equals position risk. Know where you can withdraw before you need to, not after. Checking a venue takes ten minutes.
