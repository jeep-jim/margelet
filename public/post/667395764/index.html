As a Bible prophecy teacher, I am often asked about the outcome of future elections. Teaching Bible prophecy, however, does not make someone a prophet. In fact, the Bible never reveals the results of future elections in our modern era. What it does reveal in remarkable detail are the future events surrounding Israel, the nations, and God’s prophetic plan for the world. Elections are about people’s choices, and those choices reflect their convictions. Voting is not merely a privilege - it is a responsibility. Every vote is a moral decision, and as believers we are called to make those decisions in a way that honors God. “And let us not grow weary while doing good, for in due season we shall reap if we do not lose heart.” (Galatians 6:9, NKJV) God instructed the people of Israel to consider two options - good and evil, life and death - and then commanded them: “I call heaven and earth as witnesses today against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing. Therefore choose life, that both you and your descendants may live.” (Deuteronomy 30:19, NKJV) But how do we discern good from evil, or life from death? That is why God gave us His Word. “Your word is a lamp to my feet And a light to my path.” (Psalm 119:105, NKJV) “All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness.” (2 Timothy 3:16, NKJV) “Sanctify them by Your truth. Your word is truth.” (John 17:17, NKJV) Anything that stands against His Word, His character, or His ways is wrong. His Word is the standard by which we measure everything. As elections approach in Israel on October 27, 2026, and in the United States on November 3, 2026, I urge the citizens of both nations to choose wisely. Stand firmly against every agenda that is anti-God, anti-biblical, anti-Israel, anti-family, and supportive of the massacre of innocent unborn children. Vote according to biblical convictions rather than cultural pressures or political trends. “Righteousness exalts a nation, But sin is a reproach to any people.” (Proverbs 14:34, NKJV) “Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, The people He has chosen as His own inheritance.” (Psalm 33:12, NKJV) Awaiting His Return, Amir
