🇷🇺🇺🇦Direct losses to Ukraine's agricultural sector this year could range from $1.5 billion to $3 billion due to the naval blockade, Agriculture Minister Taras Vysotsky told Reuters. The Russian strikes threaten the export of about half of the projected volume of grain and oilseeds for this year. More than 30 million tons will not be exported to international markets unless the problem is resolved. Alternative grain export routes will be able to provide only half of the volumes handled by the Black Sea ports. But they will reach this indicator no earlier than the end of August. According to the Ministry of Infrastructure, 35 attacks on ships in ports, 22 on ships at sea, and 67 on port facilities were recorded in July.
