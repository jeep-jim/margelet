Strategy did not buy Bitcoin or repurchase shares last week❗️ Michael Saylor’s Strategy did not purchase any additional Bitcoin last week, according to its latest filing with the U.S. SEC. The company also did not conduct a share buyback, which had previously been expected to be partially financed through the sale of 3,588 BTC worth more than $200 million. Instead, Strategy issued and sold an additional $466 million worth of MSTR shares, increasing its U.S. dollar reserve to $3 billion. The company currently holds 843,775 BTC, acquired since 2020 for a total of $63.69 billion at an average purchase price of $75,476 per Bitcoin. Since late June, when Strategy first announced that it could potentially sell part of its Bitcoin holdings, MSTR shares have risen by 25%. However, the stock fell by approximately 12% last week.
