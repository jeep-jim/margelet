Air defenses include RAM and enhanced SeaSparrow missiles (15 and 50 km range, respectively) and Phalanx CIWS (1.5 km) if things get desperate 🌏 The Boxer is an older, Wasp-class assault ship. 1.1k crew, room for 1.9k Marines, floodable interior deck for 3 LCAC hovercraft, 2 conventional landers, or 40+ wheeled Marine ACU armored combat vehicles. 6 F-35s or Harriers, 4 attack choppers, 12+ transports and a few utility helicopters. Air defenses: identical 🌏 Loading the ships up with troops and shiny, expensive military hardware isn’t the problem. It’s the offloading safely after sailing into a hornet’s nest part.
