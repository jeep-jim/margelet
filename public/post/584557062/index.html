❗️Most Trading Problems Aren’t About Strategy Many traders waste years chasing the "perfect" setup. The real issue? Emotion. Small losses spark frustration. You feel the market “owes you.” Then comes revenge trading: bigger positions, broken rules, emotional choices. Discipline fades. By day’s end, you’re chasing losses, not trading your edge. Try this: Review past trades. Split losing days into: 📊 Days you followed your plan—normal. 🛍 Days you broke rules—revenge trades, moved stops, overtraded. Remove the second group. Recalculate. Many find they don’t have a strategy problem—just emotional blowups. Your journal should ask: 🪙 When do I break rules? 🪙 After how many losses does judgment slip? 🪙 Is there a time my trading suffers? 🪙 Which mistake costs most monthly? These beat any new chart pattern. Lasting traders spot emotional drift early—and walk away. ✅ @futures Risk everything
