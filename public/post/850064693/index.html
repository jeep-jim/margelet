Andrew Bridgen was one of the only members of the UK Parliament who spoke out during COVID. I wanted to know what he was actually up against when he did. During the lockdowns, he was watching more people commit suicide in a month than he would normally see in a year. He went to his government. He said you're killing my people. They told him there was no link between suicides and lockdowns. A million people under 25 in the UK came out of those lockdowns on waiting lists for mental health support. But there's no link... He told himself - they were just lying. At the same time, his government was being pressured to authorize experimental vaccines for children as young as six months old. No healthy child that age had died anywhere in the world. They were at no risk. And Andrew stood up in the House of Commons and said what I've believed for a long time: any society willing to sacrifice its children to protect adults has lost its way. Just like he said, I expect to make sacrifices for my children. I don't expect them to make sacrifices for me. @DrPaulMarik
