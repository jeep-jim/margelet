New York School Introduces Humanoid Robot Teacher A New York school district is set to become one of the first in the United States to introduce a humanoid robot into the classroom. Named "Sally," the robot will teach coding, robotics, and AI to 11th and 12th grade students at Salamanca High School. It has a Western New York accent, can move its arms and legs, recognize students through individual logins, and costs about $57,000. School officials say Sally will assist teachers and "never" replace them. The robot was developed by Realbotix, a company that also manufactures companion robots.
