A key engine powering Strategy’s Bitcoin buying spree is showing signs of slowing down. Michael Saylor’s preferred shares, STRC, have traded below their $100 par value all month—recently hovering around $92. While that might seem like a small gap, the implications for Bitcoin are significant. STRC has been Strategy’s main fundraising instrument, fueling the acquisition of over 112,000 BTC—more than 13% of its total holdings. But here’s the catch: as long as STRC trades below par, Strategy can’t issue new shares or raise additional capital through this vehicle. To continue accumulating Bitcoin, the company has turned to selling its common stock (MSTR), funding two separate $100M BTC purchases this month alone.
