🇬🇧UK Gambling Commission redirects settlement funds to central government 🔵On July 22, the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) confirmed that future regulatory settlement funds will be paid into the government’s Consolidated Fund, leaving the government to decide whether the money supports gambling-related harm initiatives or wider public spending. 🔵The decision followed a consultation with 28 responses, where half of the respondents disagreed and warned that moving settlement funds outside the gambling ecosystem could weaken their connection to harm prevention and industry accountability. 🔵The Commission said the statutory levy should provide sufficient funding for gambling-related harm research, prevention, and treatment, while the new settlement funding rule applies immediately. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: This is a governance shift rather than a direct change to licensing obligations, but it changes the policy meaning of UK enforcement settlements: funds will no longer be structurally tied to gambling harm programs.
