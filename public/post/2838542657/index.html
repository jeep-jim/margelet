⚠️ BabyDogers ⚠️ As we are getting closer to our new Quest on Monday, we’re dropping some tips as promised. Today’s topic: your social media accounts. Before you start posting, please think carefully about which account you want to use. Using your personal account is not always a good idea. Why? Because your personal account already has its own “history”. For example, if you usually post your daily life, selfies, food, school, family, random memes, or local content, then the platform already understands your account in that way. So when you suddenly start posting BabyDoge clips, dog memes, and crypto content, the algorithm may get confused. It may show your videos to the wrong people. For example: your friends your classmates your family people who liked your old personal content But these people may not be interested in BabyDoge or crypto. If they scroll away fast, don’t watch, don’t like, and don’t comment, then the platform may think your video is not interesting. And then your reach can be suppressed. That’s why a separate Quest account can be better. A new account helps the platform understand one clear thing: “This account is about BabyDoge, dogs, memes, and crypto.” Here’s how to prepare it: 1. Make your account look real Add a profile picture, simple name, and short bio. Examples: BabyDoge Clips BabyDoge Memes BabyDoge Community Dogs & Crypto Clips 2. Warm up your account Before posting, use the platform like a normal person. Watch videos about dogs, memes, crypto, pets, and BabyDoge. Like some videos. Follow a few related accounts. Leave a few normal comments. This helps the platform understand what your account is about. 3. Don’t spam Do not post 10 videos in 10 minutes. Start slowly: Day 1: prepare profile + watch content Day 2: post 1 video Day 3: post 1–2 videos Consistency is better than spam. 4. Keep your account focused If your account is about BabyDoge, dogs, memes, and crypto, then keep it that way. Do not post random unrelated content. The platform needs to understand who should see your videos. Prepare your accounts now. The Quest starts on Monday. 👨‍💻 Quest | Web | X | Telegram | Instagram | YouTube | TikTok | Discord
