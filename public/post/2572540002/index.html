[ARTIKEL] CORTIS set a new record with "REDRED," which surpassed 100 million streams on Spotify in just 57 days, making it the fastest song by a boy group that debuted within the last five years to reach the milestone. Less than a year after debut, CORTIS now has three songs with over 100 million streams: "GO!," "FaSHioN," and "REDRED." "REDRED" also dominated major music charts, including Melon, Bugs, and Spotify Korea. CORTIS became the only boy group making a comeback this year to top the Melon Weekly Chart and the first boy group in the past five years to remain on Spotify's global charts for nearly two months. The song's popularity continued to grow through performances at various events and the viral "Palang-gwi Dance" trend. Meanwhile, their nickname, "Young Creator Crew," evolved into the popular term "Youngkeukeu (영크크)." Forbes also named CORTIS to its "30 Under 30 Asia 2026" list. Their success has also been reflected in their first tour, the "2026 CORTIS TOUR &lt;PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN&gt;," with tickets for the Incheon shows and all North American dates selling out. Popularitas lagu ini semakin meningkat berkat penampilan di berbagai acara dan tren "Palang-gwi Dance", sementara julukan mereka, "Young Creator Crew", berkembang menjadi istilah populer "Youngkeukeu (영크크)".
