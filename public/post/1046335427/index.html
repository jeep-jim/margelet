According to satellite images, a drone hangar was indeed destroyed, and smoke was still visible on low-res Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. (July 12, 08h16 am UTC) Osint users have been tracking the MQ-4C, and if it doesn't take off in the next 48 hours it is very likely destroyed by Iran. The drone was operating daily, last operation yesterday July 11. (H/t: MenchOsint) @FotrosResistancee
