After a quarter-century behind bars, former Palestinian prisoner Abdul Karim Al-Rimawi embraced his child for the first time following his release from Israeli prisons. Al-Rimawi, from the town of Beit Rima northwest of Ramallah, spent 25 years in prison. During those years, he lost both of his parents while still incarcerated. When he was arrested, his daughter was just one year old; today, she holds a master’s degree in media studies.
