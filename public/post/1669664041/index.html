🔔 Money is rotating from Bitcoin into XRP. What the ETF flows show ⚠️ Something unusual is happening in the flow of institutional money through crypto exchange-traded funds. In a single recent week, United States spot Bitcoin ETFs shed roughly $1.44 billion, the largest weekly outflow of 2026, while spot XRP ETFs did the opposite, drawing fresh money and extending a streak that has now reached six consecutive weeks of inflows. Those XRP funds have drawn around $1.44 billion since launching in November 2025. Ethereum funds bled too, losing hundreds of millions, while XRP and Solana products absorbed a combined couple hundred million. 🌐 On the surface, the story writes itself: institutions are rotating out of Bitcoin and Ethereum and into XRP, a changing of the guard in where the smart money wants exposure. The flows are real and the divergence is striking. But what the rotation actually means is more complicated than the headline, and understanding the nuance is the difference between reading a genuine signal and overreading a small one. ⚠️ This piece works through what the ETF flow data actually shows, why XRP is attracting money while Bitcoin bleeds, the crucial caveat about the relative size of these flows, what the rotation does and does not tell us about XRP’s prospects, why the inflows have not lifted XRP’s price, and how to read this kind of flow data without drawing more from it than it supports.
