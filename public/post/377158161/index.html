Good morning from the North Pacific Ocean as we make our way from Seattle…

Good morning from the North Pacific Ocean as we make our way from Seattle to Alaska. I woke up at 2:00 a.m. with a heart full of gratitude - for my family and for the wonderful people who have joined us for this conference aboard this magnificent ship. By 3:30 a.m., I found myself working out in the ship’s gym, where I often reflect on the times in which we live and the direction our world is heading. This morning was no different. I am also deeply thankful for this remarkable group of speakers, each of whom I regard as an outstanding teacher of God’s Word and a living reflection of His character. Truthfully, with speakers of this caliber, I would much rather sit, listen, and learn than stand and teach. A day at sea is also a day of reflection. For me, as many of you know, in light of recent events, the heaviness and sadness I carry on this semiquincentennial birthday of America are even greater. In 1948, there were great Americans who understood the magnitude of that historic moment and did everything they could to help the newborn - and only - Jewish state. Even the legendary Frank Sinatra played his part. Evading federal agents in 1948, he reportedly slipped out the back of a New York nightclub carrying a paper bag filled with one million dollars in cash to pay a ship captain for a critical, embargo-breaking shipment of munitions destined for Israel. At the time, the United States had imposed a general arms embargo on the Middle East. Although it officially applied to all parties, it left the newly established State of Israel desperately short of weapons while neighboring Arab armies were already well-equipped through other sources. Yet the Lord always raised up men and women who understood the significance of the hour and gave them the courage to act upon their convictions. Even now, as we face rising hostility toward Israel, it is worth reflecting on how extraordinary this relationship truly is. Here are two nations with no shared border, no meaningful economic dependence, and not even a common language - yet for decades they have been uniquely connected. No obvious national self-interest brought them together in the beginning. Rather, something deep within the American spirit has long been drawn to the story of the Jewish people and their return to their ancestral homeland. And here I am, aboard an American cruise ship, sailing toward God’s breathtaking creation in Alaska - not only to behold His magnificent handiwork, but also surrounded by nearly 700 like-minded believers who understand the significance of the times and seasons in which we live. Even if political leaders change course, pursue profit from the wrong places, or make deals with the worst of America’s enemies, the saints gathered here understand that in these last days courage is a rare commodity - one that God expects, requires, and calls His people to demonstrate. At times, standing for the truth may make us feel as though we are standing alone. Elijah felt exactly that way. Yet the Lord gently corrected his perception, reminding him that He had preserved a faithful remnant of 7,000 in Israel who had not bowed the knee to Baal (1 Kings 19:18). The same God who preserved a remnant then is preserving one today. God has never needed the majority to accomplish His purposes. He has always worked through a faithful remnant. Our calling is not to be popular, but to be faithful - to speak the truth in love (Ephesians 4:15), contend earnestly for the faith (Jude 3), and leave the results to Him. Hours, days, and even years of advocating for Israel ultimately mean very little if they are detached from the Word of God, the plan of God, and the love of God displayed through our precious Messiah, Yeshua. Israel matters because God says it matters. Our convictions must always be rooted in Scripture, never merely in politics or emotion. I cannot wait to share, toward the end of this cruise, my new message, “Certainly Not!” - God’s answer to the doubts, distortions, and false teachings surrounding His plans and purposes for Israel.
