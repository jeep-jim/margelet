Chukotka Peninsula, Russia 🇷🇺🌊❄️✨ At the farthest edge of Russia, where land vanishes into two mighty oceans, lies the untamed and magnificent Chukotka Peninsula — remote, raw, and stunningly beautiful. Here, something extraordinary occurs: 💧 the icy waters of the Arctic Ocean meet the warmer currents of the Pacific. This clash gives rise to fierce storms and colossal waves that can soar up to 8 meters — a true force of nature to witness. The Chukotka coast is a realm of endless horizons, freezing winds, roaming whales, walruses on the ice, and wilderness so pristine it feels untouched by time. 🌌 A place that makes you feel like you've reached the end of the Earth — profound, humbling, unforgettable.
