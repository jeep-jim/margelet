Good morning from Dubai, UAE! People often ask me why I talk about Israel all the time as a Bible teacher. Perhaps the question should be directed to God. Why did He mention Israel so many times in His Word? It would be one thing if Israel appeared only in the historical narrative of the Old Testament. But Israel is central throughout the Prophets and continues to play a vital role in the New Testament. If God were finished with Israel, why would He devote so much of His Word to their future? And if He isn’t finished with them, what does that reveal about Him and about His plans for us? “For I am the LORD, I do not change…” (Malachi 3:6, NKJV) “The gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.” (Romans 11:29, NKJV) This morning, while eating breakfast just minutes ago, I was listening to a podcast about Israel’s preparedness for future threats. An IDF colonel, whose face was blurred, said that now that the Shiite axis of evil has been significantly weakened, Israel must prepare for a Sunni axis led by a nation with superpower capabilities. I almost fell off my chair. A secular Jewish senior military officer had just described a coalition of Sunni Muslim nations led by a powerful nation - remarkably similar to what the Bible foretells. Then came an even greater surprise - my own surprise. Why should I have been shocked? This is exactly what I have been teaching for the last 30 years. It is also the central theme of Steve Yohn’s and my recent New York Times bestselling novel, The Next War. The more the world tries to disprove God’s Word, redefine it, remove parts of it, or replace its plain meaning with symbolism, the more God continues to demonstrate that His Word stands forever. “Forever, O LORD, Your word is settled in heaven.” (Psalm 119:89, NKJV) “I am God, and there is no other… Declaring the end from the beginning, And from ancient times things that are not yet done…” (Isaiah 46:9-10, NKJV) How futile it is to think anyone can stop, rewrite, or ignore what God has already declared concerning the future. So, Amir, should we simply accept evil and go along with it because the Bible says evil will increase? Certainly not! Just because God tells us what will happen does not mean He calls us to surrender to it. We are to stand for truth, proclaim the gospel, resist evil, and never grow weary in doing good. “And let us not grow weary while doing good, for in due season we shall reap if we do not lose heart.” (Galatians 6:9, NKJV) Have a blessed evening and night in the Americas, a wonderful morning in Europe and the Middle East, and a blessed day in Asia and the Pacific. Awaiting His Return, Amir
