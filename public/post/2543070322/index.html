🇷🇺🇨🇳 Russia and China Launch Joint Sea-2026 Naval Drills. The week-long exercise is officially described as defensive—not directed against any third country. Russia and China have officially begun the Joint Sea-2026 naval exercises in Qingdao, with warships, naval aviation and military personnel from both countries taking part in drills across the Yellow Sea from July 6–13. According to the Russian Navy, the exercises will focus on joint search-and-rescue operations, anti-submarine warfare, air defense missions, artillery firing, and simulated submarine rescue operations using a deep-submergence rescue vehicle. Russian exercise director Rear Admiral Sergey Sinko emphasized that the drills are defensive in nature and are not aimed at any third country. The exercises come as Moscow and Beijing continue expanding their military cooperation amid rising geopolitical tensions in the Indo-Pacific and beyond.
