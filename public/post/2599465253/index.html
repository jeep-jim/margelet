DIY IMMUNE-BOOSTING SYRUP Ingredients: Red onion Garlic Turmeric Ginger One 18 oz (500 ml) glass jar Use 6–7 large garlic cloves, 6–7 chunks of ginger, 6–7 large pieces of turmeric, and 5–6 slices of red onion. Add them to the jar, leaving about 1.5 inches of space below the rim. Pour honey over the mixture until all the ingredients are fully covered, keeping roughly 1 inch of headspace at the top. Seal the jar with its lid. Let it sit for 15 days, away from direct sunlight. Shake the jar gently each day. Once it reaches the desired consistency, transfer it to the fridge to slow fermentation. Take 1–2 teaspoons daily on an empty stomach during cold and flu season. Enjoy it throughout the winter months for a natural health boost.
