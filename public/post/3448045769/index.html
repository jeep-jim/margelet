Have you ever asked an AI to build a simple feature, only to get an overly complex codebase that breaks everything else? 🤯 --- When we jump straight from "Idea" to "Code" (aka vibe coding) with AI, we usually face two massive project-killers: 1️⃣ "Context Lost" (AI Amnesia) As the codebase grows, AI often "forgets" the initial rules. You agreed on Clean Architecture and specific database conventions, but halfway through, the AI makes up its own rules. The result? Inconsistent, unmaintainable code. 2️⃣ "Scope Creep" (Stealth Features) Your initial document just asked for an "Email Login" button. Suddenly, the AI takes the initiative to add Google Login, Apple Login, and even Redis caching. It looks cool, but it introduces over-engineering, bloats timelines, and triggers unpredictable bugs! This is exactly why we need to stop treating AI as a mere "typewriter" and start treating it as an engineering team that requires strict discipline! --- As a solution, you can now try the SDLC methodology using Custom Agents based on Spec-Driven Development, comprehensively packaged in the Awesome Copilot Indonesia project repository! 🚀 --- In this methodology, various tasks are orchestrated across specific Agents to "maintain project sanity" before a single line of code is written: 🕵️‍♂️ Clarification Analyst: Interrogates your initial product documents to eliminate any ambiguities. 🏛 Specification Architect: Locks down the architectural design and canonical terms (CONTEXT.md) so the AI Developer doesn't suffer from "amnesia". 📋 Planner Architect: Breaks down the design into a concrete, measurable implementation plan. ⚖️ Artifact Consistency Checker: Acts as the "police," auditing documents to ensure no Scope Creep or "stealth features" sneak in! With this approach, AI-assisted software development becomes significantly safer, more predictable, and highly structured. -------- All prompts, workflows, and methodologies are open-source.
