Munnar, India If the Earth could whisper serenity, it would sound like a morning in Munnar. Known as the "Kashmir of South India," this hill station, wrapped in emerald tea plantations, wakes gently beneath the mist. As the first rays of sunlight spill over the rolling hills, the air turns crisp and fresh, carrying the delicate scent of tea leaves. Silhouettes of tea pickers move through the rows like verses of quiet poetry. Every curve of the estate feels sketched by stillness itself. Munnar isn’t just a destination — it’s a still frame from a tender love letter written by nature.
