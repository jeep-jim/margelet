Strait of Hormuz traffic is picking up after 111 days of near-total gridlock, following the signing of a U.S.-Iran deal, according to CBS News. More tankers are now transiting the waterway, which under normal conditions carries roughly one-fifth of global oil supply. The agreement ends the immediate standoff, but CBS notes that Iran and the U.S. still have significant outstanding issues to negotiate - meaning the current calm is provisional at best. One IDF soldier was also killed in a Hezbollah IED explosion, according to the Jerusalem Post, a reminder that the wider regional picture has not fully stabilized alongside the Iran diplomacy.
