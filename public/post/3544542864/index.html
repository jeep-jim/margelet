🇿🇦South Africa moves to close the offshore gambling gap with tougher ads, blocking, and payment controls 🔵South Africa is fast-tracking a new gambling bill, with the NGPC reviewing whether the current national and provincial framework provides sufficient oversight of the sector, as a local media outlet reported on July 2. The government is also preparing uniform gambling advertising standards, expected to become part of licensing conditions for operators to strengthen consumer protection and address gambling addiction concerns. 🔵On the other hand, the South African Bookmakers Association (SABA) renewed its call for tougher offshore gambling laws by proposing 6 new reforms on July 6, citing estimates that unlicensed operators account for around 62% of online gambling activity in South Africa. Proposed reforms include clearer definitions of illegal offshore operators, website-blocking powers, stronger penalties for affiliates and marketing intermediaries, tighter rules for unlicensed advertising, and a centralized national enforcement capability. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: South Africa appears to be moving toward a stricter and more coordinated gambling enforcement model, combining advertising controls, website blocking, payment restrictions, and stronger action against illegal offshore supply.
