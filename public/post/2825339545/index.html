Dr. Abhishek Verma, Chief National Coordinator, ShivSena NDA Alliance & Elections, met with the President and office bearers of the Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC) Employees’ Union to hear their grievances and discuss issues affecting DTC employees. The interaction focused on workers’ welfare, service conditions, and identifying areas where ShivSena can play a constructive role in helping resolve genuine concerns through dialogue with the appropriate authorities.
