5 классных промптов для обработки фото. Просто отправляем своё селфи с промптом в ChatGPT или Gemini — и получаем шедевр. 1️⃣ За стеклом Use the attached photo as an identity reference. Vertical 9:16 fashion-editorial portrait. Subject viewed from behind through ribbed textured glass — heavy distortion, "scanning" effect on the face. Clean side profile, face turned right, calm expression. Minimal dark jacket. Gradient background: deep burgundy to bright red with subtle bloom. Harsh red key light from back-right, low neutral fill from left for near-silhouette. Bright specular streaks on glass, rim light on nose, lips, cheek. 85mm look, focus on glass plane — glass lines sharp, face slightly softened by refraction. High contrast, subtle grain, slight halation. Intense modern noir atmosphere. 2️⃣ Сквозь листву Use my reference photo as the subject (same face). A single editorial greenhouse portrait. I stand centered among tall tropical plants, facing the camera. Neutral outfit. Sunlight through the glass ceiling creates soft flares. Large out-of-focus leaves in the foreground frame my face. Soft mist in the air. Green-gold mood. Realistic skin and fabric. No extra people. 3️⃣ Пицца-тайм на заправке Grainy nighttime photo at a gas station, vertical 9:16 phone snapshot. Raw underground flash feel. Subject mid-bite into pizza — messy, casual, motion frozen. Oversized black matte bomber puffer, dark-tinted sporty sunglasses, indifferent expression. Background: a modern fighter jet parked at the station as if refueling — matte metal, glowing red-white nav lights. Harsh flash highlights on fabric, metal, food; background in deep shadow. Cold blue-white neon station lights. Slight grain, candid realism. 4️⃣ Нечаянный кадр Vertical 9:16 photo of the attached person as an accidental iPhone snapshot. Poorly framed, tilted horizon, no deliberate composition. Strong motion blur and slight defocus — camera moved during the shot. Uneven lighting, mild overexposure, cluttered background. Weird angle: too low, too high, off-level. Feels like a failed candid. Ordinary phone quality: digital noise, poor dynamic range. No studio lighting, no posing, no retouching. 5️⃣ Свет и тень Use the attached photo as the subject. Vertical 9:16 cinematic film noir, black and white. High contrast: deep crushed blacks, bright highlights. Directional hard light creates venetian blind or geometric shadows across the subject and background. Sharp face, soft falloff into darkness.
