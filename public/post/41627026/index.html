DIY Lentil Vegetable Soup 🍲 Simple, nutritious, and flavorful — ready in under an hour. Ingredients: • 2 tbsp cold-pressed oil • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds • 1/3 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp turmeric • 0.5 tsp salt • 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves, 2 carrots, 2 celery stalks, all chopped • 2 tomatoes (or 1/3 cup puree) • 1 cup lentils, rinsed Preparation: 1. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds; sizzle 10 seconds. 2. Add onion; sauté 7 minutes until golden. 3. Stir in tomatoes; cook 10 minutes. 4. Add carrots, celery, spices, salt; mix well. 5. Add lentils and 2 cups water. 6. Boil, then simmer 30–40 minutes until tender. 7. Adjust seasoning. Stir in 3 tbsp fresh parsley; cook 3 more minutes. Serve warm. #LentilSoup #HealthyEating #PlantBasedRecipes Unfiltered lens
