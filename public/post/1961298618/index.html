TOP 10 facts about Nakheel Properties 1️⃣The company was founded in 2003. Today, it is one of Dubai's largest developers, renowned for its man-made island projects. 2️⃣The name Nakheel comes from Arabic, meaning 'palm tree'. 3️⃣In 2024, Nakheel and Meydan merged under the umbrella of the state-owned Dubai Holding. 4️⃣The company gave the world legendary archipelagos: Palm Jumeirah, Palm Jebel Ali, Dubai Islands, and The World. Thanks to Nakheel's projects, Dubai's coastline grew by 410km. 5️⃣Its portfolio includes communities such as Gardens Homes, Jumeirah Lake Towers, and Jumeirah Islands. They span 15,000 hectares and are home to 700,000 residents. 6️⃣The developer strives to combine innovation with efficient planning. Projects feature beaches, lagoons, golf courses, parks, and infrastructure for pedestrians and cyclists. 7️⃣Real estate range from ultra-premium island villas to affordable apartments in waterfront complexes. The portfolio also has hospitality, retail, and entertainment venues such as Nakheel Mall and Ibn Battuta Mall. 8️⃣In 2023, Dubai recorded one of its most expensive residential property deals – a penthouse in the 71-storey Como Residences by Nakheel➡️$136 million. 9️⃣The company is actively involved in charitable initiatives: supporting refugees, organising free health check-ups and eco-events. 🔟Nakheel has earned recognition across a broad range of fields, from property development to branding and community management.
