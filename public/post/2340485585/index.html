🏛️🇪🇺Binance withdraws its MiCA license application in Greece and exits the EU market 🔵Binance withdrew its MiCA application in Greece, stating that it will pursue authorization in another EU member state while taking measures to remain compliant with applicable requirements, as reported by Reuters on June 24. 🔵On June 26, just two days later, the exchange informed users in several EU markets that new registrations would stop and certain services would be restricted, while stating that customer assets would remain safe and accessible during the transition. 🔵At the same time, during the interview with The Block media released on June 29, Binance founder CZ claimed that the Greek application was compliant and close to approval before unspecified political forces intervened; however, he provided no supporting documents or confirmed speculation involving any particular official. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Binance’s case confirms that a MiCA authorization is not guaranteed even for the largest market participants; every crypto business seeking to serve the EU must satisfy the relevant national regulator’s licensing requirement. At the same time, the temporary absence of Binance from the EU market is likely to reshape competitive dynamics as other authorized providers seek to capture its former substantial market share, while Binance pursues approval elsewhere. VASPs operating without MiCA authorization should cease EU-facing activities unless and until properly licensed, as continued operations may trigger enforcement action, client disruption, and serious reputational damage. 🗂️ Ready-Made Solutions | 👩‍💻Tailored Legal Solution
