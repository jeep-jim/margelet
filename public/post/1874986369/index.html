Arrest has ceased to be merely a colonial measure aimed at restricting freedom; rather, it has become an integral component of the genocidal system implemented by the occupation against Palestinians, through systematic policies targeting the Palestinian person, their body, psyche, dignity, and very existence. The findings presented in this report confirm that the Israeli occupation authorities have continued to expand mass arrest campaigns, dramatically increase the use of administrative detention, and detain civilians as hostages. At the same time, they have entrenched a comprehensive system of torture, starvation, medical neglect, sexual violence, solitary confinement, and enforced disappearance. These practices no longer constitute isolated violations but have become entrenched and systematic tools for the administration of prisons and military detention camps. Testimonies documented by legal teams and statements from released prisoners further reveal that the crimes committed inside Israeli prisons have not diminished over time. Instead, they have entered a more organized and brutal phase, reflected in the continued deaths of prisoners, the spread of diseases and epidemics, and the systematic destruction of prisoners' physical and psychological well-being, in line with the genocidal approach that has governed Israeli policies since the beginning of the genocide. The information contained in this report represents only the minimum that has been possible to document, given the occupation's ongoing enforced disappearance of thousands of detainees from the Gaza Strip and its continued denial of access to them by international and human rights organizations. Consequently, the scale of crimes and violations committed inside prisons and military camps is far greater than current documentation conditions allow to be revealed.
