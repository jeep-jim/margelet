A hedgehog carrying its hoglet 🦔🥰 Baby hedgehogs, called hoglets, typically leave the nest at about 3–4 weeks old to join their mother on foraging trips. But if their nest is disturbed—or they stray too far before they’re ready—mum will carefully pick them up and carry them to safety, one by one. After roughly 10 days of learning the ropes alongside mum, the young hedgehogs set off to begin their independent lives 🦔 Nomad's Lens
