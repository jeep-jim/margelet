A photorealistic still-life composition featuring an object from a reference image that must be replicated precisely, without alteration or distortion. The object is positioned at a dynamic angle and rests on a composition of freshly cut guava. The medium close-up composition emphasizes the object and features a distinctive diagonal presentation. The object is surrounded by large guava slices with a juicy texture and visible seeds, covered in a thick, airy foam that flows down the surface of the object, the fruit, and the base of the composition, forming voluminous bubbles of varying sizes and soft, foamy streams. The surface and background are rendered in a unified, smooth pink color scheme, creating a clean studio environment free of unnecessary details and distracting elements. Bright studio lighting is used, with soft, directional light from the front and above, creating delicate shadows and emphasizing the transparency of the bubbles, the moist texture of the fruit, the volume of the foam, and the smooth reflections on the surface of the object. The atmosphere is built on a feeling of freshness, purity, and a rich, fruity cosmetic composition. The shoot was done in the style of commercial skincare and bodycare product photography, shot on a virtual 100mm macro lens, with a shallow depth of field, selective focus on the object and foam texture, crisp details in the bubbles and fruit surface, highly detailed, photorealistic, 8K resolution, and a color palette dominated by soft pinks, whites, and delicate coral shades. #предметная@ii_photolab ВК | МАХ | НАВИГАЦИЯ
