🌏Vietnam, Pakistan, and Kazakhstan advance new rules for crypto trading, crime, and mining 🇻🇳On July 16, Vietnam released Decree No. 284/2026/NĐ-CP, which introduces fines of up to $1,900 for trading through unlicensed crypto platforms and up to $7,700 for unauthorized offerings or serious AML breaches, with the decree taking effect on September 1, 2026. 🇵🇰Pakistan’s Federal Investigation Agency created a dedicated crypto investigation unit focused on cybercrime and financial fraud, while the Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA) remains responsible for licensing and digital asset regulation, DAWN media reported on July 21. 🇰🇿Separately, on July 22, Kazakhstan approved strategic mining rules linking access to regulated electricity quotas with strict infrastructure requirements and contributions of mined crypto assets to a state-backed reserve mechanism. 💭 Inteliumlaw opinion: Platforms serving Vietnamese users should reassess access, onboarding, and marketing routes before September 1, while crypto businesses entering Pakistan should treat licensing as only one part of compliance and build strong transaction monitoring, fraud response, and law enforcement cooperation procedures.
