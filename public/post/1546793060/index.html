📡 Scientific, educational ties with Pakistan enter new phase: Iran minister 📌 Islamabad, IRNA – Minister of Science, Research and Technology described his ongoing visit to Pakistan as a turning point in enhancing scientific, educational, and cultural cooperation between the two neighboring countries.
