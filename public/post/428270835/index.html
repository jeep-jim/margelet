🇺🇸🇮🇷 The US Treasury has revoked General License X, the authorization it issued just 15 days ago allowing Iranian oil sales to resume under the US-Iran memorandum of understanding. The move comes after UKMTO reported three tankers, Saudi, Qatari, and a third vessel, struck by unknown projectiles near the Strait of Hormuz. No group has claimed responsibility. Tehran has not commented. A US official called the incidents "wholly unacceptable" and promised consequences, while simultaneously insisting negotiators remain committed to a final deal. OFAC's replacement license, General License X1, gives companies until July 17 to wind down existing transactions but bars any new Iranian oil purchases from today. Worth noting: sanctions relief lasted barely two weeks before Washington pulled it, on the basis of attacks nobody has attributed to anyone. Brent crude jumped over 2% on the news.
