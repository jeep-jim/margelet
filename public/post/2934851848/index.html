New CCTV footage recovered from Nairobi's Central Police Station has revealed Albert Ojwang's final movements before his death in police custody, raising fresh questions in the ongoing murder trial. Presented in court by IPOA forensic investigators, the recovered footage shows Ojwang arriving at the station at about 9:23 p.m. on June 7, 2025, walking on his own and appearing to be in good physical condition. He is seen making phone calls before being escorted into the holding cells. Later, CCTV allegedly captures a police officer interacting with two unidentified men, who are then allowed into the cell block where Ojwang was being held. Hours later, Ojwang is seen being carried out of the cells unresponsive.
