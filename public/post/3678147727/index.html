🇺🇦 NABU MAY BE PREPARING SUSPICION NOTICE FOR "SERVANT OF THE PEOPLE" PARTY HEAD ARAKHAMIA Journalists close to anti-corruption bodies say Ukraine's National Anti-Corruption Bureau (NABU) may be preparing a suspicion notice against David Arakhamia, head of Zelensky's ruling "Servant of the People" party. According to Ukrainska Pravda, days before Fedorov's resignation, NABU and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office (SAP) obtained orders to take biological samples from Arakhamia's aide in the case over MP salary kickbacks — the same aide whose home was already searched in December. This suggests investigators are trying to tie the salary scheme directly to the presidential faction's leader via fingerprints on the cash. Journalist Yuriy Nikolov says the Arakhamia case will unfold over coming months following the Yermak playbook — half a year of searches and evidence-gathering before any formal notice, he says, and Arakhamia is now in the same position Yermak was. Sources close to grant-funded and anti-corruption media circles call Arakhamia one of the main organizers behind Fedorov's ouster as defense minister, and one of the pillars of the Zelensky regime who's effectively taken Yermak's old role.
