🎖🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 18 July 2026 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation. 📍The Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation. Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, three marine brigades, and four national guard brigades near Novogrigorovka, Novofedorovka, Dobropolye, Raiskoye, Krasny Kut, Shevchenko, Gruzskoye, Sergeyevka, Zolotoy Kolodez (Donetsk People's Republic), and Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region). ▪️The AFU losses amounted to up to 370 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, four motor vehicles, and one artillery gun. 👉 See Part 2 🔹 Russian Defence Ministry
