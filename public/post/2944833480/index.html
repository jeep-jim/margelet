We killed the bottom menu and built a drum instead Classic bottom nav breaks the moment your app grows beyond a few tabs. So we replaced it with a 3-row 3D carousel docked to the bottom of the screen - the navigation drum. Two gestures cover everything: swipe sideways to switch between tabs, swipe up/down to dive deeper or resurface. The drum always centers your current position and outlines your breadcrumb trail. Filters, search, actions, and content - all in one place. No piles of modal sheets, no lost-in-the-app feeling. More details here on Medium. We folded the navigation tree into a physical object that spins under your finger. It doesn't pretend the app is simple - it makes the complex manageable.
