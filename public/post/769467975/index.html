🌍 Iran’s Armed Forces ‘will not allow US to interfere’ in Strait of Hormuz 📌 Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters says Iran will never permit the United States to interfere in the management of the Strait of Hormuz, warning the Persian Gulf regional states that cooperation with Washington amounts to war against Iran. Colonel Ebrahim Zolfaqari said on Monday that US behavior in the waterway had created serious dangers for the entire region. ​ “America’s repeated adventurism and malicious acts to interfere in the management of the Strait of Hormuz have seriously endangered regional security, international trade, and the passage of oil tankers and commercial vessels,” he said. ​ “Unfortunately, the cooperation of some regional countries has also caused the risk of war spreading across the region to increase,” he added while issuing a direct warning. ​ “Accordingly, it is hereby declared that, following previous warnings, we will not, under any circumstances, allow the United States to interfere in the management of the Strait of Hormuz, and we will not do so in the future,” Zolfaqari said. ​ On the consequences of unauthorized US naval activity in the region, he said Iran’s Armed Forces would respond with force. ​ “The Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran will deal severely with any disruption or insecurity caused to commercial vessels and oil tankers by the aggressive and piratical American army operating outside Iran’s designated route and without the authorization of the Armed Forces,” he said, adding that recent operations by the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps and Army served as proof of this commitment. ​ Zolfaqari directed a separate warning at regional states. ​ “Regional heads of state are warned that any cooperation with America and any logistical support for its aggressive army will be considered an act of war against Iran’s sovereignty and national security,” he said. ​ “Should war spread across the region, the flames of that war will engulf all regional countries,” he added. ​ He said responsibility for any resulting instability would fall squarely on Washington and its partners. ​ “Responsibility for all insecurity and the spread of war in the region rests with America and the countries cooperating with its criminal army,” Zolfaqari said. 📲 @Irna_en​
