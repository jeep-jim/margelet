📊 Candlestick Breakouts: Strong vs Weak This graphic breaks down the difference between a weak breakout and a strong breakout something every trader needs to understand before entering a trade. A weak breakout closes in the lower portion of the candle for bullish setups (or higher portion for bearish setups). It shows hesitation, lack of momentum, and usually leads to fakeouts. 🚫 Don’t enter. A strong breakout closes in the top range for bullish moves (or bottom range for bearish moves). This signals conviction, real pressure, and a higher chance of continuation. ✅ These are the breakouts worth trading. Strong body = strong momentum. Weak body = weak signal. Simple, but game-changing when you apply it. 🔥 📱 @nursejannah
