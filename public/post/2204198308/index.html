🇺🇸 Trump allegedly used the market crash — which he himself triggered — to buy shares of major tech giants at a steep discount. On average, the U.S. President made around 62 securities transactions per day in 2025. However, on April 8, that figure was exceeded more than fourfold, reaching 327 transactions. That date coincided with the final day of the market downturn, which had been triggered by Trump’s announcement of sweeping tariffs against nearly every country in the world. The next day, Trump posted that it was a “GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!” before announcing a partial rollback of the tariffs — a move that helped the S&P 500 jump by around 9.5%, marking one of its best single-day performances on record. Trump’s buying spree on April 8 was reportedly focused mainly on large-cap tech stocks, including Apple, Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, and Nvidia, all of which surged sharply after the tariff rollback.
